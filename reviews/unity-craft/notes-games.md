# Unity games: a striking look, kept fast

Web research, 23 October 2026. Quotes are exact page text, read through a page-reading tool asked for verbatim text. I could read the loopit.dk PDFs that way but not download them. [primary] means the makers' own words; [secondary] means a report or encyclopedia; [unverified] means I could not open it.

## INSIDE (Playdead, 2016)

- **Engine:** a modified Unity on its light-prepass path. "Essentially use vanilla light-prepass rendering (unity: "Legacy deferred")" http://loopit.dk/rendering_inside.pdf [primary]. "INSIDE was made using a pretty heavily-modified Unity engine." https://blog.playdead.com/articles/the_lighting_of_inside/lighting_of_inside.html [primary]. For us: one stock path, with their own work in lights and post. That is our shape too.
- **Hand-placed decals, not GI:** bounce, specular, rim, AO and shadow decals, tuned by distance from the camera and by story focus. "there is no global illumination or even automated AO at play here." (blog) [primary]. For us: our camera is free, so a generator must place these from facts: AO under eaves, bounce off a limewashed wall.
- **SSAO refused:** "we don't wanna use SSAO due to it's lack of local control and artefacts associated with screen space effects." They used sphere AO, box lights and point-AO decals on character bones instead (slides) [primary]. For us: measure our SSAO's milliseconds at 1440p. Test baked per-part AO, plus capsule AO under people.
- **Shadows:** static lights' shadow maps are spread across several frames, and a smaller map with a better filter beats single-sample PCF (slides) [primary]. A torch gets decal shadows because "casting from a point light would have been much too expensive." (blog) [primary]. For us: the sun moves slowly, so refresh the town's static shadows over several frames.
- **Fog:** "Simple linear fog!" and "We clamp the fog to a max-value to enable bright objects to "bleed through"" (slides) [primary]. For us: clamp our gradient fog so lit windows stay readable at distance. It is nearly free.
- **Noise as a system:** "our solution dithers after every pass". Bayer "causes moiré-like patterns, which are very noticeable", so they switched to blue noise. Volume light uses "3 samples instead of 32", and TAA integrates the rest (slides) [primary]. Their TAA was released MIT-licensed for Unity 5 https://gamedeveloper.com/art/-i-limbo-i-dev-open-sources-its-unity-5-anti-aliasing-tech [secondary]. Gjøl's banding deck: "Dithering after quantisation does not remove banding". It does not cover temporal stability http://loopit.dk/banding_in_games.pdf [primary]. For us: their blue noise leaned on TAA, and we run MSAA. Blue noise alone may not cure our cross-fade shimmer.

## Firewatch (Campo Santo, 2016)

- **Stack:** Unity, with Marmoset Skyshop for lighting and Amplify Color for grading https://blog.camposanto.com/tagged/unity [primary]. The pipeline isn't named, but the game shipped before Unity had scriptable pipelines. For us: buy the generic parts and build only the look.
- **Colour from light:** "A lot of the color is from lighting + fog + color correction." "Most of the rocks in the game actually has grey diffuse" https://blog.camposanto.com/post/102893584324/i-asked-twitter-if-anyone-had-questions-about-the [primary]. For us: test value-led albedos under our grade. Hue stays only where facts give one (brick, paint).
- **One driver:** a custom "interpolation manager" moves the sky, the sun, the sun disc (the god-ray source), "our two fog systems" and "our color correction post effect all at the same time." (same) [primary]. For us: drive sun, beams, fog and grade from one set of curves.
- **No lightmaps:** the many lightmap sets needed for different times of day "made it rather untenable." (same) [primary]. For us: our history and sun change too, so the same holds.
- **Custom shaders:** "we are using custom shaders for everything right now" (same). There was "a directional stylistic fog system" and shaders for "painterly distant mountains" (tag page). The sky's gradient, sun disc and halos "simply get added together" and are set by colour, not scattering terms https://blog.camposanto.com/post/112703721804/this-blog-post-is-an-in-detail-explanation-of-a [primary]. For us: warm the fog toward the sun, and build the sky from about four values.
- **Launch hitches:** "Auto-saves are now far less frequent, as they were causing the worst framerate hitches". The PS4 patch also changed streaming and raised the draw and shadow distances https://blog.camposanto.com/post/139569669074/firewatch-ps4-patch-102-is-live/amp [primary]. For us: measure frame-time spikes during town assembly and saves, not only the average.

## Sable (Shedworks, 2021)

- **Stack:** Unity, with ProBuilder for about 80% of environments and terrain pre-generated by MapMagic https://unity.com/resources/shedworks-sable-modular-design-approach [primary quotes, Unity case study]. No source I opened names the pipeline or the line method. A tutorial writer says it is post-processing, uncited https://www.alanzucconi.com/?p=9507 [secondary].
- **Flat shading, depth rebuilt:** light, fog and lines put the depth back. "Lighting is really important to readability and depth". "We needed players to be casting shadows as often as possible to make sure they were readable on the surface." https://www.gamedeveloper.com/marketing/how-shedworks-refined-the-art-of-sable-in-pursuit-of-readability [primary quotes, GDC 2022 report]. For us: give every person a cheap contact shadow, separate from the sun's shadow map.
- **Fog first:** distance fog was "really, really key": "It definitely had the biggest impact overall." Fog is set per biome and carries day and night (same) [primary]. For us: tune fog before adding far detail.
- **Fade to hide pop-in:** lines fade with distance. "At one point we didn't use this technique and objects would just pop in -- which was ugly and a real problem." (same) [primary]. For us: fade contrast before the swap distance, so the dithered cross-fade can be shorter or go.
- **Performance came late:** after launch they "Added a custom culling system to improve performance and frame rate across the game", plus draw-distance and shadow settings https://www.gogdb.org/product/1928501328/releasenotes [primary patch notes]. PS5 was never fixed because the team was too small https://www.everyeye.it/notizie/sable-problemi-performance-ps5-sviluppatori-non-risolveranno-689667.html [secondary]. For us: build culling for a city-scale street before the content.

## Genshin Impact (miHoYo, 2020)

- **Engine:** "A deeply customized Unity engine for mobile". The console pipeline is separate: "We couldn't just rely on Unity's own PS4 platform implementation". The look is "PBR based stylized rendering", with lighting models per material. Built-in or SRP is not stated. Zhenzhong Yi, "From Mobile to Console" https://docswell.com/s/UnityJapan/KWRPQ5-210617-unity-dojo20211mihoyozhenzhongyi [primary]. For us: one shade function branched by data is the same idea.
- **Principles:** "Avoid excessive features". "Cool-looking technologies do not necessarily match the style of our game". The art words were "Fresh, bright, clean, anime-like" against "Dirty, noisy, dark" (same) [primary]. For us: write our two word lists, then judge SSR, SSAO and beams against them and against their milliseconds.
- **Cached cascades:** "First 4 cascades are updated with every frame", "Last 4 cascades are updated via interleaving", and "All cascades are updated at least once every 8 frames" (same) [primary]. For us: refresh our far sun cascades every few frames. This is likely custom work in URP (check).
- **Penumbra mask:** "Soft shadows are only calculated for pixels marked in penumbra areas", cutting "2 – 2.6ms ➔ 1.3 – 1.7ms" (same) [primary]. For us: filter only at shadow edges.
- **Fog, reflections, grade:** volumetric fog under 1 ms, and god rays as a separate half-resolution pass for art control. SSR costs about 1.5 ms on PS4 Pro. "ACES pipeline (RRT + ODT) was not used as it does not fit our game's style", and hue shift is baked into the LUT (same) [primary]. For us: this supports our own palette grade. Price our SSR against theirs.
- **Open-world art talk (GDC 2021):** the NPR world was tuned to match the characters, with a light-direction shadow mask on faces. Tree shadows have a minimum light, grass carries one artist-tinted vertex colour, and rock materials shift across near, mid and far https://www.famitsu.com/news/202107/23228135.html [secondary, Japanese report]. For us: a near/mid/far change inside the material is a cheaper near and far fade than more geometry levels.
- **Honkai Impact 3rd talk (2018):** geometry outlines with vertex-colour width, and image-space outlines from normals and depth for scenes, at constant cost. Planar reflection runs at 1/3 resolution https://docswell.com/s/UnityJapan/KWLQP5-unite-tokyo-20183rd [primary]. For us: image-space lines for the town, geometry lines on people. The Genshin character-shader details found online are fan reverse-engineering, so I did not use them.

## Return of the Obra Dinn (Lucas Pope, 2018)

- **Stack:** Unity (Wikipedia [secondary]; the devlog mentions static batching). Scenes render in 8-bit grey and a post pass makes them 1-bit. "All scene rendering is at 800x450 now (up from 640x360), which helps legibility without sacrificing the low-res style." https://dukope.com/devlogs/obra-dinn/tig-32/ [primary]. For us: internal resolution is a style choice and a budget at once.
- **The shimmer problem:** a screen-fixed dither under a moving camera: "What should be solid shapes and shades now read as a wiggling mess of pixels." He tried (same post) [primary]:
  - texel space, but "Any resampling from one space to another like this will result in aliasing"
  - motion-vector warping
  - a rotation offset, "DitherOffset = ScreenSize * CameraRotation / CameraFov"
  - a cube map around the camera
  - a sphere-mapped pattern that runs "the dither thresholding at a higher resolution and downsample."

  The result: "the dither can be pinned to the geometry during camera rotation but not during camera translation." It took about 100 hours. For us: this is our shimmer. The cheapest test is his rotation offset on our cross-fade mask. Walking will still crawl.
- **Blue noise:** "Blue noise loses a little range over the bayer matrix, but I prefer it overall for its smoother gradients." https://dukope.com/devlogs/obra-dinn/tig-02/ [primary].
- **Surface-stable dithering:** after Obra Dinn, Rune Skovbo Johansen made "the dots in the dither patterns stick to surfaces" at near-constant screen size. It runs on Unity's built-in Forward path, is tested on Unity 6, and is MPL-2.0 https://runevision.com/tech/dither3d, https://github.com/runevision/Dither3D [primary]. For us: a cross-fade mask that holds while walking. It needs a URP port and a cost measurement.

## The other games

- **Tunic:** Unity, with "entirely flat colour on low poly geometry, relying mostly on lighting to add depth" https://www.thexboxhub.com/the-tunic-interview-an-exclusive-chat-with-polymath-designer-andrew-shouldice/ [primary quote]. Precomputed GI instead of many point lights, and batching. Colour: saturated bases, slightly desaturated, plus a complementary second hue and HDR bloom https://blog.unity.com/games/smoke-mirrors-and-scrolling-textures-behind-the-scenes-of-tunic [secondary]. For us: a complementary second hue as a grade rule.
- **Valheim:** Unity. The low-detail style "makes it easier for people to play that doesn't have the latest hardware", lifted by "ambient effects and post-processing" https://www.pcgamesn.com/valheim/ps1-graphics [primary quotes]. A later patch "enables the main camera to be rendered at a lower resolution than the viewport and UI." https://www.gamingonlinux.com/2023/10/valheim-testing-a-unity-upgrade-with-lots-of-optimizations/page=1/ [secondary]. For us: render the world below 1440p and keep the text native, our biggest lever on per-pixel cost.
- **Ori and the Will of the Wisps:** Unity. Six painted light-direction maps per asset combine into a pseudo-normal map that responds to dynamic light, about 30,000 maps in all, because generated lighting looked "very cheap [and] plasticky" https://www.thegamer.com/ori-and-the-will-of-the-wisps-artists-hand-painted-30000-light-maps/ [primary quote, secondary report]. Switch got rewritten rendering with less detail in the screen corners https://www.thegamer.com/ori-and-the-will-of-the-wisps-art-interview/ [secondary]. For us: bake an authored light response per part in Blender.
- **Outer Wilds:** Unity 5. It used "3D fog lookups so that we could have our fog density and color vary based on the radius of a sphere", and the sun's position. Detail split: "By using geometry for small scale detail and textures for large-scale gradients" https://www.mobiusdigitalgames.com/news/taking-on-timber-hearth [primary]. For us: look fog colour up by distance and sun angle.
- **Dredge:** Unity (Wikipedia). "We had our own solution for creating the fog". One mask texture packs ripples, fog reach and depth. Assets share small gradient maps (64×64 or 128×128), with detail in geometry https://unity.com/resources/crafting-dredge-cozy-atmospheric-horror [primary quotes]. For us: a shared palette-gradient atlas indexed by material facts, swappable per pack.
- **Cocoon:** Unity per an uncited Wikipedia infobox [secondary]. The GDC 2024 talk "Pondering Orbs" (Mikkel Svendsen) covers froxel fog and SH volumetric lightmaps "with an MSAA-friendly rendering pipeline", on platforms including Switch https://www.gdcvault.com/play/1034619/Pondering-Orbs-The-Rendering-and [primary abstract]. For us: the nearest peer to our stack. The video is worth getting.
- **Disco Elysium:** Unity (Wikipedia). Backgrounds are painted over rendered heightmaps, with NPCs and particles added in real time https://www.gamebanshee.com/k4fuh [secondary]. The GDC 2020 talk is "Turpentine Fumes and Shaders" https://gdconf.com/news/come-gdc-and-see-how-disco-elysiums-unique-style-was-achieved-0 [primary abstract]. For us: this needs a fixed camera and world. Only our far land and sky qualify.
- **Pentiment:** Unity per an uncited Wikipedia infobox https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pentiment_(video_game) [secondary]. Hand-drawn art was "way too time-consuming to revise repeatedly", so scenes started as "simple, uncolored roughs" https://www.gamedeveloper.com/art/deep-dive-the-art-of-pentiment [primary]. For us: support for greybox first.
- **Unpacking:** Unity (Wikipedia). I found no rendering source, and its GDC talk is about narrative. For us: nothing here.
- **A Short Hike:** Unity, rendering to a small texture enlarged with point filtering https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Short_Hike [secondary]. "flat cohesive shading and no anti-aliasing", with "a soft outline effect to objects to help them stand out" https://blog.playstation.com/2021/08/05/crafting-a-tiny-open-world-a-look-behind-the-scenes-at-the-creation-of-a-short-hike/ [primary]. For us: weigh lower internal resolution against MSAA.

## Five lessons that recur

1. **Fog, light and grade carry the look; materials stay simple.** Firewatch's grey rocks, Sable's fog, INSIDE's fog and silhouettes, Tunic's flat colour. Spend milliseconds on light and fog, not material complexity.
2. **Narrow, directed tricks beat general automatic features.** INSIDE's decals instead of SSAO and GI, Genshin's penumbra mask, Ori's painted light, custom fog in Firewatch, Dredge and Outer Wilds. Put an aimed alternative against each of our general effects.
3. **Spread the cost over time and pixels.** Interleaved cascades, shadow maps spread over frames, undersampled volumes, less detail in the corners, and internal resolution below output (A Short Hike, Valheim, Obra Dinn). Our cost is per-pixel, so render scale and amortised shadows come first.
4. **Noise and transitions are designed.** Blue noise, dither before every quantise, patterns pinned to the world, fades before pop-in, streaming without hitches. Fade earlier, then stabilise the pattern.
5. **Early constraints make the look cheap; skipped ones return as patches.** Genshin's "Avoid excessive features" and INSIDE's fixed camera were chosen early. Sable's culling and Firewatch's hitches were fixed after launch. Budget each system now and keep the check measuring it.
