# Question test for the docs trim

Thirty questions on the project's rules and design decisions, each hinging on a condition, a number, an exception or what would reopen it, to be answered from the always-read files after the trim and compared with the answers from before it.

Q1. At the start of a session, how much of Understory's latest comments and notes does a session read, when does it read one in full, and how does it treat what it reads?

Q2. What limits hold on the notes Overstory sends Understory (length, frequency, replies), and what happens when a note arrives from the other side?

Q3. When may one element reuse another element's part, and when must it share a system with it instead?

Q4. Work reveals an area nobody has studied, or an assumption that turns out wrong. What must the session do, and what is allowed for things that can only be learned along the way?

Q5. A session thinks the plan's order or scope should change. What must it say, what does not count as grounds, and who decides?

Q6. When should a session start fresh, and what does it do if a step calls for a different model than the one it runs on?

Q7. When may a leaner review packet replace the full set of frames in a strict review?

Q8. The world on screen starts in 1989. What role does the past before 1989 play, which era content gets made, and what happens for a game that starts earlier?

Q9. Which questions go to Øystein, in what form, and who settles look and craft questions such as the night sky's colour or the tonemapper?

Q10. During LT1, when does `main` take the experiment branch, and what happens to commits in between?

Q11. When is a kind of thing "done", and how is its state written before then?

Q12. How does the frame-rate gate work: what is its baseline, what does not count against it, what stops a step, and until when does it apply?

Q13. Before the port's foundation steps (4 and 5) count as done, what must their formats pass, and when may a format change or gain a field?

Q14. After a kind is ported to C#, what becomes of its Python source, and on what condition is it removed?

Q15. What anti-aliasing does the port build for, what may nothing rely on, and when and how is the final filter chosen?

Q16. What does the gully's accepted golden baseline prove and not prove, and is fixing one of its known defects a regression?

Q17. How must the cost of a shader or of the history layers be measured, and why is a stand-in scene not enough?

Q18. Can a player read the year of a derived patch, and what is done if a game needs that year?

Q19. How many histories may a single thing tell at each view, how are marks from one cause drawn, and how firm are those numbers?

Q20. Is the town drawn the same whether it was watched day by day or reached by a jump, and may living through the years produce a different history from jumping over them?

Q21. Which part of the first town is laid out by hand and which by rules, how are the rule-made parts checked, and at what scale is the city-scale exit criterion tested?

Q22. What can the engine's human test do for Overstory, which exit criterion can it not test, and what would that criterion need instead?

Q23. May a scene or a player's read rest on a told account as the reason for an event, and until when?

Q24. At a cold-reader gate, what must the shuffled-facts and history-layers-off control arms show for the result to count?

Q25. What share of a frame may its middle values take, what exception is allowed and on what condition, and at what size must the frame read before colour is judged?

Q26. What does a gap in the research coverage mean for building, and when is the craft of management and data interfaces studied?

Q27. If a site fact around a tree changes (a house built beside it in 1975), what is regrown, and how is a change to the growth rules themselves handled?

Q28. When a facade part still does not fit, in what order does it give way, and how does a part the diary names differ from one the resolver chose?

Q29. In a repair campaign, at what point does spot work become work on the whole part, and what material does the crew use?

Q30. When the engine's facts disagree with Overstory's curated 1989 starting record, what happens to our facts, what is redrawn, and what becomes of a curation pin that contradicts them?
