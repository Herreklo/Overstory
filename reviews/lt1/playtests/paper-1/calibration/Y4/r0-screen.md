# Round 0

A paper version of Return of the Obra Dinn.

**May 1812, off Uleåborg.** The barque *Hesper* of Hull froze into the Gulf of Bothnia in November and drifted out with the thaw. Fishermen towed her in. No one aboard is alive; her jolly boat is gone. The underwriters sent you with a book and a watch. Held to a body, the watch plays the last moments before that death, then lets you walk through the instant, frozen. Your task: name every soul aboard and say what became of them. The book confirms fates only three at a time, and never says which three.

**Aboard, now.** In the sail locker, four canvas shrouds sewn shut: two with small, even stitches, two with big, clumsy ones. Forward in the cable tier, a man in leg irons, unshrouded. In the captain's cabin: a stove, a chart with a pencil line across the ice to an island, an empty shelf where the log should be.

You cut open one of the clumsy shrouds. A young man, his temple crushed. You open the watch.

**The Bread Room, 9 February 1812.** *Dark. Wind against the hull.*
A slow voice, Tyneside vowels: "Put it back. That's the boy's share, and the clerk's."
A Hull voice, close and breathless: "The boy's eaten better than me all week, and you know it."
A second Hull voice, from above: "George, leave it. Mr Carr, he's only hungry, he's—"
The slow voice: "Thief."
A blow. Biscuit scattering. Nothing.

**The frozen moment.** A narrow store lined with lockers, a lantern on a hook. Face down in spilled ship's biscuit, a man in a canvas jacket, a red knitted cap fallen by his head, a bread bag in his fist. Over him, a stout man in a plain-buttoned blue coat, frost in his mutton-chop whiskers, a belaying pin raised. On the ladder from the hatch, a man in a red knitted cap with a split lower lip, reaching down. In the doorway to the hold, a heavy man in a stained apron, a ladle through his belt, keys and a lamp in his hands. Behind him, a man in spectacles, a frock coat over his nightshirt. Crouched behind a cask, a boy clutching a cat.

**The book**
- **Crew list:** Jonas Whitlock, Captain, Hull · Edmund Carr, First Mate, Newcastle · Lars Ekström, Second Mate, Gävle · Isaac Dunning, Carpenter, Scarborough · Rhys Morgan, Boatswain, Swansea · Antti Rautio, Sailmaker, Uleåborg · Abel Moss, Cook, Hull · George Pell, Seaman, Hull · Thomas Pell, Seaman, Hull · Hamish Reid, Seaman, Leith · Jens Holm, Seaman, Copenhagen · Michael Quill, Seaman, Cork · Ned Fairley, Ship's Boy, Hull · Oskar Lind, Passenger (timber merchant's clerk), Uleåborg.
- **Sketch** of the crew, Hull, October 1811, signed "O.L.": 1 older man, brass buttons, speaking trumpet · 2 stout, mutton-chops, blue coat · 3 young, fair beard, short jacket, pipe · 4 thin, balding, leather apron · 5 broad, whistle on a cord · 6 lean, fur hat, leather pad strapped to his palm · 7 heavy, apron, ladle in belt · 8 and 9 side by side in red knitted caps, 9 with a split lip · 10 ginger beard, bonnet · 11 long pale hair, earring · 12 pockmarked, neckerchief · 13 boy with a cat · 14 spectacles, frock coat, pencil.
- **Deck plan:** captain's cabin and bread room aft; the hold and sail locker amidships; forecastle and cable tier forward.
- **Causes:** clubbed, stabbed, shot, crushed, drowned, fell, froze, starved, illness; or left the ship alive.

**What you can do** (each is a turn): walk the frozen scene and look closely at anyone or anything; study the book; write fates as "Sketch N is <name>: <cause>, by <whom>"; hold the watch to another body; search the ship as it is now.
