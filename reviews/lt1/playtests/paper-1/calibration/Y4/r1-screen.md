# Round 1

## Turn 1.1: the two men in red caps

You walk the frozen bread room. The lantern on its hook throws everything one way; the biscuit on the floor is grey with weevil dust.

**The man face down.** His head has turned to the left as he fell, so the right side of his face lies uppermost on the biscuit. Young: twenty, perhaps, with a few days of fair stubble and hollows under the cheekbone. His eyes are open. His lower lip is whole. Above the right ear the temple is broken in, the hair there dark and matted. The red knitted cap lies a hand's width from his head, inside out, as if it came off with the blow. His right fist is closed round the neck of a canvas bread bag; the knuckles are raw and chapped, the nails bitten to the quick. In the bag, its mouth pulled open by his grip: six ship's biscuits, whole.

**The man on the ladder.** Three rungs down from the hatch, one hand on the rail, the other reaching towards the man on the floor, fingers spread. A red knitted cap pulled low. Through the middle of his lower lip runs an old white scar, healed long ago, which splits the lip in two. His face has the same heavy brows as the man on the floor, and the same broad, flattened nose. His mouth is open on a word.

**The pin.** Held high in the stout man's right hand, the heavy end towards the ceiling. That end is dark and wet; a few short fair hairs are stuck to it.

## Turn 1.2: the stout man, the cook, the man in spectacles

**The stout man.** Thick in the body but not in the face: above the mutton-chop whiskers the cheeks have fallen in, and there is frost in the whiskers and on his eyebrows. His eyes are on the man at his feet. His mouth is shut and set; there is nothing startled in it. A plain blue coat with plain dark horn buttons, all fastened to the throat. A leather belt over the coat with nothing on it. No keys.

**The cook.** In the doorway to the hold, filling it. A heavy man, the apron stained brown down the front, a wooden ladle pushed through his belt. In his left hand, a tin lamp, lit. In his right, held up as though he had just turned it in a lock, an iron ring of four keys: three old and dark, one bright and new, its bit cut square. His mouth is open; his eyes are on the pin.

**The man in spectacles.** Behind the cook's shoulder, on tiptoe to see past him. A frock coat thrown over a nightshirt, bare shins, felt slippers. Round spectacles, one lens cracked across. In his right hand, a stub of pencil. In his left, pressed shut against his chest with one finger keeping a page, a small book bound in brown card.

## Turn 1.3: the cable tier

Forward, down a short ladder, the cable tier: low enough that you stoop, dark, smelling of tarred hemp and old cold. The anchor cable lies coiled in great loops filling most of the space.

**The man in irons.** He sits against the coil with his legs out, wrapped to the chin in a grey blanket. An iron band round his right ankle, its chain run to a ringbolt in the deck beam; the chain is just long enough for him to reach the ladder's foot, not to climb it. A red knitted cap. A lean man, the blanket loose on him. Through the middle of his lower lip, an old white scar that splits it in two. His lips are cracked and grey. Under the blanket, a canvas jacket, a seaman's. His hands lie in his lap: chapped, the nails broken, the fingertips black.

Within his reach: a small water cask, its bung out, dry inside. Beside it, a crust of bread wrapped in a cloth, the cloth still folded over it. On the beam above his head, cut into the wood with something sharp, nine short marks in a row. On a nail by the ladder, beyond the end of the chain, a key.

You hold the watch to him.

**The Cable Tier, 23 April 1812.** *Wind. Far off, ice grinding against the hull, a long, slow sound.*
A hoarse voice, Hull, very weak: "Ned? That you?"
Nothing.
"... Ned."
Silence. A long breath out.

**The frozen moment.** The same cable tier, darker; frost on the inside of the planking. The man in the red knitted cap with the split lip, chained by the ankle to the ringbolt, the grey blanket round him, his head tipped back against the coil, his face to the ladder. Frost on his lips. The water cask beside him, empty. The crust of bread in its cloth, untouched. On the beam above him, the nine marks, the last one fresh and pale. On the nail by the ladder, the key. No one else is here.

## Where you stand

In the cable tier, now, beside the man in irons. Aft of you, the ship as it is: the forecastle; amidships the hold and the sail locker, with three shrouds still sewn shut (two neat, one clumsy); aft, the bread room, the officers' cabins and the captain's cabin.
