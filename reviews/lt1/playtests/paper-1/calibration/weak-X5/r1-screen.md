# Round 1

**Turn 1.1. Wilf Garrow.**
You say: "Do you need a hand with that?"
Wilf takes a rag from his back pocket without looking and holds it out. "Catch that drip. There. Good." You catch it before it reaches the keel. He nods at the boat. "She'll see me out, this one." The brush goes back and forth along the third plank up, slow as breathing. "Forty-one years I took the Puffin over to the island. Rob has her now. He doesn't talk to the passengers. I talked to every one."

**Turn 1.2. Tolley & Son's window.**
You crouch to the rubbed corner. The glass is cold and smells of old putty. Through it: empty shelves, a coil of rope still on its hook, and on the back wall a calendar open at March 1988. The door beside the window is locked.

**Turn 1.3. Teddy Marsh.**
You ask him about the peephole in the chandler's window. He doesn't look up from the line. "I rubbed it. There's nothing in there. Just rope." He tips the red bucket a little so you can see the crab inside, sideways and indignant. "Just Admiral. He always comes back. I catch him every morning and let him go at teatime. He doesn't mind."

**Turn 1.4. The bench.**
The plaque is brass, screwed to the backrest, the letters filled with green where the polish doesn't reach:
FOR MAY, WHO LIKED IT HERE. 1921–1990.

---

**About noon. The Strand.** The cloud has come in low over the headland and thickened, and the water has gone the colour of pewter. The tide has turned. Wilf is still on his knees at the third plank. A smell of frying drifts from the Kettle.
