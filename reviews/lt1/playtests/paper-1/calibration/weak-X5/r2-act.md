## Thinking aloud
I'm still writing down the good lines ("sideways and indignant"), but the town keeps answering my questions with pretty dead ends. At +16 every four turns, Belonging fills in about 25 turns whatever I do. That's a timer pretending not to be one. There are two possible threads with any pull. The first is Wilf and Rob: forty-one years of talking to every passenger, and a son who talks to none. May (1921–1990) fits Wilf's age as his wife, and Rob's mother. The second is the chandler's: shut in March 1988, two years before May died, so it's a different loss. Teddy says there's nothing in there, but kids don't polish a peephole to look at rope. A postwoman would know whether anyone still gets letters at a shut shop. And I'll test the one verb the game is built on: sit and watch.
