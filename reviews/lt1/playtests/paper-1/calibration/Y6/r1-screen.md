# Round 1

**Act 1, floor 6. Elite: Gremlin Nob.**

## Turn 1

You play **Bash+** (2 energy). 10 damage, Nob is Vulnerable: **15**. Nob 84 → **69**. Vulnerable 1 + 3 = **4**.

You leave the four Defends in hand and end your turn with 1 energy unspent. The Defends go to the discard pile.

**Nob: Bellow.** He throws his head back and roars. A new icon appears beside his health bar:
> **Enrage 2.** Whenever you play a Skill, gain 2 Strength.

End of round: Nob's Vulnerable 4 → 3.

## Turn 2

You draw: **Pommel Strike, Strike, Strike, Anger, Strike.** Energy 3/3. Block 0.
**Nob's intent: Attack 14.**

- **Pommel Strike** (1): 9 × 1.5 = 13.5, rounded down: **13**. Nob 69 → **56**. You draw 1: **Shrug It Off**.
- **Anger** (0): 6 × 1.5 = **9**. Nob 56 → **47**. A copy of Anger goes into your discard pile.
- **Strike** (1): **9**. Nob 47 → **38**.
- **Strike** (1): **9**. Nob 38 → **29**.

Energy 0. Left in hand: Strike, Shrug It Off. Fire Potion (20) would leave him on 9, so you keep it. Nob's 14 would leave you on 44, not under 30, so you keep the Block Potion. You end your turn; the Strike and Shrug It Off go to the discard pile.

**Nob: Rush.** He charges with his club up. **14** damage, no Block. You: 58 → **44/80**.

End of round: Nob's Vulnerable 3 → 2. Nob's Strength: 0 (no Skills played).

## Turn 3

Your draw pile holds 2 cards: you draw **Strike, Strike**. The draw pile is empty, so your discard pile (12 cards) is shuffled into a new draw pile, and you draw 3 more: **Defend, Shrug It Off, Strike.**

**Hand:** Strike, Strike, Strike, Defend, Shrug It Off. Energy 3/3.
**Nob:** HP 29/84. Vulnerable 2. Enrage 2. Strength 0.
**Nob's intent: Attack 14.**

Three Strikes make 27. Shrug It Off would draw the top card of the new pile, Anger, but paying for it leaves energy for only two Strikes: 9 + 9 + 9 = 27 again. The cards alone fall 2 short. With the Fire Potion, he dies.

- **Strike:** 9. Nob 29 → **20**.
- **Strike:** 9. Nob 20 → **11**.
- **Strike:** 9. Nob 11 → **2**.
- **Fire Potion:** **20** (the potion's damage is flat; Vulnerable doesn't touch it). Nob 2 → **0**.

Gremlin Nob crumples onto his club.

**Victory.** Turns: 3. Damage taken: 14.
**Burning Blood:** heal 6. HP 44 → **50/80**.

The extra Anger copy was made in combat and disappears with it. Your deck is 13 cards again.
