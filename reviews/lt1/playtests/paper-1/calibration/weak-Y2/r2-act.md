## Thinking aloud
Belonging went up 16% and I got a geranium in a yoghurt pot for catching a varnish drip, meeting a crab and reading a plaque. That is a number paying me for turning up. The geranium is sweet, I'll admit, but I want to earn things by deciding, not by existing. I also noticed that I inspected four things and was never offered a sketch. I don't know what is sketchable or how to tell.

The journal again wrote my feelings for me ("I can see why"). It's getting under my skin a little.

What's actually pulling me is the chandler's shop. The shop has been shut since March 1988, Teddy rubbed the window to look at a coil of rope, and Wilf varnishes one plank at a time while the boat sits dry. May, 1921 to 1990, "who liked it here," sits on a bench where three people are all quietly avoiding saying something. I have a hunch these are one story: someone in the shop, or in the family, left in 1988, and the town goes around the gap. The cardigan on the noticeboard postcard is probably a door into the sisters, so I'll walk it through. I'll ask the town, not the signs.

## Prediction
The Hales will know who May was and what happened at Tolley's in 1988, and the cardigan is theirs. The sisters have watched the stair all their lives; they would have seen anyone go up or down it that spring.
