# Round 1

## Saturday morning

**Turn 1.1. Wilf Garrow**
You say: "Do you need a hand with that?"
He hands you a rag without looking up. "Catch that drip. There. Good." A bead of varnish was creeping down towards the keel; you take it off with the rag, and he nods and goes on with the brush, short strokes along the grain.
"She'll see me out, this one."
He dips the brush and wipes it on the lip of the tin. "Forty-one years I took the Puffin over to the island. Rob has her now." He tips his head at the little blue boat by the steps, where the skipper is still coiling the same rope. "He doesn't talk to the passengers. I talked to every one."
The plank under his brush is the third up from the keel, glossy as toffee. The rest of the Marigold is dull and dry, waiting.
*Next time, with Wilf:* 1. "How's she coming along?" 2. "Can I help today?" 3. "Tell me about the Puffin."

**Turn 1.2. Tolley & Son, the rubbed corner**
You bend to the clear patch in the whitewash and cup your hands to the glass. Inside, the light is brown and still. Empty shelves to the ceiling, their edges pale where tins once stood. A coil of rope still on its hook behind the counter. On the far wall, a calendar from a marine paint company, open on March 1988: a photograph of a trawler in a heavy sea. Dust on everything.

**Turn 1.3. Teddy Marsh**
You say: "Caught anything?"
He tips the red bucket towards you. One crab, the colour of wet slate, sitting in an inch of seawater with its claws folded. "Just Admiral. He always comes back."
He winds a turn of line round his finger. "I catch him every morning and let him go at teatime. He doesn't mind."
You ask about his plans, and whether he's the one who rubbed the chandler's window.
"I rubbed it." He doesn't look round. "There's nothing in there. Just rope."
*Next time, with Teddy:* 1. "Caught anything?" 2. "What's the bait?" 3. "Can I have a go?"

**Turn 1.4. The bench**
A slatted bench, silvered by the weather, facing the water. The brass plaque is screwed to the backrest, its letters worn soft by sleeves and shoulders:
FOR MAY, WHO LIKED IT HERE.
1921–1990.
A gull lands on the far end of the bench, looks at you, and leaves.

## The Strand, about noon

The cloud has thickened from the west and the water has gone the colour of pewter. The tide is turning; weed lifts on the quay steps.

**In view**
- **The Kettle.** The radio is on to the lunchtime news. **June Askew** is wiping the table by the window.
- **Wilf Garrow**, at the top of the slip, varnishing the third plank of the **Marigold**.
- **Teddy Marsh**, at the quay's edge, his line in the water. **Admiral** is in the red bucket.
- **Tolley & Son, Ship Chandlers**, whitewashed windows, the rubbed corner.
- **The noticeboard**, by the **phone box**. LANTERN WALK · TONIGHT.
- **The ferry board**: EIDER ISLAND · THE PUFFIN · 10.30 · 13.00 · 15.30. The **Puffin** at the steps, **Rob Garrow** in the stern, coiling a rope.
- **The bench**, FOR MAY.
- **The brown Volvo**, **Skipper** asleep in the back.
- **The foot of Wren Steps.** **Sal Brennan** on the low wall, sorting letters, her red bicycle beside her.
- **The Hales' window**, halfway up the steps, two white heads behind the geraniums.
- **Mooring rings** along the quay edge.
