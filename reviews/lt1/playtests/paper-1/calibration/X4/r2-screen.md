# Round 2

**Turn 1. The second clumsy shroud, opened.**

The stitches are big and uneven, pulled so tight in places the canvas has puckered. Inside: a stout man, frost long gone from his mutton-chop whiskers, in a blue broadcloth coat with plain horn buttons. The coat is cut through on the left side below the ribs; the cloth round the cut is black and stiff. You open the watch.

**The Watch, 1 March 1812, night.** *Wind. A lantern creaking on its hook. Footsteps on a frozen deck, slow, then stopping.*
A Tyneside voice: "Pell? You're not on this watch. Get below."
A Hull voice, quiet, very close: "No. This is for George."
A grunt. A body falls.

**The frozen moment.** The quarterdeck under stars, and ice to the horizon in every direction, grey-white and ridged. The lantern hangs from its hook at an angle. The stout man with mutton-chop whiskers sags against the binnacle, a knife in his side. Holding the knife: the man in the red knitted cap with the split lip, his face a hand's breadth from the other man's. At the rail, turned and frozen in shock, a pockmarked man in a neckerchief. Climbing out of the forecastle hatch, mouth open mid-shout, a man with a ginger beard and a bonnet.

**Turn 2. A neat shroud, opened.**

The stitches are small and even, each the same length as the last, the seam as straight as a ruled line. Inside: a thin, balding man. His chest is crushed in on one side. There is sawdust in the creases of his knuckles. You open the watch.

**The Pressure, 3 December 1811, afternoon.** *Timber groaning: a long creak, like a door in wind.*
A Welsh voice, sing-song even when shouting: "Out of there, Isaac, she's going!"
A Yorkshire-coast voice, tight with effort: "One more shore. Hold that lamp steady, Tom!"
A crack like a musket. A scream, cut off.

**The frozen moment.** The hold. The hull planking is bowed inward and split along its length, and grey ice shows through the split. The thin, balding man in a leather apron lies pinned under a sprung frame timber, a maul still in his hand. Holding the lantern above him: the man in the red knitted cap with the split lower lip. Halfway down the ladder: a broad man, a whistle on a cord swinging from his neck, mouth open mid-shout. Passing timber from the shadows: a lean man in a fur hat, a leather pad on his palm. At the foot of the ladder, the boy, holding a bucket of tools. No cat.

**Turn 3. The forecastle, searched.**

A low triangle of a room in the bows, the deck sloping up to the stem. It smells of damp wool, tobacco and lamp oil.
- **Hammocks:** nine still slung from the beams. Seven have been stripped of their blankets; two still have theirs, rolled at the foot.
- **Pegs** along the beams, where oilskins and sea bags would hang: bare.
- **Sea chests,** initials burnt into the lids: G.P. and T.P. side by side against the same beam, both half full. H.R. and M.Q., lids up, empty. J.H., lid shut: a full chest, clothes folded square, untouched.
- No names are cut in the beams or the hammocks.

## Now

**The forecastle, May 1812.** Grey light comes down the ladder from the deck. Overhead, a fisherman walks the length of the *Hesper* and back, his boots loud on the planking, and calls something in Finnish to someone in the boat.

**Aboard, now.**
- **Sail locker,** amidships: one shroud still sewn, with small even stitches. Three lie open: the young man with the crushed temple, the stout man with the knife wound, the thin man crushed in the hold.
- **Cable tier,** forward: the man in irons.
- **To search:** the officers' cabins, the captain's cabin, the galley and bread room, the hold, the deck. (The forecastle you have searched.)

**The book**
- **Crew list:** Jonas Whitlock, Captain, Hull · Edmund Carr, First Mate, Newcastle · Lars Ekström, Second Mate, Gävle · Isaac Dunning, Carpenter, Scarborough · Rhys Morgan, Boatswain, Swansea · Antti Rautio, Sailmaker, Uleåborg · Abel Moss, Cook, Hull · George Pell, Seaman, Hull · Thomas Pell, Seaman, Hull · Hamish Reid, Seaman, Leith · Jens Holm, Seaman, Copenhagen · Michael Quill, Seaman, Cork · Ned Fairley, Ship's Boy, Hull · Oskar Lind, Passenger (timber merchant's clerk), Uleåborg.
- **Sketch,** Hull, October 1811, signed "O.L.": 1 older man, brass buttons, speaking trumpet · 2 stout, mutton-chops, blue coat · 3 young, fair beard, short jacket, pipe · 4 thin, balding, leather apron · 5 broad, whistle on a cord · 6 lean, fur hat, leather pad strapped to his palm · 7 heavy, apron, ladle in belt · 8 and 9 side by side in red knitted caps, 9 with a split lip · 10 ginger beard, bonnet · 11 long pale hair, earring · 12 pockmarked, neckerchief · 13 boy with a cat · 14 spectacles, frock coat, pencil.
- **Memories,** in order of date: The Pressure, 3 December 1811 · The Bread Room, 9 February 1812 · The Watch, 1 March 1812 · The Cable Tier, 23 April 1812.
- **Fates written:** none.
- **Deck plan:** captain's cabin and bread room aft; the hold and sail locker amidships; forecastle and cable tier forward.
- **Causes:** clubbed, stabbed, shot, crushed, drowned, fell, froze, starved, illness; or left the ship alive.
