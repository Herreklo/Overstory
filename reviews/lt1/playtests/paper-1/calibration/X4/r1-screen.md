# Round 1

**Turn 1. The Bread Room, walked.** A walk through a frozen scene is one turn, however much of it you look at.

You step into the instant. The lantern on its hook has stopped mid-swing, its light leaning to one side. Biscuit hangs in the air above the dead man's back like thrown gravel.

- **The store.** Lockers line both sides, their doors standing open on bare shelves. At the head of the ladder the hatch is open on darkness; its hasp has been wrenched out of the coaming, screws and all, and the padlock is still shut on it. By the dead man's knee, a cask with its head off: a hand's depth of biscuit at the bottom, no more. The bag in his fist is half full.
- **The keys.** The heavy man in the doorway to the hold holds an iron ring of five keys, one large and four small, on a tarred cord looped to his belt. He is in his stockings. His lamp is lit.
- **The clerk.** Behind the cook, in the dark of the hold: spectacles, a frock coat thrown over a nightshirt, his hands empty. A pencil stub behind one ear; the corner of a small notebook shows at his coat pocket. His mouth is open.
- **The stout man's coat.** Blue broadcloth, worn shiny at the cuffs, darned at one elbow. Plain horn buttons; no braid, no brass. The end of the belaying pin in his raised hand is dark.

There is no one else in the store. The boy behind the cask has both arms round the cat, his face pressed into its fur.

**Turn 2. The cable tier, searched.**

Forward, down a short ladder, the cable tier is so low you stoop. The anchor cable lies coiled on the deck, thick as a man's thigh, tarred and stiff. The smell is tar, bilge and cold.

The man sits against the curve of the hull, wrapped to the chin in a grey wool blanket. A red knitted cap. His lower lip is split by an old scar, healed white. No whiskers; a few weeks' stubble. Under the blanket, a canvas jacket. His hands are inside the blanket, crossed over his chest.

An iron shackle is locked on his left ankle; four feet of chain run from it to a ringbolt in the frame. On a nail at the foot of the ladder hangs a key. The chain stops an arm's length short of it.

Beside him, a small water cask, its bung out, dry. A crust of bread wrapped in a cloth, the knot still tied. On the beam above his head, nine short scratches in a row.

**Turn 3. The watch, held to the man in irons.**

**The Cable Tier, 23 April 1812.** *Wind. Far off, ice grinding against the hull.*
A Hull voice, hoarse and very weak: "Ned? That you?"
A long wait.
"Ned."
Silence. A long breath out.

**The frozen moment.** The same low dark space, the cable coiled like a sleeping animal. The man in the red knitted cap with the split lip, chained by the ankle to the ringbolt, the blanket round him, frost on his lips. Within his reach, the water cask, empty, and the crust in its cloth, untouched. On the beam above him, nine scratched marks. On the nail by the ladder, out of his reach, the key. No one else is here.

## Now

**The cable tier, May 1812.** The watch closes in your hand and the dark comes back to ordinary dark. Water drips somewhere aft. Through the hull you can hear the fishermen's boat that towed you in, knocking against the *Hesper*'s side as the swell lifts it.

**Aboard, now.**
- **Sail locker,** amidships: three canvas shrouds still sewn shut, two with small even stitches and one with big clumsy ones. The fourth lies open: the young man with the crushed temple.
- **Cable tier,** forward: the man in irons. You are here.
- **To search:** the forecastle, the officers' cabins, the captain's cabin, the galley and bread room, the hold, the deck.

**The book**

- **Deck plan:** captain's cabin and bread room aft; the hold and sail locker amidships; forecastle and cable tier forward.
- **Causes:** clubbed, stabbed, shot, crushed, drowned, fell, froze, starved, illness; or left the ship alive.
