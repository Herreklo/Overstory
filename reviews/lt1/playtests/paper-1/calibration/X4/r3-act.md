## Thinking aloud
Sorted so far: 2 Carr, 4 Dunning (Scarborough, Yorkshire coast), 5 Morgan (Welsh, whistle), 6 Rautio (sailmaker's palm), 7 Moss, 8 George, 9 Thomas, 13 Ned, 14 Lind. The bonnet says Leith, so 10 is Hamish Reid. The long pale hair says Copenhagen, so 11 is Jens Holm and 12 is Michael Quill (Cork). Reid and Quill saw Carr die, then emptied their chests and went. They're likely the ones who chained Thomas and walked to the island.

The big thought: Rautio is from Uleåborg, and the clerk is too. The neat stitches didn't stop because the sailmaker died. They stopped because the one man who knew this ice took the others home across it. So the last neat shroud is an early death, from before anyone left.

Where's the cat? It wasn't there in December, the boy held it in February, and Ned hasn't turned up living or dead. Thomas called for him in April.

## Prediction
The last neat shroud is Jens Holm. His chest is full and untouched because he died early, in December or January, before anyone packed to leave, of illness or a fall.
