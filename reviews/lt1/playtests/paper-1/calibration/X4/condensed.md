# Condensed: X4, Return of the Obra Dinn (paper), the anchor

Explorer persona, opus. Three rounds, nine turns, from the plan; one seeded roll (which neat shroud: A, Dunning).

**Arc.** R1: walked the Bread Room, searched the man in irons, watched him (The Cable Tier). R2: opened the clumsy shroud (The Watch) and a neat one (The Pressure), searched the forecastle. R3: wrote three fates (all right), opened the last shroud (The Stove), searched the captain's cabin. Ended 3 of 14 confirmed, five memories seen.

**Turns by verb.** Watch 4, search 3, walk 1, write fates 1.

**Pull by round.** R1 8, R2 8, R3 8; exit overall 8.

**Marks.** Engaged 5, curious 4; no bored, confused or would-quit. Delight on four: proved wrong about the man in irons by his split lip; J.H.'s untouched chest; the January crossing; Ned's drawing.

**What it valued.** Evidence read in the ship as it is now (stitches, the torn hasp, the chests) over evidence heard. Choosing the order of memories.

**What was hollow, by its account.** Names spoken in the memories make identification a lookup; the frozen moments list everyone, so nothing can be missed; the confirm-in-threes rule never bit because it wrote only sure fates; the revenge line was the obvious one; The Cable Tier is nearly empty.

**Predictions.** Three: wrong (the man in irons is Carr), right (Carr stabbed by Thomas), partly (the last neat shroud is Holm, early; cause wrong). At the end it named 10, 11 and 12 rightly, and misread Rautio as guiding the others home.

**Needs that recurred.** Who chained Thomas and who left when; a way to tell froze from starved. Unmet when the session ended.

**Rendering notes.** A stray pipe stem cut before sending; one sound line harmonised with its frozen moment.
