# GM notes: X4, Return of the Obra Dinn (paper)

Calibration run. Player kind: explorer. Twin run: Y4 (same opening, same ship). Render the game at its best and no better: sound first, then the frozen scene to walk; faces readable only when the player looks; the book as the only notebook; inference across scenes (accents, clothes, who stands beside whom, who is named, where people sleep); confirmation only in threes, never saying which. Don't explain deductions; don't hint beyond what the scene shows. Answer "look closely at X" with exactly what is visible there.

The *Hesper* is original to this calibration (not the real game's ship or story).

## Round plan
- **R1 to R3:** each turn is a look, a body, a search or a set of fates. Each **r-screen:** what was seen or heard, any new memory in full (sound, then the frozen moment), and the confirmation line if three or more fates are newly correct ("Three fates are confirmed" / "Six fates..."). Wrong guesses get no comment.
- **r3-screen** also: the count of confirmed fates, then exit questions: overall pull 0 to 10, how this paper session compares with the real game, what was best and worst.

## Confirmation rule
A fate is correct when the sketch number, the name and the cause are right, and the killer when there is one. Each time the number of correct fates crosses a multiple of three, announce it. Count all fates the player has written so far (latest version of each).

Story: frozen in near Hailuoto island in November. 3 Dec, the ice crushes the hull inward and kills the carpenter. 14 Jan, Ekström, Rautio and Holm set out across the ice for the island; Holm goes through and the other two drag him back; he dies by the cabin stove on 15 Jan. Rautio sews his shroud. 16 Jan, Ekström and Rautio set out again and don't come back (no one aboard learns their fate; they reached the island). After that the shrouds are sewn by clumsier hands. 9 Feb, rations short; Carr kills George Pell over stolen biscuit. 1 Mar, Thomas Pell stabs Carr on night watch. 2 Mar, the captain puts Thomas in irons in the cable tier for trial at Hull. 14 Apr, the ice starts to break; the seven left take the jolly boat across the ice to Hailuoto. The captain won't take a murderer in an open boat. The boy leaves Thomas bread, water and a promise. Thomas dies about nine days later. The captain took the log.

## The bodies and their memories
**Neat shroud A: Isaac Dunning. "The Pressure," the hold, 3 December 1811, afternoon.**
Sound: timber groaning, a long creak like a door in wind. A Welsh voice, sing-song even when shouting: "Out of there, Isaac, she's going!" A Yorkshire-coast voice, tight with effort: "One more shore. Hold that lamp steady, Tom!" A crack like a musket. A scream, cut off.
Frozen: the hull planking bowed inward, split, grey ice showing through. A thin, balding man in a leather apron pinned under a sprung frame timber, a maul still in his hand. Holding the lantern: a man in a red knitted cap with a split lower lip. Halfway down the ladder: a broad man, a whistle on a cord swinging from his neck, mouth open mid-shout. Passing timber from the shadows: a lean man in a fur hat, a leather pad on his palm. At the foot of the ladder, the boy, holding a bucket of tools (no cat).

**Neat shroud B: Jens Holm. "The Stove," the captain's cabin, 15 January 1812, night.**
Sound: the stove ticking. A Swedish voice, urgent, half in Swedish: "Jens. Jens! Stay awake, you hear me? Håll dig vaken." A Finnish voice, few words, flat: "The water had him to the chest. Too long." An older Hull voice, tired: "More blankets. Mr Ekström, you'll try again at first light, you and Rautio both." Breathing that stops.
Frozen: a long-haired man with an earring, soaked and blue, laid on blankets by the stove; ice in his hair. Kneeling at his head: a young man with a fair beard in a short blue jacket, a pipe in his pocket. Standing by the door, pulling on a fur hat: the lean man with the leather palm pad. Holding out a blanket: an older man in a brass-buttoned coat. At the stove with a steaming pot: the heavy man in the apron. A man in spectacles holds a lamp and is writing in a small book.

**Clumsy shroud C: Edmund Carr. "The Watch," the quarterdeck, 1 March 1812, night.**
Sound: wind, a lantern creaking on its hook, footsteps on frozen deck. The Tyneside voice: "Pell? You're not on this watch. Get below." A Hull voice, quiet, very close: "No. This is for George." A grunt. A body falls against the rail.
Frozen: stars, ice to the horizon. The stout man with mutton-chop whiskers sags against the binnacle, a knife in his side. Holding the knife: the man in the red knitted cap with the split lip. At the rail, turned and frozen in shock: a pockmarked man in a neckerchief. Climbing out of the forecastle hatch, mid-shout: a man with a ginger beard and a bonnet.

**Clumsy shroud D (the second clumsy one): George Pell.** The opening memory, "The Bread Room."

**The man in irons: Thomas Pell. "The Cable Tier," about 23 April 1812.**
Sound: wind; ice grinding against the hull, far off. A hoarse Hull voice, very weak: "Ned? That you? ... Ned." Silence. A long breath out.
Frozen: a dark, low space, the anchor cable coiled like a sleeping animal. The man in the red knitted cap with the split lip, chained by the ankle to a ringbolt, wrapped in a blanket, frost on his lips. Within reach: an empty water cask, a crust of bread wrapped in cloth, untouched. On the beam above him, nine scratched marks. On a nail by the ladder, out of reach: a key.

## The ship now (for searches)
- **Sail locker:** the four shrouds; the sailmaker's bench, his palm pad gone from its peg; the clumsy shrouds use the wrong needle (a cook's trussing needle lies by them).
- **Forecastle:** nine hammocks still slung, seven stripped of blankets. Sea chests: G.P. and T.P. side by side; H.R., M.Q. empty; J.H. full and untouched.
- **Officers' cabins:** the second mate's cabin bare, his sea boots and chest gone; the first mate's cabin untouched, a Newcastle almanac on the shelf.
- **Captain's cabin:** chart with the pencil line to Hailuoto; empty log shelf; a child's drawing of a cat pinned by the bunk, signed "Ned".
- **Galley and bread room:** the bread room lock is new and heavier than the door; the key ring is gone.
- **Deck:** the jolly boat's chocks empty; sledge runners' grooves in old ice at the side; the binnacle with a dark stain.
