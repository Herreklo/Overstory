# Round 3

**Turn 1. Fates written.**

You write in the book:
- Sketch 8 is George Pell: clubbed, by Edmund Carr.
- Sketch 2 is Edmund Carr: stabbed, by Thomas Pell.
- Sketch 4 is Isaac Dunning: crushed.

The book is still for a moment. Then the ink of all three settles darker into the page.

**Three fates are confirmed.**

**Turn 2. The last neat shroud, opened.**

The same small, even stitches as the carpenter's. Inside: a man with long pale hair and a ring in one ear. His clothes are stiff and puckered, as if they had dried on him. You open the watch.

**The Stove, 15 January 1812, night.** *The stove ticking as it cools and heats.*
A Swedish voice, urgent, half in Swedish: "Jens. Jens! Stay awake, you hear me? Håll dig vaken."
A Finnish voice, few words, flat: "The water had him to the chest. Too long."
An older Hull voice, tired: "More blankets. Mr Ekström, you'll try again at first light, you and Rautio both."
Breathing, slow and catching. Then it stops.

**The frozen moment.** The captain's cabin, lamplit, the stove door open and red. The long-haired man with the earring lies on blankets by the stove, soaked and blue, ice in his hair. Kneeling at his head: a young man with a fair beard in a short blue jacket, a pipe in his pocket. Standing by the door, pulling on a fur hat: the lean man with the leather palm pad. Holding out a blanket: an older man in a brass-buttoned coat. At the stove with a steaming pot: the heavy man in the apron. A man in spectacles holds a lamp and is writing in a small book.

**Turn 3. The captain's cabin, searched.**

The cabin you stood in a moment ago, months on. Cold now, and grey with the light from the stern windows.
- **The chart,** spread on the table and weighted at the corners: the Gulf of Bothnia, the coast at Uleåborg, and offshore the low island printed *Hailuoto*. A pencil cross out on the open water, and from it a pencil line straight across to the island's southern shore. No initials, no date, no note.
- **The stove:** cold, its grate packed with grey ash. An empty kettle on the hob.
- **The shelf** over the table, with a bar across its front to hold books in a seaway: empty.
- **The bunk:** a bare straw mattress. No blankets. Pinned to the panelling beside it, a child's drawing of a cat, in pencil, carefully shaded, signed "Ned".
- **By the door,** a row of hooks with nothing on them. In its bracket beside them, a brass speaking trumpet, green at the mouth.

---

## Now

**The captain's cabin, May 1812.** Gulls on the stern rail. Every shroud in the sail locker is open now; forward, the man in irons sits where he sat.

**The book**
- **Memories,** in order of date: The Pressure, 3 December 1811 · The Stove, 15 January 1812 · The Bread Room, 9 February 1812 · The Watch, 1 March 1812 · The Cable Tier, 23 April 1812.
- **Fates written:** Sketch 2, Edmund Carr: stabbed, by Thomas Pell · Sketch 4, Isaac Dunning: crushed · Sketch 8, George Pell: clubbed, by Edmund Carr.
- **Fates confirmed: 3 of 14.**
