# Round 1

## Pull
8. The shrouds hooked me before the watch opened: two hands sewed this ship's dead, and one of them stopped.

## Thinking aloud
The dead man is easy, perhaps too easy. Red cap fallen by his head, so he's 8 or 9. The man on the ladder has the split lip, so he's 9, and the dead one is 8. The voice from above says "George, leave it", so the thief is George Pell and the ladder is Thomas Pell, his brother (both Hull). "Mr Carr" plus Tyneside vowels plus mutton-chops and a blue coat makes the stout man Edmund Carr, sketch 2. Pencilled in my head, not spent yet: **Sketch 8 is George Pell: clubbed, by Edmund Carr.** I'll write it once I have three, because the book confirms in threes.

I also have 7 (Moss, apron and ladle), 13 (Ned and the cat) and 14 (Lind, spectacles and frock coat) present and alive. What I really want to know is everything around the kill. The sailmaker is sketch 6 (that leather pad is a sailmaker's palm). His neat stitches are on two shrouds and George's is clumsy. So by 9 February Rautio was dead, or gone, or someone else did the sewing. My guess is Thomas sewed his own brother badly. And where is the captain? A starving ship, and the mate hands out the bread and the justice. The chart line across the ice and the missing log both say someone walked off this ship.

One worry: the voices hand me names ("George", "Mr Carr"). If every scene does that, it's a lookup, not a deduction. The stitches are the kind of clue I came for.

## Prediction
The man in leg irons is Carr. After George, the crew (Thomas at its head) put the mate in irons, and he froze or starved there after the rest left over the ice for the island. That's why nobody sewed him up.

## Needs
- Is "look closely" one turn for the whole frozen scene, or one turn per person or thing?
- Can I tell in the frozen scene whether the locker was forced or opened with a key?
- Where are the captain, the sailmaker and the other mates on 9 February?

## Turns
1. Walk the frozen bread room closely. If this costs a turn per look, take them in this order: the locker George robbed (is the lock forced, or opened with a key, and how full are the other lockers?); the cook's keys (whose are they?); what the clerk holds or carries (he signed the sketch "O.L."; is there paper on him?); Carr's coat (are there brass buttons, or anything showing rank beyond mate?). Expect: the lock was forced and the lockers are nearly bare. The ship has been starving for weeks.
2. Search the cable tier: the man in leg irons. Look at his face, whiskers, coat and hands, whether the irons are locked, and where the key is. Expect: mutton-chops and a blue coat, so it's Carr, with no key in reach.
3. Hold the watch to the man in leg irons. Expect: a late scene. He's alone or nearly so, the boat is already gone, and he froze or starved. Voices will name who walked to the island and who stayed.
