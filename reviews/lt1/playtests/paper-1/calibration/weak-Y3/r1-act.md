# Round 1

## Pull
7 — the place is lovely and I want to see my room, but I'm already wondering what I'll actually *do* here.

## Thinking aloud
Honestly, I'm charmed. The mooring rings polished on top where the ropes work them, toast in the air, the rubbed-clear corner of the chandler's window at a child's nose height: that last one is the kind of detail I'd stop and look at. The opening screen reads like a postcard I'd want to live in, and it isn't a wall of numbers this time, which I'm glad of.

But two lines on the store page bother me a little. "No wrong answers" and "you can't miss anything." Part of me likes it, because no stress. The other part remembers my last game, where nothing ever went wrong and by the end the world just seemed to agree with me. Here they've promised that up front. So when Wilf gives me three lines, does it matter which I pick? It also makes the lantern walk odd. It says TONIGHT, but the game says nothing is missed. Which is it?

What I'm missing is something to make or grow. In Stardew I had a field and a coop to save for. Here I have "Belonging %", a sketchbook out of 48 and keepsakes out of 20. That sounds like a checklist dressed up as a cottage. I'll find out. For now I want people, a kind act, and my room with the blue window.

The grey cardigan postcard is the first thing that feels like a small job I could do. My guess is it belongs to someone up the steps.

## Prediction
Rob Garrow, who skippers the Puffin, is Wilf's son. Wilf will say his father built Marigold and she hasn't been in the water for years. There'll be a soft sadness between the old wooden boat and the son's motorboat. And the cardigan belongs to Dot or Ivy Hale.

## Needs
- What Belonging does and how it grows: kind acts? talking? just visiting places?
- Is the lantern walk only tonight, or will it come back? The screen says both.
- Does the day have a clock that matters (the ferry times say yes, the store page says no)?
- Why my character is here "for as long as the autumn lasts". Am I running from something, or resting?
- What a keepsake is and how it's different from a sketch.

## Turns
1. Answer Wilf with 2: "Do you need a hand with that?" — Expect: he won't let me varnish, but hands me something to hold or a rag, and tells me about Marigold and his father.
2. Walk into the Kettle and talk to June: say hello, ask for my key, and ask about the grey cardigan on the noticeboard. — Expect: she gives me the key, shows me the cardigan and says she's not sure whose it is, or that she thinks it's one of the Hale sisters'.
3. If I have the cardigan or a name, walk up Wren Steps to the geranium window and knock to give it back. If not, go to the quay's edge and ask Teddy what he's hoping to catch. — Expect: a warm thank-you from the sisters and an invitation in, or Teddy telling me his big plan.
4. Inspect the brass plaque on the bench by the water, and sit there a while if it's before the 10.30 ferry. — Expect: a name and dates, someone the town lost or loved, maybe connected to the Garrows.
