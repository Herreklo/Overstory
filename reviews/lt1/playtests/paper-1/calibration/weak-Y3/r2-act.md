## Thinking aloud
The writing is still lovely. But I'm starting to see the pattern I worried about. Everyone hands me a pretty line and goes back to what they were doing. The geranium in my room is cute, but I didn't earn it. I just turned up. At this rate I'll be at 100% by teatime and won't know why. What I want is a small job that's mine, and the cardigan is the only one on offer. June says a visitor's, but someone darned both elbows in a slightly different grey. A visitor doesn't darn a cardigan and then leave it. Someone here mends their things. And the Hales have watched that stair all their lives, so if anyone dropped it there, they saw.

## Prediction
May on the bench is June's mum, the woman squinting in the 1962 photo by the till. And the cardigan isn't a visitor's: Dot or Ivy will know whose it is the moment they see the darns.

- That sitting would eat the morning. I'd have liked to choose when to get up.
- Who May was.
