## Thinking aloud
The places tell me more than the people do. A boarded window, a brand-new red roof at step 60, a handrail worn bright: those make me wonder. The people are like vending machines. Each gives one pretty line, goes back to wiping or knitting, and offers the same three options next time. "Is my room ready?" is still on June's menu. Stardew villagers repeat themselves too, but there a gift lands, someone remembers it, and the field grows. Here my one kind act was locked to a hook. Belonging went to 37% and a cat turned up on my sill for asking around about something nobody cares about. The cat is sweet, but it isn't mine. I didn't put anything on that shelf. I'm dropping the cardigan. The game has told me three times it doesn't matter.
