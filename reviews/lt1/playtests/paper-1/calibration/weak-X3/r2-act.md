## Pull
7 — I want to know about the chandler's, May and the cardigan. But Belonging jumped 16% and a geranium appeared in a room I haven't seen, and I can't tell what earned it.

## Thinking aloud
The people are the best part. Wilf, June and the shut chandler's tell me this town has a history of people who stopped, were left, or stayed, and I'd like to pull on those threads. I also like that the journal wrote itself in my voice.

What worries me is the lack of a handle. The clock jumped from 8:10 to noon after four turns, and no one asked me to do anything, which is "no pressure" but also "no reason". Belonging is a number that went up. I didn't see a link between holding a rag and a geranium in my room. If I can't tell what raises it, I stop caring about it. I would like to see my room, since it's the thing that is supposed to grow.

The cardigan is the best loose thread: found on the Wren Steps, and two sisters watch that stair from a window. Taking it up the steps and asking is exactly the small kind act I like. I'll also ask June who May was, because the plaque and the chandler's feel connected.

## Prediction
Sal Brennan will not know the owner, or will know and be coy. She'll say the sisters at the window see every soul on the steps and are the people to ask. Dot and Ivy will say the cardigan is no one's they know, but will tell me a story about the steps instead.
