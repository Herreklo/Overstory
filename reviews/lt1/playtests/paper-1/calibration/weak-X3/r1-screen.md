# Round 1

**Turn 1.1.** You say: "Do you need a hand with that?"

Wilf doesn't answer at once. He holds out a rag, folded square and stiff with old varnish. "Catch that drip. There, at the edge. There. Good." You hold it where he shows you while the brush goes along the plank, slow, the wood darkening to honey under it.

"She'll see me out, this one."

He dips the brush and wipes it on the lip of the tin. "Forty-one years I took the Puffin over to the island. Rob has her now." A nod toward the blue boat at the steps, where Rob is still coiling his rope. "He doesn't talk to the passengers. I talked to every one."

Next time, Wilf:
1. "How's she coming along?"
2. "Can I help today?"
3. "Tell me about the Puffin."

**Turn 1.2.** The Kettle's door has a bell on a spring. Inside it is warm and smells of toast and wet coats: six tables with oilcloth, cakes under glass domes on the counter, the radio on the shelf reading the forecast for Lundy and Fastnet. By the till, a photograph: a woman outside this window in 1962, squinting into the sun beside the same table. June is wiping that table now.

You say: "Is my room ready?"

"All aired and ready, love." She dries her hands on her apron and fishes a key on a red wooden fob from her pocket. "Sit yourself down. Tea's on the house: you live here now." The cloth goes round the table again. "It was Mum's café, then mine. I've wiped this table every morning for thirty-one years."

You ask about the cardigan. She nods at the hook by the door, where it hangs, grey and hand-knitted. "Been there since August. Must be a visitor's."

Next time, June:
1. "Is my room ready?"
2. "What's good here?"
3. "What a lovely place."

**Turn 1.3.** You bend to the rubbed corner of Tolley & Son's window. Through the clear patch: empty shelves going back into the dark, a coil of rope still hanging on its hook, and on the far wall a calendar turned to March 1988. You try the door. It is locked.

**Turn 1.4.** You sit on the bench. The brass plaque is polished at the edges by sleeves: FOR MAY, WHO LIKED IT HERE. 1921–1990.

For a while you just watch. The Puffin's engine coughs and catches, and she goes out past the harbour wall with three passengers and a box of groceries, gulls lifting off the water behind her. Wilf's brush goes along the plank and back. Two gulls argue over a crust on the cobbles until a third takes it. The light moves on the water, grey, then silver, then grey.

**About noon. The Strand.** The cloud has thickened over the headland and the wind has dropped; the water has gone flat and pewter-coloured.

**In view**

- **The noticeboard**, by **the phone box**: the lantern walk tonight; the brass band; the found cardigan.
