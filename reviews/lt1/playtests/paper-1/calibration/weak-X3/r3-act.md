## Thinking aloud
At first I thought Saltlight was a gentle game with something under it. Now I'm less sure. The details of the town are good, and the paint layers on the Pennock house tell me someone's life changed there. But the cardigan, which looked like my first kind act, is a dead end: nobody knows, nobody can lend me the thing, and everyone gives me the same shrug. Belonging went to 34% without me doing anything I could point at. I haven't seen the cat; I've read about it. If the room is where the game shows me I'm becoming part of the town, I want to be in it.

What I'd like instead of the cardigan: a person with a real wish. Wilf talking to every passenger is closer. The jam jars are waiting at the chapel, so at least the walk needs nothing from June. I'll ask the sisters about the Pennocks, in my own words, since they've seen everyone go up and down for sixty years.
