# Round 2

**Turn 1. The square.** Ten minutes up from the river. Evelyn sits back on her heels when she sees you, a trowel in one gloved hand.

"Oh, good morning, dear. Aren't the tulips lovely this year? I put the bulbs in last autumn and worried all winter." She pats the soil round a red one. "George says I fuss. Well, somebody has to."

Lewis is by the noticeboard, reading it as if he hadn't pinned up half of it himself. "Morning, farmer! Fine day for it. I hear the rain's coming tomorrow, so make hay while the sun shines, eh?" He chuckles at his own joke and walks on towards his house.

You take Demetrius's card off the board. **Journal: "Wanted: one daffodil" (deliver to Demetrius, up the mountain; 2 days).**

At nine the bell over Pierre's door jingles. Pierre is behind the counter in his apron. "Parsnips! The sensible choice. Quick, cheap, and they never let you down." Eighteen packets, 360g. 9:10.

**Turn 2. Planting.** Twenty minutes back to the farm. You take a daffodil from the chest. The old parsnip and potato beds are still loose and tilled from the morning, so there is no hoeing to do: you press thirty parsnip seeds into the first rows and five strawberry seeds at the end nearest the house, where you'll see them from the porch. The packets say parsnips take 4 days; strawberries take 8, and fruit again every 4 after that.

A seed only grows overnight if its soil was watered that day, so you water them all. The can had 28 left from the morning; one more trip to the pond. When you stand up, every row on the farm is dark and wet, and the field is full again for the first time since you came. 10:45. **Energy 120/270.**

**Turn 3. The Community Center and the mountain.** The path to the mountain goes up past the Community Center, north of the square: a big tired building with ivy in the cracks and a boarded window. Inside, the bundle on the wall of the pantry takes the parsnip and the potato; the little slots glow as they go in. Something small and green peeks round the boiler-room door and is gone. **Spring Crops: 2 of 4.**

Ten more minutes up the mountain to Robin's, where the lake is flat and bright and someone is sawing in the yard. Demetrius comes out of his laboratory at the back in a white coat, takes the daffodil, and holds it up to the window.

"Ah. Narcissus pseudonarcissus. Good, it's fresh; the anthers are intact." He looks at you as if he'd forgotten you were there. "Thank you. That's... very helpful. Here." He counts out 90g. "For the study. You've saved me an afternoon of looking."

Robin, at the counter, laughs when you ask. "A coop? Sure. Bring me 4,000g, 300 wood and 100 stone, and I'll start the next morning. Three days and it's done. You'll have chicks by the end of the week if you're quick."

**Your prediction.** Evelyn was warm and talked about her tulips: right. Demetrius took the daffodil, paid 90g, and was dry and scientific about it: right. The cauliflowers and the rain come due tomorrow.
