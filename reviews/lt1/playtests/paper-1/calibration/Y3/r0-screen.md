# Round 0

A paper version of Stardew Valley.

**Thursday, Spring 18, Year 1. 6:00 am.** Sunny. 2,315g. Energy 270/270. Health 100/100.

**The TV.** Weather for tomorrow: rain. The fortune teller: the spirits are in good humour today (good luck). *Livin' Off the Land:* today is the last day for salmonberries; the bushes along the roads won't fruit again until next spring.

**The farm.** You've cleared about a third of the land.
- 15 parsnips: ready to harvest.
- 20 potatoes: ready to harvest.
- 24 cauliflowers: ready tomorrow. Need water today.
- 18 green beans on trellises: first pods in 2 days. Need water.
- 10 kale: ready in 2 days. Need water.

That is 52 tiles to water. Your basic watering can holds 40 and costs about 2 energy a tile; the pond is beside the house. A furnace stands by the porch.

**In your chest:** 5 strawberry seeds (from the Egg Festival), 12 parsnip seeds, 3 daffodils, 2 leeks, 4 wild horseradish, 1 quartz, 1 amethyst, 31 copper ore, 2 copper bars, 3 coal, 160 wood, 85 stone, 40 fibre.
**Tools:** basic hoe, axe, pickaxe and watering can; scythe, rusty sword, bamboo rod.
**Skills:** Farming 3, Foraging 3, Mining 2, Combat 2, Fishing 1. Deepest mine floor: 22.

**The mailbox (2 letters)**
- **Mayor Lewis:** the Flower Dance is on Spring 24 in the forest clearing, 9 am to 2 pm. Ask a partner before the day, and mind your feet. (No one will say yes below 4 hearts.)
- **Marnie:** she has chicks looking for a home as soon as you have a coop. Robin builds them for 4,000g, 300 wood and 100 stone.

**Calendar** (on Pierre's wall): today, **Pam's birthday**. Spring 20, Shane's birthday. Spring 24, the Flower Dance. Ten days of spring left.

**Notice board:** "Wanted: one daffodil, for a pollen study. Within two days. Pays 90g. Demetrius"

**Friendship** (hearts out of 10): Pam 2 (gift log: a leek, which she liked). Emily 2, Leah 2, Abigail 2, Haley 1, Sebastian 1, Shane 0. Gus 3, Lewis 3, Marnie 2, Robin 2. Each person accepts up to two gifts a week, one a day. A birthday gift counts eight times.

Pam lives in the trailer by the river, south of the square. She is usually home in the morning and at the Saloon from late afternoon.

**Community Center:** Spring Crops bundle (parsnip, green bean, cauliflower, potato), 0 of 4.

**In town**
- **Pierre's** (9 am to 5 pm): parsnip seeds 20g, potato 50g, kale 70g, green bean 60g, cauliflower 80g.
- **Clint's** (9 am to 4 pm): smelts and upgrades tools. Copper watering can: 2,000g and 5 copper bars; he keeps the can for two days.
- **The Saloon** (noon to midnight).

**What you can do** (each is a turn, and each costs time on the clock): harvest, water, hoe and plant, clear land, forage, fish, go down the mines, smelt and craft, shop, ship (the bin pays overnight), talk to someone, give a gift, take a request from the board, fill a bundle, go to bed. Sleep before midnight or you start tomorrow tired; at 2 am you collapse wherever you are.
