# Round 2

**Turn 1. Brasko.** You don't ask. You press the button under the counter, and the bell goes at the gate. The sergeant with the moustache comes himself, unhurried; the name tape on his coat says DANIK. Brasko doesn't argue and doesn't look at you. Danik lifts the canvas bag off his shoulder, takes him by the elbow, and walks him through the side door. The door shuts. The queue moves up a place.
11:25 (the detention cost 10 minutes). +5. Detention bonus from the guards: 5, paid with the day's wages.

**Turn 2. Dubova.** Passport against ID card. 1919.11.14. 1919.11.04. You stamp DENIED and push the papers back. She takes a while to get them into the string bag, between the onions. She looks at the stamp, then at the window above your head, where the clock is, and goes out the way she came, one hand on the rail.
12:40. +5.

**Turn 3. Liskov.** Yurko City, the numbers agree, the work pass has its seal and its end date. While you are checking the seal, the booth door opens behind you and the cold comes in. Danik leans in past your shoulder, his voice low, his eyes on the queue.

"Next but one. A woman with a press card. Find something wrong with her and detain her. Ten credits from me tonight. Nobody will ask."

He straightens up and goes back to the gate before you've turned round. APPROVED. Liskov takes his papers, says "Thanks," and goes through.
13:55. +5.

No slips from the printer.

**Your prediction** (the sergeant comes for Brasko; a slip comes out for the 5; Brasko goes quiet rather than pleading): it was the sergeant. No slip: the printer prints only citations, and the bonus is paid with the wages. Brasko said nothing.
