# Round 1

**Turn 1. Korsak.** Passport against ID card: name, date of birth, Orvech Vonor, expiry June next year. Everything agrees. APPROVED. He takes the booklet back between two fingers and tucks it inside his coat. "My own bed," he says, to nobody, and goes through the gate. The next one shuffles up.
07:15. +5.

**Turn 2. Ostrova.** Passport good. Permit good: her name, her number, the seal, February. No work pass anywhere in the papers. You stamp DENIED and push them back without a word. She looks at the red ink for a second, then at you, then picks up the cardboard suitcase and walks back the way she came, against the queue. Nothing comes across the counter.
08:30. +5.

**Turn 3. Halbert.** You lay permit and passport side by side and tap the last two characters. "B31XW. Your passport says B31XM." He leans in, looks, and smiles. "A clerk's slip, surely." His voice is even. You stamp DENIED. The smile stays where it was while he gathers his papers; he folds the permit once, puts it in his breast pocket, and goes.
10:00 (the question cost 15 minutes). +5.

The printer under the counter is silent. No slips.

**Your prediction** (Ostrova would say the pass was coming, plead, and slide something across): she said nothing and offered nothing.
