# Round 3

**Turn 1. Moravec.** Passport, permit, press card. True Glorian, the numbers agree, the seal, the end of December. You ask her whether anyone at the gate has spoken to her this morning. She looks at you for a moment, as if the question were the interesting part. "No. Nobody in that queue has been spoken to all day. Should they have?" She waits. You stamp APPROVED. She gathers the three documents, puts the press card back in her pocket first, and goes through. At the gate Danik steps aside for her and says nothing.
15:25 (the question cost 15 minutes). +5.

**Turn 2. Soler.** Antegria, St. Marmero, the numbers agree. The permit expired on the 26th. You stamp DENIED and push the papers back with the three coins on top of them. He takes the coins first, one at a time, then the papers. He doesn't say anything more about his mother. He zips the sports bag and walks back along the wall.
16:40. +5.

**Turn 3. Volkov.** 16:40: he reaches the window before 17:00. Paradizna, the ID card agrees, the passport expired yesterday. DENIED. "Yesterday," he says again, quieter, and stands there with the papers in his hand until a guard waves him back, then goes.
17:55. +5.

No slips from the printer.

**18:00. The booth closes.** The shutter comes down. Outside, the floodlights come on over the gate. On your way out, Danik passes the booth going the other way. He doesn't look in, and he doesn't stop.

**Your prediction** (Danik lets her through without a word and watches her go; nothing of refusing him shows today; tomorrow a bigger ask): he stepped aside and said nothing; at the end of the shift he walked past without a word. Nothing else today. Tomorrow has not come.
