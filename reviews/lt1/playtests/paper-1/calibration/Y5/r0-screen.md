# Round 0

A paper version of Papers, Please.

**Day 7. 29 November 1982. Grestin border checkpoint, Arstotzka. 06:00.**

You are the inspector in the booth. The queue is long and the wind is cold. Each entrant you process correctly pays you 5 credits. A mistake brings a citation: today's first two are warnings; after that, each costs 5 credits. The booth closes at 18:00. On paper, each entrant takes about 75 minutes of the clock; each question you ask adds 15, each detention 10.

**Family, as of last night.** Savings: 14 credits. Wife: well. Son: sick. Mother-in-law: cold. Uncle: hungry.
Tonight's bills: rent 20 (must be paid), food 10, heat 10, medicine 15.

**Bulletin, Ministry of Admission**
- New today: workers (entry permit purpose WORK) must also carry a **work pass** sealed by the Ministry of Labour.
- The guards pay 5 credits for each entrant detained for forged or stolen documents.

**Rulebook**
- Every entrant needs a valid passport: not expired, and issued by a city of their country.
- Arstotzkan citizens need an ID card that matches their passport.
- Foreigners need an entry permit: name and passport number matching the passport, not expired, with the Ministry seal.
- Deny anyone whose documents are missing, invalid, expired or mismatched. Detain for forgery.
- Issuing cities. Arstotzka: Orvech Vonor, East Grestin, Paradizna. Kolechia: Yurko City, Vedor, West Grestin. Antegria: St. Marmero, Glorian, Outer Grouse. Republia: True Glorian, Lesrenadi, Bostan. Obristan: Skal, Lorndaz, Mergerous. United Federation: Great Rapid, Shingleton, Korista City.

**What you can do** (one entrant is one turn): read and compare the documents; question the entrant about anything that doesn't fit; stamp approved or denied; detain; take or refuse anything slipped across the counter. At the end of the day you choose which bills to pay.
