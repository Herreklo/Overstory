# Round 2

**Turn 2.1. Brasko.** You don't ask. You press the detain button with his papers still square on the counter. He doesn't argue and he doesn't look at you; he looks at the side door until it opens. The guard takes the holdall as well as the man. The printer is silent. **+5**, and **+5 from the guards.** 11:50.

**Turn 2.2. Dubova.** You put the ID card beside the passport and point at the two dates, 11.04 and 11.14. She looks at them for a long time, the string bag hooked over her wrist. "A clerk wrote it wrong in 1961. I have lived in Grestin forty years. My flat is two streets from here." She has no other paper. You stamp DENIED. She reaches up for the passport, puts it in the string bag with the onions and goes out past the wire, slowly, against the queue. The printer is silent. **+5.** 13:20.

**Turn 2.3. Liskov.** Passport, permit and work pass agree, and the dates are in order. You stamp APPROVED.

While you are still turning the work pass over, the guard on the door leans into the booth, one elbow on the shutter. The tab on his coat says DANIK. He keeps his voice under the noise of the heater. "The next one. A woman with a press card. Find something wrong with her and detain her. Ten credits from me tonight. Nobody will ask." He straightens up before you can answer and goes back to the door.

Liskov folds the work pass into his coat and goes through. The printer is silent. **+5.** 14:35.

**Today so far:** wages 30, guards 10, citations none (two warnings still in hand). **Savings at home:** 14. **Clock:** 14:35.

**Day 7. 29 November 1982. Grestin border checkpoint. 14:35.**

The light over the wall is already going yellow. The queue is shorter now, perhaps forty people, and quieter. Sergeant Danik stands at the side door with his hands behind his back, looking at the queue, not at you.

**Bills tonight:** rent 20 (must be paid), food 10, heat 10, medicine 15. **Family, as of last night:** wife well, son sick, mother-in-law cold, uncle hungry.

**The shutter comes down at 18:00.** Nobody is called to the window who can't be finished by then.

**Rulebook** (unchanged)
- Every entrant needs a valid passport: not expired, and issued by a city of their country.
- Arstotzkan citizens need an ID card that matches their passport.
- Foreigners need an entry permit: name and passport number matching the passport, not expired, with the Ministry seal.
- Deny anyone whose documents are missing, invalid, expired or mismatched. Detain for forgery.
- Issuing cities. Arstotzka: Orvech Vonor, East Grestin, Paradizna. Kolechia: Yurko City, Vedor, West Grestin. Antegria: St. Marmero, Glorian, Outer Grouse. Republia: True Glorian, Lesrenadi, Bostan. Obristan: Skal, Lorndaz, Mergerous. United Federation: Great Rapid, Shingleton, Korista City.
