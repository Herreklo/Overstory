# Round 1

**Turn 1.1. Korsak.** Passport and ID card agree: name, birth date 1941.03.14, Orvech Vonor, valid to 1983.06.02. You stamp APPROVED. He takes the passport back without looking at it. "My own bed," he says, and goes through the gate. The printer is silent. **+5.** 07:15.

**Turn 1.2. Ostrova.** You ask for her work pass. She looks at the permit in your hand, then at you. "Nobody told me. The works said the permit was enough." Nothing else comes out of her coat. You stamp DENIED. She puts the passport inside her coat, picks up the cardboard suitcase and walks back the way she came. The printer is silent. **+5.** 08:45.

**Turn 1.3. Halbert.** You turn the permit round and lay it on the counter beside his passport, the two numbers side by side: B31XW, B31XM. He reads them upside down and smiles. "A clerk's slip, surely." He has nothing else to show you. You press the detain button. A guard comes through the side door and takes his elbow; Halbert lets himself be led out, the good wool coat still buttoned. The printer is silent. **+5**, and **+5 from the guards.** 10:25.

Nothing was slid across the counter this morning.

**Today so far:** wages 15, guards 5, citations none (two warnings still in hand). **Savings at home:** 14. **Clock:** 10:25.

**Day 7. 29 November 1982. Grestin border checkpoint. 10:25.**

The wind has dropped and the queue has not. Behind the wire it runs back past the tank traps, sixty or seventy people, bags at their feet, a few smoking. The heater under the counter ticks. The bulletin is still pinned up beside the shutter: workers need a work pass sealed by the Ministry of Labour; the guards pay 5 for each detention for forged or stolen papers.

**Bills tonight:** rent 20 (must be paid), food 10, heat 10, medicine 15. **Family, as of last night:** wife well, son sick, mother-in-law cold, uncle hungry.

**Rulebook** (unchanged)
- Every entrant needs a valid passport: not expired, and issued by a city of their country.
- Arstotzkan citizens need an ID card that matches their passport.
- Foreigners need an entry permit: name and passport number matching the passport, not expired, with the Ministry seal.
- Deny anyone whose documents are missing, invalid, expired or mismatched. Detain for forgery.
- Issuing cities. Arstotzka: Orvech Vonor, East Grestin, Paradizna. Kolechia: Yurko City, Vedor, West Grestin. Antegria: St. Marmero, Glorian, Outer Grouse. Republia: True Glorian, Lesrenadi, Bostan. Obristan: Skal, Lorndaz, Mergerous. United Federation: Great Rapid, Shingleton, Korista City.
