## Thinking aloud
There are 205 minutes left, which is two entrants and 55 spare. Volkov won't be called. Moravec is clean all the way down. Danik wants me to invent a fault in a journalist who has just asked whether guards are paid per arrest. Detaining her straight after that question would prove her story. His 10 is only a promise for "tonight", and the stamp and the citation would both be mine. No. The other option is to answer her question without saying a word, and I want to see whether the game lets me do that and what it costs. Soler's permit expired on the 26th, and there are 15 credits under his passport. The two warnings are free today and, I assume, gone tomorrow, so the cold play is to spend one here: take the money and let a man see his mother. Compassion and profit point the same way, which makes it easy. That's a weakness in the design. The colder move is to pocket the 15 and deny him anyway: +20 and no warning. If the game lets that go with no cost, the economy is broken. I won't do it, but I'm naming it.

## Prediction
Danik takes it badly. Tomorrow I either get a worse post or a guard who stops paying for detentions. If I point Moravec at the bulletin, her article runs, and within a day or two the Ministry quietly drops the per-detention bounty, which costs me a source of income I've been relying on.
