# Round 3

**Turn 3.1. Moravec.** Passport, permit, dates and seal all agree. You stamp APPROVED. As you slide the papers back you tap the bulletin beside the shutter. It hangs on your side of the glass, facing you; from where she stands it is the back of a sheet of paper and your finger on it. She looks at the finger, then at you, waits a moment for an answer that doesn't come, closes the notebook and goes through. At the side door Sergeant Danik watches her go and says nothing. The printer is silent. **+5.** 15:50.
(The booth has no way to answer an entrant: you can question, stamp, detain, take or refuse.)

**Turn 3.2. Soler.** You take the 15 credits and stamp APPROVED. He is through the gate before the ink is dry, the hospital letter still in his hand. The printer chatters:

> CITATION: Protocol violated. Entry permit: expired.
> Warning 1 of 2. No penalty.

No wage for this entrant. **+15**, from the counter. 17:05.

**Turn 3.3. Volkov.** Not called. At 17:05 there is no time to finish anyone before 18:00. The man in the fur hat is at the front of the queue when the shutter comes down. Through the slats you hear him say something to the guard, loudly, and the guard say something back.

**18:00. End of shift.** The lights in the booth go off one by one. Sergeant Danik walks past the booth on his way out, close enough to touch the glass, and doesn't look in or say a word.
