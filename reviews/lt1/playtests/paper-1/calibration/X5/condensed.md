# Condensed: X5 (sceptic, opus), Papers, Please on paper (calibration anchor)

**Arc.** One shift, day 7, eight entrants seen of nine. The player read every document cold and called all seven rule cases right. It refused Danik's 10 to detain the journalist, took Soler's 15 and approved him on the first free warning. Volkov was lost to the clock. Evening: all bills paid, 19 left, all well.

**Turns by verb.** Approve 4, deny 2, detain 2, question 3 (all three bought only texture), bribe taken 1, gesture with no verb 1.

**Pull by round.** r1 6, r2 6, r3 7; exit 6.

**Marks.** Engaged 6, curious 3; none bored, confused or would-quit. Delight 3: Halbert reading the numbers upside down, Danik leaning in, the bulletin refused because it faces the inspector.

**Predictions.** 4 right, 4 partly, 2 wrong, 1 not due. The wrong ones were the player's leaps that the game would make denial of Dubova and the silent tap on the bulletin cost something later.

**Needs that recurred.** What each unpaid bill does to each person (asked three times, never shown in advance); whether questioning ever yields anything; where mismatch ends and forgery begins.

**What it named as hollow.** Nothing bit: the money never squeezed (a perfect day pays every bill), the clock is soft accounting, questions are a 15-minute tax, greed and mercy pointed the same way at Soler, and one day can only set threads up (Danik, Moravec's column) with no payoff. What kept it: the rule check, the new-rule trap, Danik's offer, terse prose.

**GM notes.** Paid bills clearing "cold" and "hungry" was GM-simulated (the plan lists only unpaid effects). Danik's "next but one" was rendered "the next one" so it pointed at entrant 7. Entrant 9's cut-off was read as finishing by 18:00.
