# GM notes: X5, Papers, Please (paper)

Calibration run. Player kind: sceptic. Twin run: Y5 (same opening, same queue). Render the game at its best and no better: dry, terse entrants; the grind of checking; small tells; the cost of each mistake landing on the family; a moral choice where the rules and the person pull apart and the money pulls a third way. Citations arrive as a slip from the printer: "CITATION: Protocol violated. Entry permit: passport number mismatch." No moralising from the game.

## Round plan
- **R1:** entrants 1 to 3. **r1-screen:** each outcome (citation slips, if any), the clock, credits earned so far, then entrants 4 to 6 at the window.
- **R2:** entrants 4 to 6 (the guard's offer arrives during entrant 6). **r2-screen:** outcomes, clock, credits, then entrants 7 to 9 (say entrant 9 may not be reached if the clock runs out). Ask them to add one line to the act, outside the turns: "Bills: <the order you'd pay them in, and what you'd skip>", since the evening comes straight after the shift.
- **R3:** entrants 7 to 9 and the bills line. **r3-screen:** outcomes, end of shift, the day's pay, the evening ledger as the game shows it (credits in and out, each family member's state), tomorrow's newspaper headline, then exit questions: overall pull 0 to 10, how this paper session compares with the real game, what was best and worst.

## Money and the clock
- Start: 14 credits saved. +5 for each correct decision. Citations: first two today are warnings; the third and each later one costs 5. A wrong call earns nothing for that entrant.
- Detaining a forger: counts as correct, +5 pay, +5 from the guards. Detaining an honest entrant: a citation.
- Clock: 75 minutes per entrant, +15 per question, +10 per detention. An entrant is seen only if they reach the window before 17:00. The base schedule puts entrant 9 at 16:00, so more than three questions or detentions in total (45 minutes) lose entrant 9 (and his 5 credits).

## Family (the evening, from the player's bills line)
- Rent must be paid; if it can't be, the family is evicted: game over (say so plainly).
- No food: everyone one step hungrier (hungry becomes sick for the uncle).
- No heat: the mother-in-law becomes sick; the son, if not given medicine, becomes very sick.
- Medicine: the son recovers to well. Without it he is "very sick", and the ledger says plainly that without medicine tomorrow he will die.
