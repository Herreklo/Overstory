## Thinking aloud
Lagavulin is on 59 with no Block, no Metallicize and no Vulnerable. I have 3 energy and Strength 2. The best line is Bash+ first (12, puts 3 Vulnerable on him), then Pommel Strike (11 x 1.5 = 16, draws one). That's 28, leaving him on 31, and uses all my energy. Pommel first would be worse: 11 with no Vulnerable behind it, 23 total. If Pommel draws an Anger, it's free and worth 12 more.

Next turn he has Vulnerable 2, and the draw pile (Strike x5, Defend x2, Anger x2, minus whatever Pommel takes) means any 5-card hand has at least 3 attacks. Three attacks at 12 each is 36, more than the 31 he has left. So he dies on turn 4 whatever I draw, and the Anger case makes it easier. Blocking this turn instead (Bash+ and Shrug It Off) drops what I take from 18 to 10, but leaves him on 47, and then I'd need four attacks next turn, which isn't guaranteed. If that failed, I'd take another 18 and he might siphon my Strength. So I take the 18 and end at 26, then Burning Blood and the Rest on floor 10.
