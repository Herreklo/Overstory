# Round 1

**Turn 1.1. Talk: Wilf Garrow.**
You say: "I am. That's a lovely boat."
Wilf runs his eye along her as if he hadn't looked in a while. "She is that." He dips the brush, draws it the length of the third plank up from the keel, and the wood goes from dull to honey. "She'll see me out, this one."
He sits back on his heels. "Forty-one years I took the Puffin over to the island. Rob has her now. He doesn't talk to the passengers. I talked to every one."
Next time, Wilf:
1. "How's she coming along?"
2. "Can I help today?"
3. "Tell me about the Puffin."

**Turn 1.2. Inspect: the bench's brass plaque.**
A wooden bench, silvered by weather, facing the water. The plaque is screwed to the backrest, polished at its edges by shoulders: FOR MAY, WHO LIKED IT HERE. 1921–1990.

**Turn 1.3. Inspect: the rubbed corner of Tolley & Son's window.**
You bend to the clear patch, about the size of a hand, smeared at its edges where a sleeve has worked it. Inside, in brown half-light: empty shelves running back into the dark, a coil of rope still on its hook, and on the far wall a calendar showing March 1988. The door is locked. Your breath fogs the glass, and the shop goes away.
*Tolley & Son's window can now be sketched.*

**Turn 1.4. Talk: Teddy Marsh.**
You say: "Have you ever been inside Tolley's?"
Teddy doesn't look round from the water. "I rubbed it. There's nothing in there. Just rope." He nods at the red bucket, where one crab sits in an inch of seawater, folded up like a purse. "Just Admiral. He always comes back."
The line twitches; he lets it. "I catch him every morning and let him go at teatime. He doesn't mind."
Next time, Teddy:
1. "Caught anything?"
2. "What's the bait?"
3. "Can I have a go?"

**About noon.** The cloud has thickened over the headland and come down until the top of the hill is only grey. The light on the water goes flat and pewter. The Puffin's engine coughs and settles to a mutter at the steps. Someone in one of the houses above has put a lamp on. The smell of toast has turned into the smell of soup.
