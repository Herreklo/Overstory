# Round 2

**Turn 2.1: salmonberries.** You take the long way up from the river, along the hedge by the bridge where the bushes grow thick. The berries come off at a touch, warm already, and stain your fingers pink; a robin watches from the top of the hedge and doesn't move. Twelve in your pocket. You're at 180 energy, so you keep them all. **9:05 am.** Pierre's shutters are up.

**Turn 2.2: Pierre's.** The bell over the door jangles. Pierre is behind the counter with his apron on and a pencil behind his ear, Caroline restocking jars at the back.

"Morning! Parsnips again? You can't go wrong with a parsnip. Eighteen? Coming right up." He counts the packets out onto the counter two at a time. "Shame about spring running out on us. Ten days left."

18 parsnip seeds, **360g**. **9:15 am.**

**Turn 2.3: the request and the bundle.** At the board outside you pull Demetrius's note off its pin; it goes into your journal: *Deliver one daffodil to Demetrius. 2 days. Reward 90g.*

Then up the square to the Community Center. The door sticks and gives. Inside it is as it always is: dust in the light from the broken window, boards gone soft in the corners, a smell of old wood and mice, cobwebs in the rafters. In the pantry the golden scroll still hangs on the wall, its writing in a language you can't read but somehow understand. You open the Spring Crops bundle and set the parsnip in its slot, then the potato. Each lands with a soft chime and a puff of something like pollen. Something small and green scurries behind the old fireplace when you turn; when you look, there's nothing there.

**Spring Crops: 2 of 4.** Still missing: green bean, cauliflower. The bundle's ribbon shows the reward: **20 Speed-Gro.** **9:30 am.**

**Your prediction** (bundle takes the parsnip and potato, 2 of 4, a dusty room, a promise once it's done): the bundle is at 2 of 4, the room is dusty, and the reward is 20 Speed-Gro.
