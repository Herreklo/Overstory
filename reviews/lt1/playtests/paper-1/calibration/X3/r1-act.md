# Round 1

## Pull
7 — Easy, familiar morning with a nice list of small kind things to do. A few deadlines pile up at once, though, so I can't tell yet if this will feel calm or like homework.

## Thinking aloud
Okay, a sunny morning, the farm is humming, and a lot of it is ready. First thing is always the watering: 52 tiles, my can holds 40, the pond is beside the house, so two trips and around 100 energy out of 270. That leaves plenty. Rain tomorrow means I get a free day after this, which is cozy: I could take it slow.

What I actually care about today is the people. It's Pam's birthday and I've got two leeks, and she already liked one. That is the kind act of the day, and a birthday counts eight times, so I want to do it in the morning when she's home. Demetrius wants a daffodil and I have three, so giving him one beats shipping it, and the 90g is a bonus. The last of the salmonberries are a small pang. I'd forage a few on the way if the road passes any, just because "last day" makes me not want to miss it.

The to-do list is dense, and some of it is on a clock (daffodil in two days, Pam today, berries today), which edges toward pressure. I like the two crop bundles that fit right in: a parsnip and a potato are ready, so the Spring Crops bundle is 2 of 4 the moment I'm in town. The Flower Dance mail leaves me a bit flat: nobody says yes below 4 hearts and my best is 2, with six days left. I would like to go, but it reads as a gate I can't pass in time, so I'm not going to chase it yet.

Money: 2,315g. The coop (4,000g) is the dream, because chicks for Marnie sound lovely. The copper can is 2,000g. I'd rather think about that after I see what the harvest brings.

## Prediction
Pam will be at the trailer this morning. She'll be pleased with the leek, and because it's her birthday I expect her to go from 2 hearts to around 3, maybe a bit more. She might say something a little gruff but warm.
