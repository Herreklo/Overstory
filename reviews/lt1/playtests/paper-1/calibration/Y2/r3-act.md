## Thinking aloud
Tobin did not hit Pell; Pell slipped on the step while they levered the gate with the miller's bar. Shadowheart's read of the wound backs the story, so I believe him. But under the Ford's law "a hand raised against the mill hangs", breaking the sluice is still what hangs him, and Pell too if he wakes and talks. Two traps: if I tell Crane, I hang a kid and maybe Pell; if Pell wakes unprepared and says "I fell at the weir," he puts himself and Tobin in the noose with his own mouth. So I do not rouse Pell yet. Shadowheart can keep him warm and hush him if he stirs.

The lever isn't mercy but law: Hedda said the moot never gave Vask leave for the higher weir. If that is true, the gate was broken on a work built against the Ford's own rules, and Crane has been looking to Vask because he is in his pocket. I need proof of that, not guesses. The locked back room is the best bet, and Astarion is the right tool, but Vask is probably still in it, so I'll draw him out by walking in and talking while Astarion works. I'll keep Tobin's secret from the party for now; I'd rather decide what to do with it than have Karlach decide for me.
