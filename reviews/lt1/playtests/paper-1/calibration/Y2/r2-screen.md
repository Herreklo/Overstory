# Round 2

## What happened

**1. Shadowheart goes to the shrine**
One room, whitewashed, smelling of tallow and boiled linen. Pell lies on a cot under the window: sixteen, thin, a bandage round his head gone brown on the left side. His breathing is slow and even. **Sister Maud**, small and grey, sleeves rolled, lets Shadowheart in without a word and lifts the bandage for her.
"It's the shape of a step," Maud says, "not a cudgel. I've seen both."
`[MEDICINE] Shadowheart: d20 7 + 5 = 12 vs DC 10. Success.`
Shadowheart looks a long while. "She's right. One straight edge, flat, and the bruising runs along it. A club leaves a round mark; a bar leaves a line with swelling either side. This is a man going down hard on cut stone."
Will he wake? Maud shrugs. "Tonight, or in a week, or not at all. Heads keep their own time." She tucks the bandage back. "Vask paid for his keep here, I'll say that for the man. Paid before I asked."
Shadowheart, quietly, when she rejoins you: "I could try to bring him round. Healing Word and careful hands. It might give us a minute of him. It might not be a clear minute."

**2. Astarion goes to the sluice gate**
He's gone from your side before you've finished saying it, and nobody comes out of the mill to see him go. He crouches on the weir among the reeds for a while.
`[INVESTIGATION] Astarion: d20 7 + 1 = 8 vs DC 10. Failure.`
He comes back wiping his boots on the grass. "The gate is broken. Water is going through it. Half the village has been tramping about on that bank since dawn, and peasants, it turns out, have enormous feet. I learned nothing, and I've ruined a perfectly good pair of boots." A pause. "The miller, though, went straight into a little room at the back of the mill and shut himself in. It has a very handsome lock. I'm only mentioning it."

**3. You speak to Tobin through the back of the barn**
Nobody follows you round. Gus stays at the door; a couple of farmers on the wall watch you go and say nothing. The hay is stacked to shoulder height against the back boards; above it there's a gap you can speak through, and Tobin, at the post, can hear you if you keep it low.
You (reply 6): "You heard. You've got until Pell wakes. Who are you protecting?"
"Nobody." Too quick. Then: "Is he going to wake? Maud said he might not—" He shuts his mouth.
You (reply 7): "Tell me what happened at the weir, and I'll make it count."
`[PERSUASION] Tav: d20 10 + 7 = 17 vs DC 15. Success.`
A long breath. When it comes, it comes all at once, in a whisper.
"It was me and Pell. The weir's killing the farms, you've seen the barley. Pell thinks so too, even though Vask feeds him. We went up last night to open the sluice, just open it, let the water down. Pell knew where Vask keeps the iron bar. He got it under the gate from the pond side and leaned on it, and the gate went all at once, burst, and he slipped on the green stones and went down on the step. I heard it." He swallows. "I got him up on the bank. He was breathing. I ran for Maud and Crane had me on the green road before I got there. I told him someone was hurt at the weir. That's all I told him.
"If you tell Crane Pell was there, Vask throws him out. He's bound to the mill. He's got no family, nowhere to go. Don't. Please. I'd sooner they did it to me."

No companion was near enough to hear.

**Your prediction.** Astarion couldn't tell from which side or with what the gate was broken. By Tobin's account it was levered from the pond side, the mill's side, with the mill's own iron bar, by Pell.
