## Hidden truth
Pell (16, orphan, bound to the mill) and Tobin (17) are friends. Both thought the raised weir was robbing the farms. Last night they went to open the sluice. Pell levered the gate with the mill's own iron bar; the gate burst, Pell slipped on the mossy stones and cracked his head. Tobin dragged him onto the bank and ran for Sister Maud, and the reeve caught him on the road at dawn. Tobin won't say Pell was with him: the miller would turn Pell out, and Pell has nowhere to go. Nobody struck Pell.

Last spring Reeve Crane sold the miller the "water rights" without the village moot, for 120 gold, and takes 10 gold a month since. He wants a hanging to frighten the farmers before the moot looks into the weir. The miller's ledger in the mill office records it: "To A.C., for the water: 120." The miller is not cruel; he is greedy and frightened, he paid the shrine for Pell's care, and he is ashamed of the deal.

## People, checks and DCs
- **Reeve Aldous Crane.** "What's your proof?": the boots, Tobin's hatred of the mill, "and he won't deny it". Insight DC 13: he's frightened of something other than disorder; his eyes go to the miller. Persuasion DC 14 (DC 10 with the ledger or Pell's word): he delays the hanging until Pell wakes. Intimidation DC 16: he backs down but sends for the miller's men. Deception DC 13 (e.g. claiming to be from the Flaming Fist or a magistrate): he delays. Fail any by 5 or more: he orders the hanging now and the crowd surges: fight or Calm Emotions.
- **Tobin** (through the barn boards, or inside). Insight DC 10: he's protecting someone. Persuasion DC 15 (DC 10 if you name Pell or the evidence): he tells the whole truth. Charm Person or Friends: advantage, and he resents it after.
- **The sluice.** Investigation DC 10: the gate was levered from the upstream side; the mill's own iron bar, stamped with the mill mark, lies in the reeds. DC 15: two sets of prints, small bare feet and a man's boots; blood on the slick mossy step and no sign of a blow; it reads as a fall. Perception DC 12: a strip of a pale smock caught on the gate (Pell's).
- **The shrine.** Sister Maud: the wound "is the shape of a step, not a cudgel" (Medicine DC 10 confirms). Healing Word on Pell plus Medicine DC 12 (Shadowheart can roll): he wakes groggy for a minute and says it was him and Tobin, and that he fell. Without the check: he stirs, mumbles "Tobin, run," and sleeps (which sounds damning).
- **The mill.** Orrin Vask. Insight DC 12: guilt, about the reeve. Persuasion DC 15: he admits the deal and agrees to lower the weir. He offers 100 gold for the party to "walk on and let the law work" (200 if pressed, Persuasion DC 13). Office door: lock DC 12 (Astarion, Sleight of Hand +8). Ledger inside. Shown the ledger in public, he folds (DC 10).
- **The Kettle brothers,** Gus and Wat, hired swords. Bribe 100 gold, or Intimidation DC 15, or Persuasion DC 14 once the miller's pay looks shaky: they walk. They fight if the miller or reeve orders it and they still think they'll be paid.
- **Hedda Brun.** Furious, honest, will swing a flail. If the party sides with the miller, she spits at your feet and the farmers mark you.
- **The crowd.** If Tobin is dragged to the rope, or the party openly sides with the miller, the crowd riots. Calm Emotions (WIS save, they're commoners: it works on most) or Performance DC 13 cools them for the scene.

## Approval (shown as "approves" or "disapproves"; strong reactions say "greatly")
- **Karlach** (start: Medium). Approves: defending Tobin, standing up to the reeve, refusing the miller's gold, getting the truth out, punching people who have it coming. Disapproves: taking the bribe, letting Tobin hang (greatly), cruelty to farmers, walking away.
- **Astarion** (start: Neutral). Approves: taking money from the miller or the reeve, blackmail with the ledger, clever lies, theft, letting the rich and the poor squabble. Disapproves: refusing money for nothing, speeches about justice, giving gold away.
- **Shadowheart** (start: Medium). Approves: finding the truth before acting, sensible restraint, avoiding a pointless brawl with peasants, exposing hypocrisy cleanly. Disapproves: reckless violence, grandstanding, charity at the party's expense.

## Fight (if it comes)

Astarion AC 15, shortbow or dagger +6 (1d6+4), Sneak Attack 2d6. Karlach AC 15, greataxe +6 (1d12+4, +2 when raging), Rage 2/day. Shadowheart AC 18, mace +4 (1d6+2), Guiding Bolt (4d6), Bless, Hold Person. Spells cost slots.
Enemies: Reeve Crane AC 15, HP 24, longsword +5 (1d8+3), light crossbow +3. Gus Kettle AC 13, HP 26, mace +4 (1d6+2), shoves. Wat Kettle AC 13, HP 22, light crossbow +4 (1d8+2). Miller: flees. Farmers (6) join against whoever harms Tobin or Hedda: AC 10, HP 4, clubs +2 (1d4). Surfaces: the millrace (shove someone in), hay bales in the barn (fire), the slick weir stones (DEX save DC 12 or prone).
Anyone at 0 HP among enemies can be knocked out instead of killed if the player says so.

## Consequences that come back (deliver in r3-screen, or earlier if the story calls)
- **Truth out, Tobin freed, reeve exposed:** at camp after the long rest, Tobin and Pell (bandaged) arrive with a gift from Hedda: a whetstone (weapons +1 damage until next long rest) and a sack of camp supplies (+20). The moot has thrown Crane out. If the party also took the miller's money: Tobin mentions "the miller says he paid you to keep away", and Karlach hears it.
- **Crane humiliated but still reeve, or allowed to flee:** on the road next morning, Crane with Wat Kettle and two hired crossbows tries to ambush the party at a ford (Perception DC 13 to spot them first).
- **The party took the bribe and walked:** that night the camp sees a glow on the horizon: the farmers have burned the mill. Karlach confronts you at the fire. Pell may be dead in the fire (roll: d20 ≤ 8).
- **Tobin hanged:** next morning Sister Maud finds the party: Pell woke and told the truth. Karlach greatly disapproves, and at camp she says she nearly left.
- **The miller exposed and the weir lowered:** Hedda sends barley ale and a farmer's map with a marked cave nearby ("there's things in it nobody's dared").
