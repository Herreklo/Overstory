## Thinking aloud
Everyone is now pointing at Vask: the reeve's eyes, Hedda's "who gave him leave", and Vask walking off silent. That is too tidy for me, and in an RPG the person the whole screen points at is often a decoy. Tobin is the real thing here. He looked at his mother and said nothing, so he is protecting someone, maybe her, maybe a friend, and he will not tell me with Hedda and Gus listening. The barn has hay bales along the back wall, so I can get to the boards on the far side, away from the door and the cudgel man. I want to split the party: Shadowheart to the shrine for Pell (her Medicine is +5, and I want to know if the wound fits a stone or a swung tool), Astarion to the broken gate (how it was smashed, from which side, with what), and me with Tobin. Karlach stays by the barn and glares at Gus, which suits her. The clock says walking is free, but I do not trust that Pell wakes on a polite schedule, so I will not dawdle.
