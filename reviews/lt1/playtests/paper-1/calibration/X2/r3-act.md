## Thinking aloud
My persuasion is coming back to bite me. I tied the rope to whatever Pell says, Pell has now said "Tobin, run", and Maud will repeat it if she's asked. Something else is bothering me too. Crane said the gate was *smashed*, and Tobin says he *opened* it. If those are different things, someone else was at the gate after Tobin. The mill light is on and Vask went up there, and the sluice is just past the mill, so whatever is up there may not stay the way it is tonight. It's the last light, and the gate is the one place nobody has looked. Honest gripes: my bard, the talker, is stuck in a sickroom with nothing to say. Three turns for four people, with walking costing a turn, makes the party feel like one person. Tav also has no story of their own yet. So far I'm a skilled stranger in someone else's quarrel.
