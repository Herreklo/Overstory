# Round 2

**Hob's Ford. The sun is touching the hills; the stream has gone the colour of brass.**

## Turn 2.1: Wake Pell

"Shut the door, Sister, and stay." Maud slides the bolt and stands with her back to it, the wet cloth still in her hand.

You kneel by the cot, touch Pell's temple and speak the word. *Healing Word.* A warmth goes out of your fingers. Shadowheart takes his wrist and his jaw, ready to hold him steady as he surfaces.

**[MEDICINE] Shadowheart: d20 3 + 5 = 8 vs DC 12. Failure.**

He comes up too fast. His eyes open and don't find you; they slide past your face to the ceiling. His hand closes on your sleeve, hard. "Tobin," he says, thick and urgent. "Tobin, *run*." Then his grip goes slack and he is under again, breathing slow and deep.

"You're safe," you tell him anyway. He doesn't hear it.

Shadowheart sits back on her heels. "That could be a boy warning his friend. Or a boy naming him. Crane will only ever hear one of those." She lays his hand on the blanket. "And no, before you ask: not again tonight. You'd be dragging him up through a cracked skull. He needs sleep, not magic."

Maud hasn't moved from the door. "I heard that, same as you." She looks at the bolt, then at you. "I'll not run to the reeve with it. But if Aldous Crane stands in my door and asks me what the boy said, I'll not lie to him." She comes back to the basin. "His colour's better, mind. He'll come round in his own time. By morning, if the gods are kind."

## Turn 2.2: "Was Pell up there with you?"

Astarion drops the drawl. "Was Pell up there with you?"

**[INSIGHT] Astarion: d20 7 + 1 = 8 vs DC 10. Failure.**

"No." No pause, no change in his voice. "I was on my own. Pell's got nothing to do with it." Tobin turns his face to the post. "Leave him out of it."

Astarion studies him through the gap and can make nothing of it: a farm boy who lies well, or a farm boy telling the truth. He rocks back on his heels. "How refreshing. A peasant I can't read."

## Turn 2.3: Watch the Kettles

Karlach picks a spot by the water trough and fixes her eyes on the two men in leather, openly, the way she'd stare down a dog. Neither leaves the wall. The farmers drift off by twos and threes to their cottages to eat, and come back. Crane sits on the top step with his crossbow across his knees.

As the light goes, Karlach catches the brothers muttering. The younger, with the crossbow: "How long are we stood here for?" The taller one, Gus, not looking at her: "Till he pays us to stand somewhere else." Wat: "And if he doesn't?" Gus shrugs.

Karlach doesn't blink. Gus, at last: "Something you want, red?" She grins with all her teeth and says nothing.
