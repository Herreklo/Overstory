# Round 1

## Pull
7: this is a BG3 opening and it does its job. A rope, a deadline, a mother screaming, and four companions who each see it differently. I want to know who split that boy's head.

## Thinking aloud
The scene gives the answer away a bit. A new, higher weir upstream and dying barley downstream means the miller is holding back the water and the farmers are paying for it. Someone smashing the sluice is the most natural thing in the world here, and it may even have been Tobin. What matters is who hit Pell. The reeve has keys and a crossbow, he wants this settled by sundown, and he is standing a lot closer to the miller's men than to the farmers. Astarion's line reads like a hint that the reeve is bought. I don't want to charge in on a hunch, though. Karlach is right that nobody has asked the kid anything, and Shadowheart counting the guards tells me this could turn into a fight against fifteen scared farmers, which I don't want. My plan is to read the reeve, buy time with my mouth, then split up and investigate. My worry is that this turns out to be a whodunit with one correct culprit. I want it messier than that: the farmers have a real grievance, Tobin may actually be guilty of the gate, and I have to decide what the gate is worth.

## Prediction
At sundown Hedda Brun or the farmers will rush the barn whatever I do, and the miller's crossbowman will shoot first. Pell will wake up and either can't remember or names someone from the mill. My guess is a hired man caught Tobin at the sluice and Pell got hit in the scuffle.
