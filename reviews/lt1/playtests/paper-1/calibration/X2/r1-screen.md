# Round 1

**Hob's Ford. Late afternoon, the light going long and yellow over the barley.**

## Turn 1.1: Watch the reeve

Shadowheart touches two fingers to your shoulder and murmurs a word under her breath. *Guidance.*

You hold your tongue and let Crane talk. "The Ford was here before the mill, and the mill was here before any of you. A sluice is the mill's. A hand on it is a hand on the mill. That's the law, and I keep it." His voice is level. His thumb works the ring of keys at his belt while he speaks. Whether his eyes go to Vask you can't be sure: the crowd shifts, and a hayfork rises between you.

**[INSIGHT] d20 7 + 3 (Insight) + 1 (Guidance) = 11 vs DC 13. Failure.**

Either he believes every word or he has said it so often that it sounds the same. You can't read him.

## Turn 1.2: "Hang no one until the apprentice wakes"

You step onto the bottom stair, close enough that only Crane and the front of the crowd can hear. "Reeve, you have fifteen farmers and four strangers watching. Hang the wrong boy, and when the apprentice wakes and names someone else, it's your neck they'll be measuring. Wait. Let him speak. Then nobody can say you didn't."

**[PERSUASION] d20 19 + 7 = 26 vs DC 14. Success.**

Crane is quiet for a long moment. Then: "The apprentice speaks first. When Pell wakes and says who did it, the law has its witness, and the rope will still be here." He raises his voice for the crowd. "No hanging tonight. Not till the boy at the shrine can talk."

A ragged noise from the farmers, half cheer, half suspicion. Hedda Brun stops shouting, which is somehow louder. The miller says a few low words to the taller of his hired men, then walks off up the path to the mill, fast, without looking back. The two in studded leather stay, leaning on the barn wall.

> *Karlach approves.*
> *Shadowheart approves.*

Karlach, grinning: "Ha! Talked a rope off a kid. I could kiss you." Astarion: "Marvellous. Now the whole thing hangs on an apprentice who may never wake. Do try to win something that pays, darling."

The hanging now waits on Pell. When he wakes and speaks, Crane will act on what he says.

## Turn 1.3: The party splits

**Astarion, behind the barn.** He slips round by the midden and crouches at a gap in the back boards. Inside, Tobin: seventeen, big in the shoulders, wrists tied behind the post, boots caked grey to the ankle. His knuckles, where Astarion can see them, are clean and unbroken.

Astarion: "Hello, condemned. Quick questions, before your reeve gets bored. Where were you last night, and why are your boots full of river?"

Tobin, low, through the boards: "At the weir. I opened the sluice. Me. That weir's been robbing every field below the mill since spring, and nobody would do a thing, so I did." "And the apprentice?" Tobin's jaw sets. "I never touched Pell. I'd never. I didn't see anyone up there." A beat. "Who are you? Why do you care?"

Astarion: "A friend of a friend, darling. I'm still deciding which friend."

**Karlach, at the barn front.** She plants herself between the crowd and the steps with her arms folded, and the farmers find it easier to grumble at her than to push past. Hedda Brun comes to stand beside her, flail on her shoulder.

Hedda: "Your lot did good just now. I'll say it once." Karlach: "So what's the deal with the weir?" Hedda: "Went up in spring, two foot higher. Since then the water gets as far as the mill, and the mill keeps it. Crane says the miller has it by right. Nobody asked the moot. We've asked the moot to look into it; Crane says there's nothing to look at." She spits. "Tobin would open that gate. He's said so all summer, loud, to anyone. But split Pell's head? Those two have been thick as thieves since Pell came to the mill. Pell's an orphan. Bound to Vask till he's grown." She jerks her chin at the men in leather. "The Kettle brothers. Gus and Wat. Vask's hired swords."

**You and Shadowheart, at the shrine.** A short climb up the lane: a whitewashed stone room with one lamp, a smell of vinegar and herbs. **Sister Maud**, grey-haired, sleeves rolled to the elbow, is wringing a cloth into a basin. On the cot, **Pell**: sixteen, thin, a bandage round his head, breathing slow. His pale smock lies folded on a stool, muddy to the knee and torn at the hem.

Maud looks you over. "Look, then. I've had a good look myself. That's the shape of a step, not a cudgel."

Shadowheart unwinds the bandage and tilts Pell's head to the lamp.

**[MEDICINE] Shadowheart: d20 7 + 5 = 12 vs DC 10. Success.**

Shadowheart: "One wound. Long and straight, the edge of something flat, with grit in it, and moss. No second blow. Nothing on his arms or hands, nothing on his face. Nobody hit him: he hit stone." She winds the bandage back. "Whether someone helped him onto it, a wound can't tell you."

Maud: "He came to me soaked to the bone. He hasn't opened his eyes since."

## Where you stand

**Hob's Ford, dusk coming.** The party is split three ways.

- **You and Shadowheart, at the shrine:** Sister Maud, Pell asleep on the cot, his smock on the stool.
- **Astarion, behind the barn:** Tobin at the post, on the other side of the boards.
- **Karlach, at the barn front:** Hedda Brun beside her; the farmers, quieter now; Reeve Crane on the steps; **Gus** and **Wat Kettle** on the barn wall, one with a crossbow.
- **Orrin Vask** has gone up to the mill. Upstream, unvisited: **the sluice gate** on the new weir.

**Sister Maud** (you're speaking)
1. "Can he be woken?"
2. [MEDICINE] Cast Healing Word on Pell and keep him lucid long enough to talk. (A level 1 slot. Shadowheart can roll the Medicine, or you.)
3. "Who's paying for his care?"
4. Leave him to rest.

**Tobin** (Astarion is speaking; Insight +1, Persuasion +0)
1. [INSIGHT] Watch his face: is there something he isn't saying?
2. [PERSUASION] "Someone was with you up there. Tell me, and I can keep your neck out of that rope."
3. [SLEIGHT OF HAND] Cut his ropes through the gap.
4. "Sit tight." Leave.
