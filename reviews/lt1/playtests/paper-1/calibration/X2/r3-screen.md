# Round 3

**Hob's Ford. The last light, then night.**

Making camp doesn't use a turn: night falls after your three, and the camp is where it finds you.

## Turn 3.1: Astarion goes up to the sluice

He leaves the barn by the midden, takes the ditch along the barley and goes up the bank in the shadow of the alders.

**[STEALTH] Astarion: d20 10 + 8 = 18 vs DC 12. Success.**

Nobody sees him go. The mill's lit window is at head height as he passes. Through the thick old glass: Orrin Vask, alone at a table by the lamp, his coat still floury, a cup in front of him that he isn't drinking from. Behind him, a stout door with an iron lock. He doesn't look up.

## Turn 3.2: Astarion examines the gate

The weir is a long ramp of stones, black and green with moss, the water sheeting over them. The sluice gate sits in a stone channel at the near end, and it hangs open: one board split clean down its length, the latch and the iron staple torn out of the wood together.

**[INVESTIGATION] Astarion: d20 5 + 1 = 6 vs DC 10. Failure.**
**Inspiration spent. Reroll: d20 10 + 1 = 11 vs DC 10. Success.**

The winch chain is still pinned; nobody wound it. The gate was forced instead: bright fresh scars in the wood on the upstream side, where something was driven between gate and post and leaned on until the latch gave. Astarion follows the scars down to the water, crouches and fishes in the reeds. An iron bar, as long as his arm, flattened at one end. Stamped into it, a wheel with four spokes: the same mark that is painted above the mill door.

He tries the stones for tracks, and for whatever step a boy might crack his head on, but in this light the weir is a mess of mud and moss and fast water, and he can't make it out.

**[PERCEPTION] Astarion: d20 2 + 3 = 5 vs DC 12. Failure.**

He leaves the bar where it lies.

## Turn 3.3: Karlach and the Kettles

"Your boss walked off. Who's paying you to hold up that wall?"

Gus Kettle, the taller one: "Vask is. Coin at the week's end." Wat says nothing; Gus says nothing more.

Karlach digs two coins out of her belt pouch and spins them in the air one after the other. "Ten each. What did he say to you before he went?"

Gus catches his, looks at it, pockets it. "Stay with Crane. Do what he tells you. If it turns ugly, come up and fetch me." He settles back against the wall. "And if you want us gone, red, that's a hundred, and we walk."

> *Astarion disapproves.*

**Gold: 194.**

## Night: camp at the edge of the Ford

You make camp in a hollow by the stream below the last cottage, where the barley stops. Supplies for a long rest: 40 spent, 6 left. Shadowheart doesn't come down. She stays at the shrine with Maud and the boy, a chair pulled up to the cot.

The fire burns low. Across the stream the barn is a dark shape with one lantern on its steps.

Karlach sits with her knees up and her hands spread to the flames, though she is the warmest thing for a mile. "You know what I keep thinking about? That kid's spending the night tied to a post. We bought him a night, sure. He's still on the post." She is quiet a while. "Someone made a deal over my head once. Sold me to a devil for a seat at a table, and I didn't find out till I was already in the Hells. A kid in a barn and a rope over a beam, and a weir nobody asked the moot about. Smells the same, soldier." She bumps your shoulder with hers. "Don't let it go the same way, yeah?"

Astarion, lying on his back with his hands behind his head: "Twenty gold for 'stay with the reeve'. I'd have told you that for nothing, darling, and thrown in a better haircut." Then, to the stars: "The mill's own bar, though. Either your sweet farm boy stole from the mill, or somebody at the mill handed it to him. I know which story I'd pay to hear."

Nobody comes to the camp in the night. You sleep.

**Long rest.** HP full. Slots restored: level 1, 4/4; level 2, 3/3. Bardic Inspiration 3/3. Inspiration 1.

## First light

Shadowheart comes down the lane in the grey, pale with lack of sleep, and crouches by the ashes before she says anything.

"He woke. An hour before dawn, properly this time. Knew his name, knew Maud, knew where he was." She keeps her voice low. "He says it was him and Tobin. The two of them went up to open the sluice, because of the farms. He had the bar from the mill. The gate went all at once, he lost his feet on the step and came down on it. Tobin pulled him out of the water. He remembers telling him to run." She looks at you. "Nobody hit him. He says so himself."

"Then he took my hand and begged me not to let Master Vask know it was him. The mill's the only roof he has." She rubs her eyes. "Maud heard all of it. She'll tell it the same way to anyone who asks."

Down at the Ford, the first chimney smoke is rising. The barn door is still shut. The lantern on its steps has burned down.

**Approval.** Karlach: Medium, rising. Shadowheart: Medium, rising. Astarion: Neutral, falling.

**Party.** Tav HP 31/31, Astarion 28/28, Karlach 41/41, Shadowheart 30/30. Gold 194. Camp supplies 6.
