# Round 0

A paper version of Baldur's Gate 3.

**Act One. Hob's Ford, a mill hamlet on the river road. Late afternoon.**

**Your party**
- **You, Tav:** half-elf Bard, level 4. HP 31/31, AC 14. Persuasion +7, Deception +7, Performance +5, Insight +3, Intimidation +3, Sleight of Hand +2, Investigation +1, Perception +1, Medicine +1.
  Spell slots: level 1, 4/4; level 2, 3/3. Bardic Inspiration 3/3. Inspiration 2 (each rerolls a failed check).
  Spells: Vicious Mockery, Friends, Minor Illusion; Healing Word, Dissonant Whispers, Charm Person, Tasha's Hideous Laughter; Hold Person, Calm Emotions.
- **Astarion,** rogue, HP 28/28. Stealth +8, Sleight of Hand +8; picks locks.
- **Karlach,** barbarian, HP 41/41. Athletics +6, Intimidation +3.
- **Shadowheart,** cleric, HP 30/30. Insight +5, Medicine +5. Guidance, Healing Word, Bless, Sanctuary.

Gold 214. Camp supplies 46.

**The scene.** A dozen cottages along a stream. Upstream, the mill and its new, higher weir. Downstream, barley gone yellow in the dry. Outside the tithe barn, fifteen farmers with flails and a hayfork. On the barn steps stands **Reeve Aldous Crane**, keys at his belt, a crossbow across his back. To one side, the miller **Orrin Vask**, flour on his coat, with two hired men in studded leather: cudgels, one crossbow. At the front of the crowd, a dwarf woman, **Hedda Brun**, is shouting at the reeve. Through the gaps in the barn boards you can see a young man tied to a post: her son, **Tobin**.

From the shouting you piece it together. Last night someone smashed the sluice gate on the weir. At dawn the miller's apprentice, **Pell**, was found on the weir stones with his head split; he hasn't woken, and **Sister Maud** is tending him at the shrine. The reeve caught Tobin on the road at first light with weir mud on his boots. He means to hang him at sundown.

Crane notices your weapons. "Travellers. You'll do as witnesses. The law of the Ford is plain: a hand raised against the mill hangs."

Karlach, under her breath: "A rope for a kid, and nobody's even asked him? No." Astarion: "The man with the mill pays for things, darling. Worth remembering." Shadowheart says nothing; she is counting the miller's men.

**Reply to the reeve**
1. "What's your proof?"
2. [INSIGHT] Watch the reeve while he talks about the law.
3. [PERSUASION] "Hang no one until the apprentice wakes and says who hit him."
4. [INTIMIDATION] "Nobody hangs while I'm standing here."
5. [BARD] [PERFORMANCE] Start a song to cool the crowd.
6. "Not our business." Leave the Ford.

**What you can do** (each is a turn): choose a reply; go to the sluice gate, the mill, the shrine or the barn; examine anything (Investigation, Perception); talk to anyone, or let a companion speak for the party; cast a spell, in or out of conversation; sneak, pickpocket or pick a lock; split the party; start a fight, or find a way round one. A check is a d20 plus your bonus against a difficulty you learn when you roll. Inspiration rerolls a failed check; Guidance adds 1d4; Bardic Inspiration gives a companion 1d6. Your companions approve or disapprove of what you do, and say so.

The hanging is set for sundown, which comes when you leave the Ford or rest. Push the reeve too hard and he may not wait.

Write your act file to calibration/X2/r1-act.md, up to three turns.
