# Round 1

## Pull
3 — The prose is pretty, but I've been told twice that I can't fail, can't miss anything and have no timer. So what am I here to win? A percentage.

## Thinking aloud
Okay. Pleasant harbour, nice smells, a dog in a Volvo. I've read this page before under other names. Seventeen things are listed and every one is a flavour card. The store page says there are no fail states and no wrong answers, which also means no stakes. Everything I can do is "look closer", and nothing I do can cost me. Nothing is a risk, and nothing is scarce: I have no money, no time limit and no inventory. The only number on the screen is Belonging, and it's a bar that fills if I show up.

Still, three things are not flavour. The chandler's window with one corner rubbed clear at a child's nose height is a small question someone could answer, and that's the nearest thing to a puzzle here. The lantern walk is "tonight", but the game also says I can't miss anything. Those two statements can't both be true, so I'll test the second one. And Teddy has a bucket and "very big plans" in the blurb, which is a person with a want, and a want is the only thing that could become a goal.

I'll give it two rounds. If the answer to every question is a warm paragraph and Belonging +2%, I'm out. I won't be drawn in by the music or the town.

## Prediction
Wilf will tell me about Marigold and who built her. It will be a kind little speech that ends with an invitation to come back, and Belonging will tick up by a point or two. He won't ask for anything real, because the game has promised that nothing matters.

## Needs
- What does Belonging unlock, concretely? "Watch your room" is not an answer.
- Is the lantern walk a one-night thing or not? "Tonight" and "you can't miss anything" disagree.
- Does what I say to people change anything later, or is each line a mood?
- Is there any decision in this game where I give something up?
- What are the sketches for besides the count?
