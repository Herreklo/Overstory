## Thinking aloud
Belonging went +16, then +17, for different acts. That is one sixth of a bar per round, so it pays for time passing, not for what I do. If the cardigan goes nowhere and I still get +17, I'm calling it a clock. Look at the world too. At five Wilf is still on the third plank, Sal is still sorting the same letters she had at eight, and Rob is still holding the same single-word question open. The only thing that has moved since breakfast is the weather. It's a stage set that turns when I look.

What worked: doing, not asking. Pulling the line got a disclosure the menu never offered. I also put two lines together myself: the postcard says the cardigan was found on the steps, and the store page says two sisters have watched the same stair all their lives. They're witnesses. That deduction felt like the nearest thing to a game so far, and it came from the text, not from a prompt. The lantern walk is tonight at dusk, and a one-night event can't wait until tomorrow, so I still don't know if "you can't miss anything" is true.

## Prediction
Dot and Ivy will know whose the cardigan is in one breath, and they'll disagree about it, because there are two of them and the blurb gave them one window. One will name someone I've met or heard of, and the other will say it's too small for them. I'll be sent to Sal for the tiebreak. It costs me nothing to be wrong.
