- Turn 1.4: curious — Admiral, caught every morning and let go at teatime, is a small ritual and I liked it. But I asked a menu question and got a sweet answer, exactly as predicted. Surprise: delight — the crab raising a claw at the sky, small.

## Pull
4 — Two threads have hooks: Wilf and Rob, and Tolley's stopped at 1988. Pull is still not a want to win something.

## Thinking aloud
Belonging went to 16% and a geranium appeared in my room. For what? I held a rag, looked at some glass and a noticeboard, and asked a boy about a crab. It paid out for turning up, not for anything I chose, so I've no reason to prefer one act to another. A bar that fills however I play is the thing I distrust most. By the maths it's about six more rounds to 100%. I'd like to know what happens at 100%, and I suspect the answer is "a nicer room".

I planned to ask Teddy about his big plans, but his menu has no such line. Those three lines are all small talk, so it's the menu steering me, not the boy. I want to see if my own words actually work.

Best lead: the postcard that says FOUND ON THE STEPS, ONE GREY CARDIGAN. ASK AT THE KETTLE. It's the only item on the screen with an owner who doesn't know where it is. That is a task, however small, and one with a right answer, and a right answer is what I'm missing. Second lead: Wilf, forty-one years on the Puffin, and a chandler that died in March 1988. Six years closed. I suspect they're connected.

## Prediction
Wilf will tell me Tolley's shut when old Tolley died or retired, and that no one took it on. He'll say it kindly, with a shrug, and go back to the brush. It will be a sad fact with no handle on it: nothing to do, nothing to find.

## Needs
- Can Belonging go down? If I'm cold to someone or walk off mid-sentence, does anything happen?
- Do my own words work, and does anything read them, or is it just matching to the nearest numbered line?
- Is the cardigan a thing I can carry and return, or only another look?
- Does the ferry's 13:00 actually leave without me?
- Who is Tolley, and who was the "& Son"?

## Turns

If I can carry it to someone and be wrong or right, that's the first game-shaped thing so far. If she says "oh, it'll turn up", I'm back to flavour.
3. If I'm given the cardigan: show it to Sal Brennan, the postwoman, who knows every doorstep. If not: talk to Teddy, line 3, "Can I have a go?" — Expect: Sal reads the wool or the stitching and names two possible owners, or Teddy hands me the line and nothing can go wrong. I'll note it if nothing can.
4. Talk to Rob on the Puffin: "Is there a boat to the island at one?" — Expect: a one-word reply. If the ferry has a clock and it can leave without me, I'll note it, and then I'll know whether "can't miss anything" is true or a polite lie.
