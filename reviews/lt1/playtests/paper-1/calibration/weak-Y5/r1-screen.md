# Round 1

**Turn 1.1. Wilf Garrow.**
You say: "Do you need a hand with that?"
He looks at you over his glasses, then fishes a rag out of his pocket and hands it up. "Catch that drip. There. Good." A bead of varnish was creeping down the plank towards the keel; you take it on the rag before it lands. He goes back to the brush, long strokes along the grain, the third plank up from the keel shining like toffee and the rest of her dull and waiting.
"She'll see me out, this one."
He works a while. "Forty-one years I took the Puffin over to the island. Rob has her now." He nods at the blue boat by the steps, where Rob is still coiling the same rope. "He doesn't talk to the passengers. I talked to every one."
He holds out his hand for the rag, and you give it back.

**Turn 1.2. Tolley & Son, the rubbed corner.**
You crouch to the clear patch in the whitewash. Inside, the shop is dim and nearly bare: empty shelves to the ceiling, a long counter with nothing on it, a coil of rope still on its hook by the door, and on the back wall a calendar from a marine paint company, open on March 1988. You try the door: locked.
*You can sketch this.*

**Turn 1.3. The noticeboard.**
Drawing pins rusted into the cork. LANTERN WALK · TONIGHT · FROM THE SEAMEN'S CHAPEL AT DUSK · ALL WELCOME · BRING A JAM JAR, in felt-tip capitals, with a jam jar drawn in the corner, light coming off it in little lines. Under it, the brass band's card: THURSDAYS 7 PM, SEAMEN'S HALL. NEW MEMBERS WANTED (ESPECIALLY TUBA), the tuba underlined twice. And the postcard in blue biro: FOUND ON THE STEPS, ONE GREY CARDIGAN. ASK AT THE KETTLE. No other time is given than dusk.

**Turn 1.4. Teddy Marsh.**
You say: "Caught anything?"
He tips the red bucket towards you without letting go of the line. One crab, a big green-brown one, sidles round the bottom and raises a claw at the sky. "Just Admiral. He always comes back."
He goes back to watching the water where the line goes in. "I catch him every morning and let him go at teatime. He doesn't mind."

**Your prediction** (Wilf would talk about the Marigold and who built her, a kind speech, an invitation back, a point or two of Belonging; he wouldn't ask for anything real): he talked about the Puffin, not the Marigold, and asked you to catch a drip.

**About noon. The Strand.** The cloud has thickened from the west and the water has gone the colour of pewter. The tide is turning; the weed on the quay steps lifts and settles. Inside the Kettle the lights have come on.
