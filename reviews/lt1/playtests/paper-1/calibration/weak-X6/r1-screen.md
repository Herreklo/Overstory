# Round 1

**Turn 1.1. Wilf.**
You: "Do you need a hand with that?"
He hands you a rag without looking up, nodding at a bead of varnish creeping down the third plank. "Catch that drip. There. Good." You catch it. He draws the brush along the plank, slow and level, the wood going from dull to honey where it passes. "She'll see me out, this one."
He sits back on his heels. "Forty-one years I took the Puffin over to the island. Rob has her now." A tilt of the head towards the blue motorboat and the man coiling rope in her stern. "He doesn't talk to the passengers. I talked to every one."
He takes the rag back and goes on with the plank.

*Wilf, next time:*
1. "How's she coming along?"
2. "Can I help today?"
3. "Tell me about the Puffin."

**Turn 1.2. Tolley & Son, the rubbed corner.**
You bend to the clear patch in the whitewash. Inside, in the grey light: empty shelves, floor to ceiling, a coil of rope still hanging on its hook, and on the back wall a calendar with a lighthouse on it, turned to March 1988. Dust on the counter. The door is locked; the brass of its handle has gone brown.

**Turn 1.3. The noticeboard.**
Cork, drawing pins rusted to the colour of the mooring rings. LANTERN WALK · TONIGHT · FROM THE SEAMEN'S CHAPEL AT DUSK · ALL WELCOME · BRING A JAM JAR, hand-lettered in poster paint, the J run a little in the damp. The brass band's card, the TUBA underlined twice. The postcard about the grey cardigan, in blue biro.
Not everything goes in the sketchbook. The noticeboard isn't one of them.

**Turn 1.4. Sal, at the foot of Wren Steps.**
You walk over. She looks up from the bundle on her knee, a rubber band round her wrist.
You: "Who should I meet first?"
"Nothing much happens here. That's the beauty of it." She squares the letters against her knee and looks up the stair. "Up and up, and then you're there. That's Wrenmouth for you."
She flexes one foot. "Eighty-seven steps, twice a day, eleven years. Calves like a footballer."
The bundle on her knee is no smaller.

*Sal, next time:*
1. "Anything for me?"
2. "Busy morning?"
3. "Which way's the chapel?"

## About noon. The foot of Wren Steps.

The cloud has thickened while you weren't looking; the light on the water has gone from silver to pewter, and the gulls have come down off the chimneys onto the quay. The tide is out now, the weed on the quay steps green below the line and black above. Somewhere up the hill a wireless is playing a brass band.

**In view**
- **Sal Brennan**, on the low wall, sorting letters. **Her red bicycle**, against the wall.
- **Wren Steps**, climbing between the houses, the iron handrail up the middle.
- **The Hales' window**, halfway up, red geraniums against the glass.
- **The Kettle**, beside you, its windows steamed. **June Askew**, inside, wiping the table by the window.
- **Tolley & Son**, its whitewashed windows.
- Across the Strand: **Wilf Garrow** at the Marigold, the same plank; **Teddy Marsh** at the quay's edge with his crab line and **red bucket**; **the Puffin** at the steps with **Rob Garrow** in her stern; **the bench** with its brass plaque; **the phone box**; **the brown Volvo**, Skipper asleep in the back.
