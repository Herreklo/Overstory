## Thinking aloud
The clock jumped from 8:10 to about noon over four turns, so time does pass. That clashes with "you can't miss anything", because the ferry runs at 10.30, 13.00 and 15.30 and the Lantern Walk is at dusk. I've already missed the 10.30 boat. One of the two claims is a lie, or the events repeat.

The real problem is measurement. Belonging went 0 to 16 across four turns that included two conversations, two places entered (Strand, Wren Steps) and two inspects. I only see the total at the end of the round, so I can't tell which act paid. Journal lists Wren Steps as a "place", so walking might pay too. If it is about 8 per person, 30+ townsfolk would overshoot 100 several times, so it is concave or capped, or places count. The geranium is probably a threshold reward.

Cleanest way to find the dominant line with one reading per round: spend the whole round on one lever. The lever that would break the game is repeat conversation. If talking to Wilf again pays, I loop him and ignore the town. If it pays nothing, breadth is the game and the cheap line is a first chat with everyone. I'll talk to Wilf four times, the last one in free text with a concrete question (Tolley's, 1988) so I also learn whether free text is read.
