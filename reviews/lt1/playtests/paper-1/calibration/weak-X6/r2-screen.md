# Round 2

**Turn 2.1. Wilf.**
You: "Tell me about the Puffin."
"The Puffin? Ten minutes across, every day of the year, near enough." He doesn't look up from the plank. "Same as yesterday. Same as tomorrow, if the weather holds."
He lays the brush across the tin and runs his palm down the hull, over the green copper heads. "My father built her the winter I was born. Larch on oak, copper all through. You won't find a nail in her."

*Wilf, next time:* 1. "How's she coming along?" 2. "Can I help today?" 3. "Tell me about the Puffin."

**Turn 2.2. Wilf.**
The first rain arrives, soft enough that you feel it before you see it. Wilf hauls a green tarpaulin over a pole between the trestles and ties it off without a word, and the two of you are under it, the rain ticking on the canvas.
You: "Can I help today?"
He hands you the rag. "Catch that drip. There. Good." You catch it. "Same as yesterday. Same as tomorrow, if the weather holds."
He's quiet a while, brushing. "My boy's out on the rigs. He'll have her one day, I expect." He feels in his jacket pocket. "Here, take this. Every boat wants a spare."
A brass rowlock, heavier than it looks, worn smooth and bright on the inside where an oar turned in it.

## About 5 pm. The Strand, under Wilf's tarpaulin.

The rain has given up as gently as it started. In the west the cloud has torn along its bottom edge and a long bar of yellow light lies across the harbour, so that every wet thing on the Strand shines: the cobbles, the Volvo's roof, the mooring rings, the third plank of the Marigold. Water drips from the tarpaulin's edge. Up at the top of the hill, along the white wall of the Seamen's Chapel, a row of jam jars catches the light.

**In view**
- **Wilf Garrow**, beside you, varnishing the third plank of **the Marigold**.
- **Teddy Marsh**, at the quay's edge in a yellow oilskin, his crab line in the water, **the red bucket** at his boots.
- **The Puffin**, at the quay steps; **Rob Garrow** in her stern, coiling a rope. **The ferry board** beside the slip.
- **The Kettle**, its windows steamed and lit; **June Askew** inside, wiping the table by the window. **Sal Brennan**, under the café's awning, sorting letters, **her red bicycle** dripping beside her.
- **Tolley & Son**, its whitewashed windows.
- **The bench**, wet, its brass plaque. **The phone box.** **The noticeboard.** **The brown Volvo**, Skipper asleep in the back.
- **The foot of Wren Steps**, and halfway up, **the Hales' window**.
