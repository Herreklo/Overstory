# Round 1

**Saturday 8 October 1994, about noon. The Strand.** The cloud has come down off the headland and thickened to a soft grey lid. The water has gone the colour of pewter. The tide is turning.

## Turn 1.1: Wilf Garrow

"Do you need a hand with that?"

Wilf hands you a rag without looking round. "Catch that drip. There. Good." A bead of varnish was creeping down the third plank from the keel; you catch it before it reaches the edge, and the rag comes away amber and smelling of pine. He goes back to his brush. "She'll see me out, this one."

He works the brush along the grain, slow and even. "Forty-one years I took the Puffin over to the island. Rob has her now. He doesn't talk to the passengers. I talked to every one."

**Wilf, next time**
1. "How's she coming along?"
2. "Can I help today?"
3. "Tell me about the Puffin."

## Turn 1.2: Tolley & Son's window

You crouch until your eyes are level with the clear corner. The glass is cold and smells of old putty. Inside it is dim: empty shelves running back into the dark, a coil of rope still hanging on its hook, and on the far wall a calendar from a ships' paint company, open at March 1988. Dust lies on everything in a soft, even fur. You try the door. Locked.

*You can sketch this now.*

## Turn 1.3: Teddy Marsh

You say: "Caught anything?"

Teddy tips the red bucket towards you, very carefully, with both hands. One crab, the size of a saucer, sits in an inch of seawater and raises a claw at you. "Just Admiral. He always comes back." He sniffs. "I catch him every morning and let him go at teatime. He doesn't mind."

You ask about the chandler's window, and the clear corner. "I rubbed it," he says. "There's nothing in there. Just rope." He goes back to his line.

**Teddy, next time**
1. "Caught anything?"
2. "What's the bait?"
3. "Can I have a go?"

## Turn 1.4: Sal Brennan

You say: "Anything for me?"

Sal riffles through the bundle on her knee without hurrying. "Give me a minute. I'm in the right street, at least." She tucks a letter under her thumb, frowns at it, and moves it to the back. "Up and up, and then you're there. That's Wrenmouth for you." She nods at the steps. "Eighty-seven steps, twice a day, eleven years. Calves like a footballer."

You ask about the grey cardigan on the noticeboard. "That's been on June's hook since August. A visitor's, I'd say." The bundle on her knee is no smaller.

**Sal, next time**
1. "Anything for me?"
2. "Busy morning?"
3. "Which way's the chapel?"

**The foot of Wren Steps.** In view:

- **The Kettle**, café, the radio murmuring. **June Askew** at the window table, wiping.
- **Tolley & Son, Ship Chandlers**, the clear corner low in the whitewash.
- **Wilf Garrow** at the top of the slip, at the Marigold.
- **Teddy Marsh** at the quay's edge, line in the water.
- **The noticeboard** and the phone box. **The ferry board**; **the Puffin** at the steps, **Rob Garrow** in the stern.
- **The bench** with the brass plaque. **The brown Volvo estate**, **Skipper** asleep in the back.
