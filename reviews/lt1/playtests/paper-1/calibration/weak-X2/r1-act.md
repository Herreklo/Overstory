# Round 1

## Pull
4. The writing is lovely, and "no timers, no fail states, no wrong answers" is the line on a store page that makes me close the tab.

## Thinking aloud
I'll be honest: this isn't my kind of game, and the store page says so three times. Nothing is expected of me, I can't fail, and I can't miss anything. To an RPG player that reads as "nothing you do matters". Still, the opening is well written. The mooring rings polished on top, and roofs looking into each other's windows, make a real place. I'm hunting for teeth, though, and there are a few hints that something here isn't fine. The chandler's windows are whitewashed from inside, but someone the height of a child keeps rubbing a corner clear to look in. "Tolley & Son" suggests the son is part of the story. A grey cardigan was found on the steps that two old women have watched all their lives. If the game is hiding a story under the cosy, I'll find it. If it's sketches and belonging percentages all the way down, I'm out in a round or two. I also don't know who I am. In BG3 a blank Tav at least has a tadpole. Here my character's whole reason for coming is "for as long as the autumn lasts".

## Prediction
The grey cardigan belongs to one of the Hale sisters, or to someone they watched go up the steps. Returning it will be the game's first little errand. Tolley's shop has been shut since something happened to the son, and Teddy is the one who rubs the window clear.

## Needs
- Who am I, and why have I come here alone for an autumn?
- Does Belonging do anything except decorate my room?
- Is there any choice in this game that changes what happens to someone?

## Turns
1. **Talk to Wilf**, option 2: "Do you need a hand with that?" I'd rather do something than admire it. Expect: he hands me a brush or a rag and starts on how his father built Marigold.
2. **Inspect the rubbed-clear corner** of Tolley & Son's window. I crouch to a child's height and look through. Expect: a dusty shop left as it was the day it closed, with something inside that a boy would want.
3. **Talk to Teddy Marsh.** "Big plans, I hear. Is one of them that shop?" Expect: he goes red, tells me his plan, and lets slip what's in the chandler's.
4. **Talk to Sal Brennan** at the foot of the steps. A postwoman knows every door in town. "Whose is the grey cardigan on the noticeboard?" Expect: gossip, and a name that points up the steps to Dot and Ivy.
