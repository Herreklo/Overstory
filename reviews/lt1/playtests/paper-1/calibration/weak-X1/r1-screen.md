# Round 1

## Turn 1.1: Wilf Garrow

You say: "Do you need a hand with that?"

He looks at you over his glasses, then hands you a rag, folded square and stiff with old varnish. "Catch that drip. There. Good." A bead of varnish runs down the plank towards the gunwale, and you catch it.

"She'll see me out, this one."

He dips the brush and draws it along the third plank up from the keel, slow and even, the length of his arm. "Forty-one years I took the Puffin over to the island. Rob has her now. He doesn't talk to the passengers. I talked to every one."

## Turn 1.2: The noticeboard

A cork board behind a little glass door that doesn't quite shut, the pins rusted into the cork.
- The lantern walk poster is hand-lettered in poster paint, with a drawing of a jam jar and a candle flame in yellow crayon. ALL WELCOME is underlined.
- The brass band's card is typed, with TUBA added in biro and underlined twice.
- The postcard about the cardigan is held by a single pin and has curled at the corners. FOUND ON THE STEPS, ONE GREY CARDIGAN. ASK AT THE KETTLE.
- Behind them, older notices layered like slate: a whist drive in August, a lost cat (found, someone has written across it), the tide tables for September.

## Turn 1.3: Tolley & Son's window

You crouch to the rubbed corner. The glass is cold. Through the clear patch, a long shop in half-light: empty shelves going back into the dark, a coil of rope still on its hook, a calendar on the wall turned to March 1988, a picture of a lifeboat above the month. Dust on the counter. The door is locked.

*You could sketch this.*

## Turn 1.4: Wilf Garrow

You say: "Can I help today?"

The rag again. "Catch that drip. There. Good." You catch it.

"Same as yesterday. Same as tomorrow, if the weather holds."

"My father built her the winter I was born. Larch on oak, copper all through. You won't find a nail in her." He taps a rivet with the end of the brush, and it rings faintly.

**Wilf's menu, next time**
1. "How's she coming along?"
2. "Can I help today?"
3. "Tell me about the Puffin."

## The Strand, about noon

The cloud has come down off the headland and thickened over the harbour; the light has gone flat and pewter, and the water has the same colour as the sky. The gulls have moved from the chimneys to the quay. Somebody in the Kettle has turned the radio up.

**In view**
- **The Marigold**, upturned on her trestles. The third plank is glossy and wet; the rest are dull and waiting.
- **Wilf Garrow**, kneeling at her side, brush in hand.
- **The Kettle**, café. **June Askew** inside the window, wiping the table by the glass.
- **Teddy Marsh**, at the quay's edge with his crab line and red bucket.
- **Tolley & Son, Ship Chandlers**, the rubbed corner at a child's height.
- **The noticeboard**, by the phone box.
- **The ferry board**: EIDER ISLAND · THE PUFFIN · 10.30 · 13.00 · 15.30. **The Puffin** at the quay steps, **Rob Garrow** in her stern.
- **A bench** facing the water, with a brass plaque.
- **A brown Volvo estate**, **Skipper** asleep in the back.
- **A mooring ring**, set in the quay's edge.
- **The foot of Wren Steps**, **Sal Brennan** on the low wall with her letters, her red bicycle beside her.
- Halfway up the steps, **Dot and Ivy Hale** at their window among the geraniums.
