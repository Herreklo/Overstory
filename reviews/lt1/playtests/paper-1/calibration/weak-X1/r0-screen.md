# Round 0

A new game, played on paper.

## The store page

**Saltlight.** A gentle exploration game. Autumn, 1994. $14.99.

You've taken a room above a café in Wrenmouth, a steep little harbour town where the coast road runs out, for as long as the autumn lasts. Nobody is expecting anything of you. Wander its wooden lanes and stone steps, ride the little ferry out to Eider Island, sit on the quay while the boats come in, and get to know the people who make the town what it is: a retired ferryman and the boat his father built, two sisters who have watched the same stair from the same window all their lives, a boy with a crab line and very big plans.

- A hand-made harbour town of more than 40 places, every corner worth a closer look
- More than 30 townsfolk, each with a story to uncover
- 48 sketches and 20 keepsakes to find
- A journal that writes itself as you go
- No timers, no fail states, no wrong answers
- An original score of fiddle, piano and rain

## How to play

- Each screen lists what is in view by its **hover name**.
- **Inspect** anything to look closer. Some things can then be **sketched** into your sketchbook.
- **Talk** to people and choose what to say: pick a numbered line, or put it in your own words.
- **Sit** on a bench or a wall and watch the town for a while.
- **Walk** anywhere on the map, or to anything in view.
- **Your journal** writes itself: the people you've met, the places you've been, your sketches and keepsakes.
- **Belonging** grows as you get to know Wrenmouth. Watch your room as it does.
- There's no wrong way to play. You can't fail, and you can't miss anything: whatever you don't see today will still be here tomorrow.

## Chapter One: Arrivals

**Saturday 8 October 1994, 8:10 am. The Strand.** Grey and mild, a little wind off the water, the tide half out.

The coast bus has gone back round the headland, and you're standing on the Strand with your bag at your feet. The quay is old granite, its edge worn round, with iron mooring rings set into it every few paces, rusted orange and polished on top where ropes have worked them. Behind the quay the hill goes up in wooden houses, white and ochre and ox-blood red, gable ends to the harbour and a television aerial on every ridge, stacked so steeply that each roof looks into the upstairs windows of the next. Gulls on the chimneys. Diesel, wet rope, seaweed and, from somewhere close, toast.

**In view**
- **The Kettle**, café. Steamed windows; a radio inside reading the shipping forecast. A card in the door: ROOMS · ENQUIRE WITHIN. Your room is the one above, with the blue window frame.
- **June Askew**, inside the café window, wiping the table by the glass.
- **Wilf Garrow**, at the top of the slip, kneeling beside an upturned wooden boat on trestles, varnishing one plank with a small brush. Painted on her stern: MARIGOLD.
- **Teddy Marsh**, at the quay's edge, a crab line wound round one finger and a red bucket by his boots.
- **Tolley & Son, Ship Chandlers.** Gold letters on the fascia, faded to the colour of tea. The windows are whitewashed from inside; one lower corner has been rubbed clear, at about the height of a child's nose.
- **The noticeboard**, beside the phone box. LANTERN WALK · TONIGHT · FROM THE SEAMEN'S CHAPEL AT DUSK · ALL WELCOME · BRING A JAM JAR. Under it: WRENMOUTH BRASS BAND, THURSDAYS 7 PM, SEAMEN'S HALL. NEW MEMBERS WANTED (ESPECIALLY TUBA). And a postcard in blue biro: FOUND ON THE STEPS, ONE GREY CARDIGAN. ASK AT THE KETTLE.
- **The ferry board**, by the slip. EIDER ISLAND · THE PUFFIN · 10.30 · 13.00 · 15.30 · RETURNS ON THE HOUR. Below it the Puffin herself, a small blue motorboat, knocks against the quay steps. **Rob Garrow**, her skipper, sits in the stern coiling a rope.
- **A bench**, facing the water, with a brass plaque.
- **A brown Volvo estate**, parked on the cobbles. **Skipper**, an old black dog, is asleep in the back.
- **The foot of Wren Steps**, between the café and the chandler's: a stone stair climbing the hill between the houses, an iron handrail up its middle. **Sal Brennan**, the postwoman, sits on the low wall at the bottom, sorting a bundle of letters on her knee, her red bicycle leaning beside her.
- Halfway up the steps, a window crowded with red geraniums and, behind the glass, two white heads close together: **Dot and Ivy Hale**.

Wilf looks up as you pass, his brush held clear of the wood. "Morning! You'll be June's lodger."
