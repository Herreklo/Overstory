## Thinking aloud
"Salt Radio" is Andy's "Salt Lanterns" and "Grey Radio" in one title. It came to the band in September, three months after Andy joined. Neil gives it to Sean, and Sean won't claim it. That's my best guess at the song credited to the wrong man. Oliver Brook was in both bands, so he's the one witness to the song, to "Cold Hours" and to why Driftwood split. If the split was the bad blood, Andy joining Oliver's new band in June may be what settled it. Then there's Karen. If his lamp burned most days, she knows the last day it burned, and that dates his last day better than anyone's "couldn't say". I'm not using the check. It costs one of three turns and answers all-or-nothing, which is insurance, not information. Lesson from case 1: no "so" in the account. Every line has to be a sighting, a witness or a record.

## Prediction
Sean Hale won't claim "Salt Radio" when Neil's words are put to him. He'll say it came out of Andy's songs, or go guarded. Neil's "start to finish" is the wrong credit.
