# Round 6: what happened

**Filed:** claims 4 to 10 as you wrote them. Your board holds ten.

**Turn 1 · Monday 7 December, morning · the fleet's berth on the quay.** The boats haven't gone out yet. Oliver Brook is on the quay by his boat, coiling a line. On the loft door above the berth there's an old poster, its colours faded:
> **DRIFTWOOD** · with Sarah Lane · the Anchor · Friday 15 March · Oliver Brook · Andy Shaw · Edward Baker · Sarah Lane · "Cold Hours" · "Salt Lanterns"

*Hover: Oliver Brook, 42, skipper, the harbour fleet; open.*
- "Who wrote 'Salt Radio'?" — "Sean."
- "Who wrote 'Cold Hours'?" — "Andy."
- "Why did Driftwood split?" — "We finished in the September. I couldn't tell you why."

He drops the line into the boat and goes aboard.

**Turn 2 · Monday 7 December, afternoon · the Old Bank Café.** Sean Hale is wiping tables. *Hover: Sean Hale, 47, the Old Bank Café; open.*
- "Neil Lyons says 'Salt Radio' is yours, start to finish. Is it?" — "It is. Mine. I brought it to them in September, and Andy played it with us at the Anchor the first night."
- "Did Andy have a hand in it?" — "He played it. Guitar."
- "How did you hear he had died, and from whom?" — "Same as everyone. It went round."

**Turn 3 · Tuesday 8 December, evening · Karen's shed.** A drawer is on the bench, its runner set. Karen is putting her tools away before cards. *Hover: Karen Holmes, 74, retired; open.*
- "On what day did you last see Andy's lamp lit?" — "The Tuesday. The day before he went."
- "From your bench, did you hear him play in his shed, and what?" — "Never heard him play in there. Mending things, he was, same as me. I saw him play at the Anchor with the new lot in September."
- "Was there anyone he'd fallen out with?" — "Larry Baker said at cards that Sarah Lane had a few words with him in the Anchor. Summer before last. Nothing came of it that I ever saw."

She padlocks the shed and goes down the steps to the cards.

**Your prediction** (Sean won't claim "Salt Radio"): Sean says it's his.

**Turn 4 · Friday 11 December, ten · the hearing, upstairs at the council office.** The coroner reads your account into the record. Oliver Brook gave no reason for the split, and no one said "Salt Radio" came from Andy, so your fifth answer reads "none found". Five people are named and summoned: Hannah Tanner, Toby Tanner, Karen Holmes, Olive Shaw and Neil Lyons.

1. **Of what did he die?** *Not established. Nobody asked could say who found him. Karen Holmes last saw his lamp lit on Tuesday 13 October.* Karen confirms it. **Stands.**
2. **Since when unwell?** *Nobody saw him unwell: at the Anchor on Friday 9 October (Olive Shaw, Neil Lyons), down for his walk on Tuesday 13 October (Hannah Tanner).* Each confirms what they saw. **Stands.**
3. **Was anyone answerable?** *No one found.* **Stands.**
4. **Who stood by him?** *No kin found. Nobody stood as family at his grave. On Fridays he sat with Neil Lyons. His band played at the Anchor after the funeral.* Olive and Neil confirm the Fridays. **Stands.**
5. **Bad blood?** *None found. Nothing found ties anything to his death.* **Stands.**
6. **The printed record?** *The notice stands. The piece of 22 October stands in part: the songs "Salt Lanterns" and "Grey Radio", the shore, the Fridays and Pirate Radio. "Seldom out of his shed until May" does not stand.* Karen confirms what she saw. *"Cold Hours", the coast road, 1990 and the marriage dates are not shown.* Toby confirms that the running and "Cold Hours" came to him as talk. *The piece of 24 September misdates the first night.* Toby confirms he wrote it. **Stands.**

**Ruling: accepted.** Nothing struck. Standing rises to **4 of 6**. The coroner records: *Andy Shaw, 85, died at home on the Gully Steps on 14 October 1998. The cause is not established. Nobody saw him unwell. He was last seen at home on 13 October. Nobody is answerable. No family stood by him. He sat with Neil Lyons on Fridays, and his band played after his funeral. No bad blood was found. The notice stands. The piece of 22 October stands but for "until May".*

# The tenth hour

**The close.** Your cases are done, and the record is opened to you.

*Mary Tanner:* nothing on record says what she died of. She had no illness on record, and nobody was answerable. Mark Holmes, Tess Lane and Andy Shaw were most often with her. She had words with Susie Lyons in 1992 and argued with Andy Shaw in the street in August 1996, and nothing on record followed. The notice and Toby's piece both stand whole.

*Andy Shaw:* (1) Nothing on record says what he died of. (2) He had no illness on record; two years before, he had an operation in the city and was home within the week. (3) Nobody caused his death. (4) Most often with him in his last three years: Oliver Brook, Edward Baker, Sarah Lane and Neil Lyons. (5) Sarah Lane had words with him at the Anchor in July 1997. (6) In June 1998 Alice Tanner and Sarah Lane both tried out for Pirate Radio, and he got the place. (7) Nothing on record follows from either. (8) "Salt Lanterns" is his, and a 1995 poster credits it to "Ash". (9) Driftwood split in September 1996, and nothing says why; Pirate Radio, from June, was his last band. (10) His routines never stopped. He joined Driftwood in December 1990, and "Cold Hours" was his: Toby's piece is right but for "until May". The 1990 items and some of the band lists carry the paper's slips. **Case 2: 55 of 100.**

**December.** Those named at the hearing heard the account read. Nobody turns, and Neil Lyons stays guarded. You keep after the song. Oliver is never at the Anchor and Neil gives one answer a visit, and every answer puts "Salt Radio" with Sean. On Friday 18 December Mona Sayer and Terry Sutton throw a party for their friends at the Anchor, and the room is full. On Christmas Day, from your corner, you see Sean Hale and Larry Baker fall out at the bar. You can't hear over what.

**January.** Neil Lyons leaves his job at the sawmill. Pirate Radio take a new song of Oliver's, "Cold Lanterns", which the Courier never prints. "At the Anchor": the quiz night goes to Lynn Newman. Nobody in the town dies. No case comes, and there is nothing to file, check or rule on. Your board stays empty, and your standing stays at 4.
