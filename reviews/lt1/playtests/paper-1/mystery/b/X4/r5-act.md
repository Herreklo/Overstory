# Round 5

## From memory
- **Hannah Tanner:** No. 5, across the steps, always at her kitchen sink with her back to the window. She sang in the choir with Mary but was no kin to her. She won a meat raffle and has painted her frames blue. She watches Andy's door, and still she couldn't say who found him.
- **Karen Holmes:** No. 8, one house up, in her lit shed with something glued in the vice (a chair leg, then a picture frame). She won't say what it's for. She's at the Anchor on Fridays, and Tuesdays for cards. I found no tie to Mark Holmes.
- **Andy Shaw:** No. 6, the green door with the brass knocker. Down to the shore at half past five and back at seven, one hand on the rail. He nodded to me. He joined a band at 84.
- I remember all three by their doors and habits. I couldn't tell you one face, and I'd only know them again on their own steps.

## Marks
- Turn 4.1: engaged — the 1990 volumes put Toby himself in Driftwood as a founder and never put Andy in it. So the man who wrote "in 1990 he joined Driftwood" was inside the story, and Oliver Brook links both bands. My prediction failed, and fairly. Surprise: delight — the obituary's author turns out to be a witness.
- Turn 4.2: curious — I like how Toby keeps what he saw apart from what he heard. But "I had it off their poster" turns my one contradiction into a copying slip. A misdated gig is true and weighs nothing. Surprise: none.
- Turn 4.3: confused — Sean is "open" and plays in the band, yet he "couldn't say" who wrote a song they play. Either he's lying and hover hides it, or the game has no answer. I can't tell which, and that's the one doubt a deduction game mustn't leave me with. The wrong café was my own mistake. Surprise: none yet, but a cheat if it turns out to be a blank.
- Turn 4.4: curious — Olive put Neil Lyons at Andy's Friday table. Neil is the bandmate who skipped the grave and announced "Salt Radio" without a credit. But her answers were three flat lines. Surprise: none.

## Pull
6 — the contradiction I was proud of was a typo, and three "couldn't say"s in a row have cooled me. Toby inside Driftwood and Neil at Andy's table keep me playing.

## Thinking aloud
Four people don't know who found Andy. With Mary, nobody gave a cause either, so questions 1 and 2 may come out "not established" in every case. If so, tell me, and I'll stop spending turns on them. The songs are the live thread. Toby can trace two of the obituary's three to his own 1995 piece, but "Cold Hours" only to talk. I now think "Cold Hours" is the store page's song credited to the wrong man, not "Salt Radio". Oliver Brook (Driftwood then, Pirate Radio now) and Neil would know. I've avoided Karen because she only ever agreed with everyone. But her steps look down into Andy's yard and his shed, and she's the other shed-worker on the steps. "Seldom out of his shed until May", then a band in June: it's the only change in his habits I know of, and I want to know what happened in May.

## Prediction
Oliver Brook will say "Cold Hours" was another Driftwood member's song (Peter Lane's or his own) and that Andy only sang it. Toby could trace two of the three songs and not that one, and the store page promises a wrongly credited song.
