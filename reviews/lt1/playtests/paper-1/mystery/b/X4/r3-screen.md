# Round 3: what happened

**Turn 1 · Monday 26 October, evening · the Gully Steps.** No. 6: the curtains are drawn upstairs and down, and there is no milk on the step. The letterbox is shut, so you can't see behind the door. In Karen Holmes's vice is a picture frame, one corner glued and clamped.

*Why: "Andy Shaw died, aged 85." October 1998. Nothing on record says why.*

Hannah Tanner comes to her door at No. 5. *Hover: Hannah Tanner, 83, retired; open.*
- "When did you last see Andy, and how was he?" — "The Tuesday before he went. Going down for his walk at half past five, same as ever."
- "Who found him, and when?" — "I couldn't tell you that."
- "Where did he go each evening at half past five?" — "Down to the shore. Out at half past five, back up about seven. Every evening."

**Turn 2 · Tuesday 27 October, afternoon · the churchyard on the church hill.** You watch from the lane by the churchyard wall. At two, ten people gather at an open grave near the top wall:
- Hannah Tanner (83, retired); Ken Scott (40, editor, the Courier); Molly Scott (71, retired); Sean Hale (47, the Old Bank Café); Trevor Lane (71, retired); Larry Baker (77, retired); Susie Lyons (74, retired); Lynn Newman (42, the garage); Oscar Ash (43, crew, the harbour fleet); Ellie Eden (44, the Old Bank Café).

They stand in a loose ring and nobody stands apart where family would. A man in a dark coat reads at the graveside. It's over in twenty minutes. By half past two they are walking down the hill in twos and threes towards the town.

**Turn 3 · Tuesday 27 October, evening · the Anchor, Harbour Row.** You take a corner seat and watch from seven.
- **Behind the bar:** Evan Newman (58, manager), Peter Scott (46), Agnes Tanner (31) and Gary Newman (39).
- **On the low stage by the window:** Pirate Radio, three of them: Neil Lyons (50, the sawmill), Oliver Brook (42, skipper, the harbour fleet) and Sean Hale. On the wall by the door, a poster:
  > **PIRATE RADIO** · first night at the Anchor · Friday 18 September · Neil Lyons · Oliver Brook · Sean Hale · Andy Shaw · with "Salt Radio"
- **At the card tables,** it being Tuesday: Karen Holmes, Audrey Scott, Jonah Scott, Kate Holmes, Mona Sayer, Trevor Lane, Larry Baker, Susie Lyons, Alice Tanner, Ellen Vaughan, Stella Lane, Sally Baker, Lynn Newman, Ellie Eden.
- **Along the bar and by the fire:** Sid Tanner, Vera Holmes, Molly Scott, Olive Shaw (68, retired) at a table with her pint, Eric Lane, Tara Myers, Terry Sutton, Steve Baker, Leo Lyons, Eve Vaughan, Tess Lane, Alex Myers, Lily Lyons. From the churchyard, Hannah Tanner and Oscar Ash.

Pirate Radio play for about an hour. Neil Lyons announces one song by name: "This is Salt Radio." The cards go on through the set. Nobody makes a speech. You see no words between anyone, and no one leaves early. By eleven the room is emptying.

**Turn 4 · Wednesday 28 October, morning · the Courier.** Ken Scott is at the counter and Toby Tanner is typing. A third desk in the back room has been cleared, its typewriter under a cover.
- **15 October:** nothing on Andy Shaw, and no "At the Anchor" lines that week.
- **22 October:** the notice you have, and on page 2, by Toby Tanner:
  > **Andy Shaw, 1913 to 1998.** Andy Shaw died last week. He was 85. Born in the town in 1913, he married Stella Shaw in 1957 and lost her in 1978. He walked the shore every evening, ran the coast road every other day, read late, was at the Anchor every Friday, and was seldom out of his shed until May. In 1990 he joined Driftwood and wrote "Salt Lanterns", "Grey Radio" and "Cold Hours". In June he joined Pirate Radio, and in September played the Anchor with them. Pirate Radio will go on without him.
- **Stella Shaw:** no notice. The volumes begin in June 1989.
- **Pirate Radio, 1998:**
  > *26 March, by Colin Berry:* Saturday's festival on the quay, Ellie Eden's second, was a washout. Larry Baker, Lisa Myers and Andy Shaw helped. On a better note, Neil Lyons, Oliver Brook and Sean Hale have started a band and call it Pirate Radio. And Olive Shaw, 67, has retired after ten years behind the bar at the Anchor.

  > *25 June, by Toby Tanner:* At Friday's open night John Holmes, 52, played on his own and did well. Oliver Brook did well after him. Pirate Radio have a new member: Andy Shaw, 84, last seen with Driftwood.

  > *24 September:* Pirate Radio played their first night at the Anchor on Friday 18 April to a packed room and were called back for more. Neil Lyons, Oliver Brook, Sean Hale and Andy Shaw.

**Your prediction** (Andy not found on the day he died; the 14th an estimate) is not yet due.

# Round 4 · Monday 9 to Friday 20 November 1998

## Your case file

**Andy Shaw, 85.** Died Wednesday 14 October 1998, at home on the Gully Steps. Papers: the notice of 22 October.

**The hearing:** Friday 11 December at ten. **This round:** four turns, Monday 9 to Friday 20 November. Then round 5 (23 November to 4 December) and round 6 (7 to 11 December, three turns and the hearing).

Standing: 3 of 6. **Check: open,** once this case. Board: no claims filed.

**The questions:** (1) Of what did he die? (2) Since when unwell? (3) Was anyone answerable? (4) Who stood by him and his household? (5) Was there bad blood, did it bear on his death, and did anything settle it? (6) Does the printed record stand?

## Your journal (case 2)

*Records:* D-04′ (22 Oct); C-35′, the piece on Andy (22 Oct); the Pirate Radio pieces of 26 March, 25 June and 24 September; the Pirate Radio poster at the Anchor.

*Statements:* Hannah Tanner, at No. 5, Mon 26 Oct, evening: "The Tuesday before he went. Going down for his walk at half past five, same as ever." · "I couldn't tell you that." · "Down to the shore. Out at half past five, back up about seven. Every evening."

*Seen:* No. 6 shut and dark, 26 Oct. The funeral, 27 Oct, ten present. The Anchor, 27 Oct: Pirate Radio as three, "Salt Radio" by name. A cleared desk at the Courier, 28 Oct. *From case 1:* Andy out of No. 6 at half past five and back at seven, 3 and 4 March 1997, and his light on after.

## Where you are · the Courier's back room · Wednesday 28 October, late morning

Rain on the back window. The 1998 issues are in a box file on the table, the bound volumes on their shelves behind you. Toby types; Ken takes an advertisement over the counter. The rest of the round's days pass. Your next turn can be any time from Monday 9 November.

## What you can do

**Talk, watch, read, wait** as before. **Check** (one act, once this case): name three claims on your board, and you learn only whether all three stand. Filing is free: file claims with their sources before you check them.

Free: walking between places within an act, inspecting, filing, and asking why of an event you have found. Two acts may share a day or time of day, in order. **The coroner's rule:** a claim stands on your own sighting, on someone who was there (or, for anything not done in private, the partner or workmate of someone who was), on a dated record, or on two people who each heard it separately. Anything else is struck. Anyone named is summoned.
