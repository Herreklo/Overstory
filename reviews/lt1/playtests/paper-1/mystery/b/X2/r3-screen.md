## Your turns

**Asked why** (free, before your turns): of Driftwood's ending, of Pirate Radio's start, and of Andy's words with Mary Tanner. To each, the file: "Ask someone who was there."

**1. Monday 26 October, evening · the fleet's berth on the quay.** Oliver Brook coils the hose and steps down onto the stones. He answers all three.

- *"Andy walked past here to the shingle most evenings. When did he stop, and how was he at your first night in September?"*
  "He never stopped. Past here most evenings, right up to the week he died. The first night he played with us, same as ever. Packed room. They called us back for more."
- *"Who wrote 'Salt Radio'? Is it the old 'Salt Lanterns', and whose name goes on it?"*
  "Andy's. He brought it to us in September. A new one."
- *"Who was with him at the end, and who found him?"*
  "He was with us from June, the band: Neil Lyons, Sean Hale and me. Who found him, I couldn't tell you."

He climbs back onto his deck.

**Your prediction** (Pirate Radio would play "Salt Radio" as Andy's, and someone old would know it began as "Salt Lanterns"): Neil named "Salt Radio" as Sean's, and nobody in your hearing spoke of "Salt Lanterns".

**3. Wednesday 28 October, afternoon · the Gully Steps.** Oliver named nobody who found him, so you went to Karen Holmes. She is at the bench in her shed, the door open. Below her, Andy's door is shut. She answers all three.

- *"When did you last see Andy, and how was he this autumn?"*
  "The week he died, in his shed with the door open, as he always was. Same as ever."
- *"Who came up the steps to him: family, a doctor, the band?"*
  "Family? Andy had none. Not since Stella."
- *"He had words with Mary Tanner in August '96. What about, and was it ever settled?"*
  "First I've heard of it."

## What you can do

A round is four turns. Write up to four acts, one a turn, in order, and say for each where you go, whom you see, and on which day and at what time of day.
- **Talk:** visit one person and ask up to three questions; write the questions in the act. Someone guarded answers one question a visit. Someone closed will not talk to you.
- **Watch:** one place for a morning, an afternoon or an evening. Nobody hears of a watch. You see who comes and goes, what happens there, and whose usual place is empty.
- **Read:** the Courier's bound volumes and this year's issues, at the Courier on Market Square, and any notice or poster where it hangs.
- **Wait:** let a day go by.
- **Check** (open now, once this case, as one turn): name three claims on your board. You learn only whether all three stand.
- **Give the account** (the hearing, round 6's fourth turn): write your answers to the six questions in that act, each claim with its source.

Free at any time, and not a turn: walking between places within an act; inspecting a person or a thing (hover shows a name, age, work and willingness); filing a claim on your board with its source; asking why of an event you have found (the file gives a reason only where the record states one).

Your journal keeps every statement word for word: who said it, where and when.
