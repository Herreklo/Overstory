Nothing else in that issue on Andy, Driftwood or Pirate Radio.

**Your prediction** (the March 1995 piece credits "Salt Lanterns" to someone other than Andy, so the obituary is the record that credits the wrong man): the piece gives "Salt Lanterns" to Andy, as the obituary does. The poster for that Friday gives it to "Ash".

**2. Tuesday 24 November, afternoon · the sawmill.** The saws are running. Sarah Lane (21, the sawmill; open) stacks cut boards at the end of the shed; Neil Lyons is at his saw across the floor. Sarah answers all three.

- *"In Driftwood, who wrote 'Salt Lanterns' and 'Grey Radio'?"*
  "Before my time, both. I joined them in the January of '96."
- *"Have you heard Pirate Radio's 'Salt Radio'? Is it new, or an old Driftwood song?"*
  "A new one, so Neil says."
- *"Why did Driftwood split in '96, and what were Andy's words with Mary Tanner that August?"*
  "I couldn't say."

**3. Wednesday 25 November, evening · the fleet's berth on the quay.** The boats are in, the lamps along Harbour Row lit. Oliver Brook is coiling rope on his deck, and steps down. He answers all three.

- *"Sean and Neil both say Sean brought 'Salt Radio' in September. You told me Andy did. Were you there, and are you sure?"*
  "Sean brought it. I was there. Andy brought us 'Grey Radio', back in Driftwood, in '94. We liked that one."
- *"You told me Andy and Mary had words. What were they about?"*
  "He never said what about. In the street, that August. He'd had his operation in the city that month too, and was home inside the week."
- *"Andy missed his next practice, surely. Who went to his door?"*
  "Who went to his door, I couldn't tell you. We went on, the three of us."

**4. Friday 27 November, morning · the Courier.** Three first-hand tellers now give "Salt Radio" to Sean, so you went to Ken Scott. He sees you in the front office. He answers all three.

- *"Who placed Andy's notice, and who told you he had died?"*
  "It ran on the twenty-second. Who brought it in, I couldn't tell you."
- *"Did anyone send word of a cause?"*
  "No. Nothing came to us on what he died of."
- *"Whose is the cleared desk?"*
  "Colin Berry's, that was. He left us in October. Vera Holmes joins us this month."
