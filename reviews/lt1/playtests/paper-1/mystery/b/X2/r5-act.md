# Round 5

## What I remember

- **Karen Holmes:** in her seventies, retired, works at a bench in her shed one house up from Andy. Plays cards at the Anchor on Tuesdays. Wouldn't say whether she's kin to Mark. Says "same as ever" about everyone and knows nothing.

## Thinking aloud
So far: Oliver says Andy wrote it; Sean and Neil say Sean did, and Sean has the stake. The obituary leaves "Salt Radio" off Andy's list, and its source for that list is Toby's own 1995 piece. That piece is the hinge. I'll also talk to Sarah Lane at the sawmill: she was in Driftwood in '96 and isn't in Pirate Radio. Then I'll put the contradiction to Oliver's face. This time I'll spend the check, because one shaky claim next to two safe ones is really a test of the shaky one.

## Prediction
The March '95 piece credits "Salt Lanterns" to someone other than Andy, probably Edward Baker, who has since dropped off the line-up. So the obituary is the record that credits the wrong man.
