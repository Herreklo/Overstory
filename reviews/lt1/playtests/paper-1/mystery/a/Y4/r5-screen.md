**Turn 1 · The Anchor, Harbour Row · Monday 23 November, evening**

A quiet Monday. Agnes Tanner is behind the bar. *Agnes Tanner, 31, bar staff, the Anchor; open.*

You: "What did you see of Andy Shaw's quarrels in the Anchor in June?"
Agnes: "Both of them, from behind this bar, the one week in June. Alice Tanner had it out with him first, and then Sarah Lane shouted at him in front of the whole room."

You: "What was each one about?"
Agnes: "I couldn't say."

You: "Where would I find the two who quarrelled with him?"
Agnes: "The sawmill, the both of them."

**Your prediction.** Agnes Tanner saw both quarrels, and she knows where the two of them work: the sawmill. What either was about, she couldn't say.

**Turn 2 · The sawmill · Tuesday 24 November, the dinner break**

The saws stop at twelve. Neil Lyons sits on a stack of planks in the yard with a tin and a flask. *Neil Lyons, 50, the sawmill; open.*

You: "When did you last see Andy Shaw, and how did he seem?"
Neil: "The night we played the Anchor, the eighteenth of September. Thin, and tired by the end, but he played every song."

You: "Who looked after him at home in his last weeks?"
Neil: "He lived on his own. I only ever saw him at the band."

You: "Who found him, and when?"
Neil: "I heard on the Thursday, here, at dinner."

He screws the top back on the flask when the saws start again.

**Turn 3 · The sawmill gate · Wednesday 25 November, evening**

Agnes sent you to the sawmill, so you wait at the gate for the shift to come off at six. John Holmes, the foreman, locks the shed behind the last of them. Sarah Lane comes out alone, with her collar up. *Sarah Lane, 21, the sawmill; open.*

You: "When did you last see Andy Shaw, and how did he seem?"
Sarah: "In June, at the Anchor, the night I shouted at him. Thin as anything. He was eighty-four."

You: "What were you and he at odds over in June?"
Sarah: "Pirate Radio wanted someone, and they took him, not me. I'd played with Oliver in Driftwood. I thought that place was mine, and I said so, loud."

You: "Did anything settle it between you?"
Sarah: "No. He took it, and kept his head down."

She goes off up the road towards the town.

**Turn 4 · The Anchor, Harbour Row · Friday 27 November, evening**

There is no band tonight. The corner under the poster is set with tables, and nobody plays. The Pirate Radio poster is still by the door. "Letting Go on a Monday" is not played, and nobody stands where Andy stood.

Behind the bar are Evan Newman, Peter Scott and Agnes Tanner. By the fire sit Edward Baker, Mark Holmes, Leo Lyons, Olive Shaw and Ben Reed. Along the bar stand Trevor Lane, Oliver Brook, Neil Lyons, John Holmes, Gary Myers and Colin Berry, and Eddie Lane in his garage overalls. At the tables are Hannah Tanner; Ivan Tanner and Molly Scott; Tara Myers and Sam Brook; Terry Sutton and Steve Baker; Toby Waller and Sylvia Waller; Harry Myers, Tess Lane and Lily Lyons; Sally Baker, Ellie Eden, Amelia Tanner and Ellen Vaughan. Lynn Newman is by the window.

Eddie Lane has his two pints at the bar and is gone before eight. Edward Baker stays at the fire all evening. Alice Tanner and Sarah Lane are not in. Trevor Lane has come in every Friday this month and never on a Friday before; tonight he stands at the bar beside Oliver Brook. Nobody who keeps a Friday here is missing.

Late on, Toby Waller says to Sylvia Waller across their table: "Andy gave up his swim at New Year in '90. Twenty-odd years he'd been off the harbour steps every morning, and then never again. I said at the time he'd be poorly."

Nothing else you hear is about him.
