# Round 6

## Marks
- Turn 5.1 (Agnes): bored. I got a first-hand sighting of both quarrels and the sawmill, but "I couldn't say" what each was about, when she told Peter Scott aloud at the wake that it was over his place in the band. Surprise: cheat — she had already said it, so the wall is false.
- Turn 5.2 (Neil): engaged. "He lived on his own. I only ever saw him at the band." That contradicts Olive's "the band, I'd think" and fits Eve's "stopped being there". Nobody has told me who found him. Surprise: none.
- Turn 5.3 (Sarah): engaged. The first person to give a plain reason and own her shout: she thought the place was hers, and "No" to anything settling it. It answers half of question 5. Surprise: delight.
- Turn 5.4 (the Friday watch): curious. Almost nothing about him, but two changes of habit: Trevor Lane in every Friday this month and never before, and Toby Waller's "gave up his swim at New Year in '90". Surprise: delight.

## Pull
7 — I now have a shape for four of the six questions and want to test it. The cause and who found him are still blank, and I'm tired of walls.

## Thinking aloud
Free first. File: (8) Agnes Tanner saw both quarrels in June from behind the bar, her own sighting. (9) Sarah Lane shouted at him because Pirate Radio took him, not her, and nothing settled it, her own statement. (10) He lived on his own, and Neil only saw him at the band, Neil Lyons, his own statement. (11) He played every song on 18 September, thin and tired by the end, Neil Lyons, his own sighting. (12) Andy gave up his harbour swim at New Year 1990, Toby Waller, his own remark, overheard 27 November. Ask why of: the swim ending in January 1990; Trevor Lane's Fridays since November.

The obituary says he swam "until illness kept him from the water". The Courier and Oliver put his illness at spring 1996. A swim that ended in 1990 cannot have ended for an illness of 1996. That is the second fault in the printed record, after the song credit. I am not guessing why he stopped: that is Toby Waller's to say.

Grief shows as changed habits. Tara Myers stopped staying for her pints after Mary died. This time it is Trevor Lane, who now comes on Fridays and stands by Oliver Brook. He sat at the funeral table too. I'll ask him. Alice Tanner I'm skipping on purpose; I have Sarah's side and the Check costs a turn.

## Prediction
Trevor Lane will be open, say he knew Andy from the choir or the band, and say he comes on Fridays for the music, probably "Letting Go on a Monday". He will not know who found Andy. Why: his habit started after the death, and the poster gives that song to nobody.

- Who wrote "Letting Go on a Monday", "March Light" and "No Ferry Tonight".
- How Olive Shaw and Trevor Lane were tied to him.
- What Mary Tanner died of. Still nothing, after a whole case.
