## Thinking aloud

(6) "Edward Baker wrote 'The Fence at the Clinic'" — Edward Baker, his own statement, 10 Nov. Ask why of: Alice Tanner's and Sarah Lane's quarrels with Andy in June 1998; Eddie Lane leaving the wake after "The Fence at the Clinic"; the funeral on 27 October, thirteen days after the death; Olive Shaw leaving the bar in March 1998.

Three things bother me. In June 1998 Pirate Radio takes him on ("they genuinely liked each other") and the same month two people shout at him over his place in a band. Eve says people "stopped being there", and Hannah only "heard on the Thursday he'd gone": he lay alone from Tuesday night, and nobody has told me who found him. And "Letting Go on a Monday" sits on the poster with no name, a twin of Peter Lane's "Coming Home on a Sunday". I can't prove anything from the titles. I'll keep the Check for round 6: Edward's claim with my two firmest ones (Hannah's sightings), so a "no" means Edward.

## Turns
1. Talk to Agnes Tanner, the Anchor, Monday 23 November, evening. (a) What did you see of Andy Shaw's quarrels in the Anchor in June? (b) What was each one about? (c) Where would I find the two who quarrelled with him? — Expect: both seen, a reason that is about the band and not about his health, and an address.
2. Talk to Neil Lyons, Tuesday 24 November: the sawmill at its dinner break, else the Anchor that evening. (a) When did you last see Andy Shaw, and how did he seem? (b) Who looked after him at home in his last weeks? (c) Who found him, and when? — Expect: he knows, since he played with him all summer, and names a neighbour or a bandmate.
3. If Agnes tells me where to find Sarah Lane: talk to her there, Wednesday 25 November, evening. (a) When did you last see Andy Shaw, and how did he seem? (b) What were you and he at odds over in June? (c) Did anything settle it between you? Otherwise: talk to Toby Tanner, the Courier, Wednesday 25 November, morning. (a) Who brought in the notice of the 22nd? (b) When did you last see Andy Shaw on the shore, and how did he walk? (c) Who, if anyone, did you see him walk with this last year? — Expect: Sarah is short but plain; Toby, who knew who brought Mary's notice, knows this one too.
4. Watch the Anchor, Friday 27 November, evening (a Pirate Radio night, if they still play). I look at who stands in Andy's place, how "Letting Go on a Monday" is introduced and by whom, who is there and who is not (Edward, Eddie Lane, Alice, Sarah), and what is said of him. — Expect: they play on without him and one song's credit is said aloud differently from the poster.
