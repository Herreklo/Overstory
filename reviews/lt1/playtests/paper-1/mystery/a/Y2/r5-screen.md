# Round 5

## Your turns

**1. Monday 23 November, morning, the Gully Steps. Hannah Tanner.**

Hannah Tanner (83, retired; open) is at the bench in her shed, the door open to the steps. She puts down what she is mending.

You ask when she last saw Andy, and how he was.
"The Tuesday evening, the night before. Going down the steps for his walk, same as every night, a hand on the rail." She looks across at his door. "He'd been at his practising every night since the summer. I could hear him through the wall. Getting ready for that band, I took it."

You ask who found him on the 14th, and who came to the door after.
"His lamp never came on, the Wednesday night. He always read late."

You ask who visited him in his last weeks.
"I couldn't say."

*Your prediction about Hannah Tanner was due.* She saw him go down the steps for his walk on the evening before he died. She named nobody who found him, and said nothing of an illness.

**2. Tuesday 24 November, evening, the Anchor. Agnes Tanner.**

Cards night again, at the same tables. Agnes Tanner (31, bar staff at the Anchor; open) dries glasses at the end of the bar.

You ask what Alice Tanner and Sarah Lane said to Andy over his place, and when.
"June, the one week. Alice Tanner had it out with him first, and then Sarah Lane shouted at him in front of everyone."

You ask how he looked on the 18th, and whether he left with anyone.
"I couldn't say."

You ask whether she saw him in here after that.
"No. Not after the eighteenth."

**3. Thursday 26 November, afternoon, Harbour Row. Olive Shaw.**

Nobody had named anyone with Andy at the end, so you go to Olive Shaw's house on Harbour Row. Olive Shaw (68, retired; open) opens the door.

You ask whether she is Andy's kin. She lets that one go by.

You ask what she saw of his health, and when.
"I couldn't say."

**4. Friday 27 November, morning, the Courier. Toby Tanner.**

Ken Scott is at the front desk, and Vera Holmes (50, the Courier; open) is at the layout table. Toby Tanner (41, reporter; open) looks up from his typewriter.

You ask who told him Andy died "after a long illness".
"The paper had him unwell from the spring of '96. Colin wrote that, when Driftwood split."

You ask where "until illness kept him from the water" came from.
"I swam with him. He stopped coming in at the start of '90."

You ask when he last saw Andy, and how he looked.
"I couldn't say."

Monday 30 November to Friday 4 December go by. Your four turns are spent.
