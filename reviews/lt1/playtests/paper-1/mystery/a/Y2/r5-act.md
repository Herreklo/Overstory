# Round 5

## From memory
- **Oliver Brook:** skipper, now 42. Friendly about Mary, but he contradicted the Courier on her cards without noticing. In Driftwood and now Pirate Radio. On Andy he went quiet: said Andy was at the Anchor "the whole night" on the 18th, let slip "the summer he'd had over that place in the band", then "couldn't say" who found him. He is steering round Andy's last month.
- **Toby Tanner:** reporter at the Courier, 41, the third shore walker. Wrote both obituaries and the "room went quiet" piece. Evasive: "I wrote her up, that's all". The strokes were "known at the time" and nothing else was sourced. "Illness kept him from the water" in Andy's piece has no source I can find.
- **Andy Shaw:** died 14 October 1998, 85, at home on the Gully Steps. Widower (Stella, 1978). Swam the harbour until New Year 1990, when he just stopped. Walked the shore "nothing slow" in March 1997. Joined Driftwood late, then Pirate Radio at 84, and played the 18th. Two women fought over his place in the band. Nobody at the clinic saw him after he died.

## Thinking aloud
Hannah is the obvious next door. She sits across the steps from Andy and she is open, so I get all three questions. Agnes saw the quarrel and Neil only pointed at her, so I need Agnes herself. Neil's account is hearsay for the board until someone who saw it says it. The paper's cause rests on nothing so far, but I can't claim it is false: Toby Waller saw nothing up to 1996, and the coroner said last time that what was printed isn't what was true or untrue. I need the last month. And I won't name anyone. Sarah shouting in a bar is not a death.

I'll use the check in round 6, on whichever three claims my account leans on, once I know which are firsthand.

## Prediction
Hannah Tanner will say Andy was in and out until about the end of September, then stopped coming down the steps. She may be the one who found him, or she saw who did. She will not call it a long illness.

## Turns
1. Monday 23 November, morning, the Gully Steps. Talk to Hannah Tanner in her shed. (a) When did you last see Andy, and how was he? (b) Who found him on the 14th, and who came to the door after? (c) Who visited him in his last weeks? — Expect: grief and a name, maybe the one who found him.
2. Tuesday 24 November, evening, the Anchor. Talk to Agnes Tanner behind the bar. (a) What did Alice Tanner and Sarah Lane say to Andy over his place, and when? (b) How did he look on the 18th, and did he leave with anyone? (c) Did you see him in here after that? If guarded, (b) only. — Expect: a firsthand quarrel and a date.
3. Thursday 26 November, afternoon. Whoever Hannah or Agnes names as with Andy at the end. If none, Olive Shaw: "Are you his kin? What did you see of his health, and when?" — Expect: a firsthand date for the illness, or a flat no.
4. Friday 27 November, morning, the Courier. Talk to Toby Tanner. (a) Who told you he died "after a long illness"? (b) Where did "until illness kept him from the water" come from? (c) When did you last see him, and how did he look? If guarded, (a) only. — Expect: a source or a shrug.
