## Pull

## Thinking aloud
Everyone who actually saw Andy saw a man who was well: Hannah (walking, practising, a hand on the rail), Toby Waller (nothing ill up to April '96), Neil (played the whole night), my own eyes (unaided on the shingle, March 1997). Nobody firsthand saw him ill. The only "illness" is the paper's, and its two dates disagree: the notice has illness ending his swimming (New Year '90), Toby Tanner has the paper dating it from spring '96. So Q6 is my strongest question. Q2 I can only answer by what witnesses saw. Q1 and Q3 stay "?" unless someone says who found him. I'll name nobody. Alice and Sarah shouting in a bar goes under Q5 and no further.

## Prediction
Colin Berry's spring 1996 piece will give "ill health" as why Andy left Driftwood, with no named source and no doctor, weeks after Toby Waller retired. The real reason will be a falling-out in the band, and the paper has repeated its cover line since.
