## Pull
6 — The band quarrel and the song credits are real hooks, and I am a turn from a hearing with a check in hand. But still no face for me, no one at my side, and nothing I can lose except standing.

## Thinking aloud
Two obituaries by the same hand, Toby Tanner's, and both have a claim someone else contradicts: Mary's cards, Andy's illness. Toby Waller says Andy stopped swimming at New Year 1990 and gives no reason. Yet Andy walked the shingle "nothing slow" in 1997 and, I'm told, sat in at the Anchor on 18 September. So "a long illness" may be the paper's phrase, as "suddenly" was. I still don't know what he died of, or who found him, and Oliver is avoiding both. The band is the live thread: Oliver says Andy fought over his place, "Letting Go on a Monday" is unattributed, and the store page promised a song credited to the wrong man. I will not accuse anyone. This time I'll use all three questions on open people, ask what they did and saw, and ask whether they saw the body themselves, since "what was entered" got struck last time.

## Prediction
Whoever answers at Andy's door on the Gully Steps will be guarded and take the one question. They will resent the paper's "long illness" and say he was up and singing until days before.

## Needs
- Who lived with Andy, and which door on the Gully Steps.
- Who attended or certified his death; whether I can ask the coroner's office for the certificate.
- How the people on the shore relate to anyone. The watch gives names, not ties.
- Whether new facts can reopen Mary's case.

## Turns
1. Monday 9 November, afternoon, the Gully Steps, Andy's house. Talk to whoever answers (Olive Shaw, if she is his kin). If open: (a) who found him on the 14th, and how was he? (b) who attended him, and did they tell you what he died of? (c) when did you first see he was ill, and what did you see? If guarded, only (a). — Expect: grief, a name.
2. Tuesday 10 November, evening, the Anchor. Talk to Neil Lyons, else Sean Hale. (a) What was the trouble this summer over Andy's place in Pirate Radio? (b) Who wrote "Letting Go on a Monday"? (c) On 18 September, did Andy play, and how did he look? If guarded, only (a). — Expect: a name for the quarrel and a song credit.
3. Thursday 12 November, morning, Toby Waller's house. (a) Why did Andy stop swimming at New Year 1990: was he ill, did you treat him? (b) Did you see him ill again before you retired? (c) Who wrote "The Fence at the Clinic" and what is it about? — Expect: a diagnosis or a denial, and a songwriter.
4. Friday 13 November, afternoon. Whoever turns 1 to 3 name as having attended or found Andy (a doctor, Olive Shaw, John Holmes), I talk to them: "did you see him yourself, and what did you see?" If no one is named, the clinic: Thea Waller, "did you examine Andy after he died, and who certified?"
