## Thinking aloud
Lessons from last time. Hearsay means I need firsthand: what someone saw or did, never what was "entered". And every claim needs a named source. Now Andy. He walked the shingle at 83 and "nothing slow" about him. Yet the notice says "after a long illness", and he was billed at the Anchor on 18 September, under a month before he died. Either the illness is short and the notice is house phrasing (like "suddenly" for Mary and Ellie), or he played sick. Two of the three shore walkers are now dead within twenty months, Mary and Andy. Toby Tanner is the third. That may be nothing, so I'll test it, not believe it. "Letting Go on a Monday" also nags: Mary died on a Monday. I'll treat that as a coincidence until a person says otherwise.
