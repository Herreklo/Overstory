# Round 4

## Your turns

**1. Monday 9 November, afternoon, the Gully Steps. Andy Shaw's house.**

Andy's door is above the half-landing. You knock, and knock again. Nobody answers, and the windows are dark. Across the steps a shed door stands open, and an old woman is at the bench inside: Hannah Tanner (83, retired; open).

*Your prediction about whoever answered Andy's door was due.* Nobody answered.

**2. Tuesday 10 November, evening, the Anchor.**

Cards night. Behind the bar: Evan Newman (58, manager), Peter Scott (46) and Agnes Tanner (31). The Pirate Radio poster is up by the door, with Andy's name on it. At the card tables: Edward Baker, Mark Holmes, Leo Lyons and Olive Shaw nearest the bar; Neil Lyons, Eve Vaughan, Sally Baker and Ellie Eden; Toby Waller, Grace Waller, Mona Sayer and Terry Sutton; Hannah Tanner, Ivan Tanner, Molly Scott and Tara Myers; John Holmes, Steve Baker, Colin Berry and Leo Holmes. At the bar with a pint: Oliver Brook, Gary Myers, Eddie Lane and Lynn Newman.

Neil Lyons (50, the sawmill; open) gets up from his table between hands and takes you to the end of the bar.

You ask what the trouble was this summer over Andy's place in Pirate Radio.
"Alice Tanner and Sarah Lane both wanted it. When it went to Andy, each of them went at him over it, in here, in front of everyone. Sarah shouted." He nods at the bar. "Agnes was on. She saw the lot."

You ask who wrote "Letting Go on a Monday".
"Sean's. He brought it to us in September. It never came to much."

You ask whether Andy played on the 18th, and how he looked.
"He played. The whole night."

He goes back to his hand.

**3. Thursday 12 November, morning, Toby Waller's house.**

Toby Waller (69, retired; open) is back from the harbour steps, his hair still wet.

You ask why Andy stopped swimming at New Year 1990, whether he was ill, and whether Toby treated him.
"Why, I couldn't tell you. He never came to me for anything that winter."

You ask whether he saw Andy ill before he retired.
"Not that I saw, up to my finishing at the clinic in April '96."

You ask who wrote "The Fence at the Clinic", and what it is about.
"Andy's. It had them called back twice at the Anchor."

**4. Friday 13 November, afternoon, the clinic. Thea Waller.**

Nobody had named anyone who found or attended Andy, so you go to the clinic. Thea Waller (41, head of the clinic; open) sees you in the back office. Eve Vaughan (54), Sally Baker (47), Trevor Myers (42) and Amelia Tanner (28) are at work.

You ask whether she examined Andy after he died, and who certified.
"No. Nobody here saw him after. What I know is what the paper said: a long illness."

Monday 16 to Friday 20 November go by. Your four turns are spent.
