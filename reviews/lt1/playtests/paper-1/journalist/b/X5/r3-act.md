# Round 3

## Marks
- Turn 2.1: bored — Lily trusts me 3 of 3, and it bought three not-for-print non-answers and no facts. "Claims, the till: it's the same money" is the only line with any grain. Surprise: none.
- Turn 2.2: bored — a whole turn on a tide table. It was my own fallback, but nothing came of it. Surprise: none.
- Turn 2.3: engaged — the first real sting. A rival is out there, and two of the eight names I walked past on the shore were its stories. Surprise: cheat — the county ran "found out" in one bare line with no source shown, which is the very thing my rules wouldn't let me do on the 13th, and it won.
- Turn 2.4: would quit — the most careful page I could make (a record plus the subject's refusal) cost three points of patience and dropped sales below 850. Ken's line told me why, which is fair, but trust has now sat on 6 through a thin page and a sourced one. I can't see what moves it. Surprise: none.

## Pull
6 — at last there are stakes. Patience is at 2, so one more bad week and I'm out. But two careful editions earned zero trust, and I suspect that meter is a locked door.

## Thinking aloud
What am I trying to win? Survive first: with patience at 2, I can't be second again. Trust at 7 needs something in one week, and I've no idea what moves it. My guess is first, named and for print. What do I risk? Being scooped twice, and Ken's rule if Sutton names Jonah or Sylvia. Lesson learned: I spent two weeks on the tip book while the town's stories were walking the shore. The county gave each of its three items one line and no voices. Depth is what I can still be first on. Sutton said "not this week" on the 8th. Now that he's been printed twice, he may want his side out. And I've stopped trusting Lily: she rang the tip in the morning after I asked her about him, and she's the one who carried the office.

Free: I hover Gary Hughes, Greta Brook, John Holmes, Kate Holmes and Sally Baker. I ask why of three public events I've now found in a record: Sutton found out over the claims, Greta Brook let go, and Gary Hughes passed over. I ask Ken: "Do empty inside pages cost me too?"

## Prediction
Sutton talks now, for print: he's paying it back and staying until the festival. Lily ends up in his chair within the year.

## Needs
- What moves readers' trust. Two editions, no movement.
- What spends patience: being second, empty inside pages, or both.
- When a crowd is listed, one detail per person, so I can tell a lead from scenery.
- Who the county's correspondent here is.

## Turns
1. **Thu 21 June, morning, council office: Terry Sutton in his room, for print.** (a) "You said 'not this week'. It's in two papers now. What's your side: what were the claims, and is the money being repaid?" (b) "Are you still chief officer, and who covers your work?" (c) "Is there a festival this December, and will you run it?" — Expect: his side and a sum, or a second refusal I can print as one.
2. **Thu 21 June, evening, the shore: Gary Hughes, for print.** (a) "Who made the call on head mechanic, and what reason were you given?" (b) "Sally Baker came two months after you. Did you know the job was hers before she came?" (c) "Are you staying at the garage?" If he won't speak for print, I ask Greta Brook the same of the sawmill instead. — Expect: bitterness, on record, and a name.
3. **Tue 26 June, morning: the other side.** If Gary spoke, I go to the person he named as deciding, or else to Sally Baker at the forecourt: how was it chosen, and what do they say to his account? If Greta spoke instead, I go to John Holmes at the sawmill yard. — Expect: a reply that makes it fair, and doesn't make it go away.
4. **Edition, Wed 27 June.**
   - **Front:** Sutton's own account, if it's for print, marked against the county record and our 21 June front. Otherwise, the garage or sawmill story with both sides.
   - **Inside 1:** whichever of those two isn't on the front.
   - **Inside 2:** the festival's future from Sutton's (c), if it's for print.
   - If Ken has the county ringed on Monday with any of this in it, I lead with what the county lacks: our named voices.
   - Anything naming Jonah, Sylvia or a Waller goes to Ken on Tuesday afternoon. Nothing off the record goes in, and I don't pad from the county.
