## Turn 2 · Thursday 21 June, evening · the shore, Gary Hughes

The shore walkers are out as last week. Hover: **Gary Hughes,** 38, the garage. **Greta Brook,** 66, no work. **Kate Holmes,** 69, retired. Gary walks alone with a face like thunder, and stops when you ask.

> **Gary Hughes, the shore, Thu 21 June, 6.30 p.m.** All *for print*.
> (a) Who made the call, what reason: "Sally Baker got it. I didn't."
> (b) Did you know the job was hers before she came: "I came in February. They'd been fifteen months short-handed. What anyone had in mind for the job, I wouldn't know."
> (c) Are you staying at the garage: "I wouldn't know."

He named nobody as deciding.

## Turn 3 · Tuesday 26 June, morning · the garage forecourt, Sally Baker

You said Sutton would talk for print, say he is paying it back, and stay until the festival, and that Lily would be in his chair within the year. He talked for print. He said nothing about paying anything back, and that he has not started on another festival. Lily's part waits on the year.

# Round 4 · Thursday 23 May 1991 · the Courier's back room, the bound volume

**The edition of 28 June sold 1,003.** "A thousand and three," Ken said that week, and wrote it in the book twice as large. Your readers' trust stood at 7 by the month's end, and on Monday 2 July Ken put you on the staff for good.

Eleven months on, you open last June's volume. Your three fronts are there as printed, with their marks, beside Toby's garage piece and Susie's harbour day. **No correction** has been printed against any of your pages.

**What the volume added after June, in one-line notices:** Maisie Myers bought the big trip abroad, and so did Toby Tanner (August). Oliver Brook and Peter Lane started a band, Driftwood; Andy Shaw joined (December). Ivan Baker born to Sally and Terry Baker (December). Peter Lane will step down as owner at the Old Bank Café next year (March). **Terry Sutton will step down as chief officer at the council office next year** (April). **Gary Hughes started work at the sawmill** (May). We shall miss her at the desk by the window. **Driftwood.** Oliver Brook, who skippers for the harbour fleet, and Peter Lane of the Old Bank Café have started a band. ## What you can do

Each chase turn is one act: **talk** (one person, up to three questions; each answer for print or not), **watch** (one place for a morning, an afternoon or an evening), **read** (the bound volumes, 1989 to this year; the county paper at the co-op on Saturdays; any notice board), or **wait** (let a day go by).

The edition, on Wednesday, is your fourth turn: a front page (500 words) and two inside stories (250 words each), written or described, every claim marked (saw it, a witness saw it, heard from someone named, record). You may soften, hold or spike a story, and write the edition as a plan with "if" in it.

Free at any time: walking between places within a turn; inspecting a person or a thing (hover shows a name, age and work); noting a tip; asking why of a public event you have found.
