Holding is free, so it was never a choice. Meanwhile the paper has printed Lily's bid and nobody else's, three weeks running. That unfairness is now bigger than the missing line about Ken. So this week I break the deadlock. I'll try a line that discloses without naming him. If he refuses that too, I run it his way. It's the one branch I haven't tested, and it's the first decision I think could cost me something. If it costs nothing as well, this game has rules but no ethics.
