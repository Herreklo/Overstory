# Round 2: what happened

## Turn 1 · Thursday 14 June, afternoon · the council office, Terry Sutton

Lily Lyons is at the counter; Sylvia Waller and Jonah Scott are at their desks. Terry Sutton sees you in his room.

Hover: **Terry Sutton,** 63, chief officer, the council office.

> **Terry Sutton, his room, Thu 14 June, 14.30.**
> (a) Who pays for the festival, and who signs the claims: "I've nothing to say about that." *No comment.*
> (b) Has anyone at the office questioned his claims this year: he looks at you for a while and says nothing. *No comment.*
> (c) Would he say it for print, under his name: "There was a mistake in the claims. That's all I'll say." *For print.*

## Turn 2 · Friday 15 June, evening · the Anchor

The Friday room is full. Evan Newman and Olive Shaw are behind the bar; Peter Scott and Sean Hale carry glasses. Hover gives the regulars: Terry Sutton, Toby Waller, Sylvia Waller, Sally Baker, Sam Brook, Neil Lyons, Mark Holmes, Vera Holmes, Leo Lyons, Larry Baker and Oliver Brook. John Holmes has his two pints at the end of the bar. Maisie Myers is at a table by the door.

At the bar, Toby Waller (hover: 61, the clinic) says to the people round him, loud enough to carry:

> "They're making a lot of a few pounds on Terry's claims. Thirty-odd years he's given that office. They'd hang a man for less up there."

> **Maisie Myers, the Anchor, Fri 15 June, 21.15.**
> (a) Was Greta's work poor, and what did she do at the mill: "I'll not say. Ask John." *No comment.*
> (b) Did something happen to her this year: "She was diagnosed with cancer in February." *For print.*
> (c) How the yard took last week's paper: "Nobody said much. Greta had her say to John's face before she went; snapped at him in the yard. She'd not been herself for months." *Not for print: he's my foreman.*

## Turn 3 · Monday 18 June · the Courier, then the council office

**At nine** Ken has Saturday's county paper open on his desk, at the page for the towns:

> **Garage gets new head mechanic.** Sally Baker, 39, who came to the town in April, is the garage's new head mechanic. Gary Hughes, who came in February, was passed over for the job. Mrs Baker had the stronger record of the two, the paper understands.

"We had Sally Baker's promotion," Ken says, "and not a word on Gary Hughes." He folds it and puts it on the spike.

Terry gave you a line for print, so your plan's first branch ran. **The council office, ten o'clock.** Terry's door is shut. Jonah Scott is at his desk.

Hover: **Jonah Scott,** 41, the council office.

> **Jonah Scott, his desk, Mon 18 June, 10.10.**
> (a) Is there a question over Terry's claims: "Not here." *No comment.*
> (b) What the claims are for: "I couldn't tell you what's in them." *Not for print: he's my chief officer.*
> (c) Would he say it under his name: "No. Not about Terry. He's given that office thirty-odd years." *Not for print.*

## Turn 4 · the edition of Thursday 21 June

Jonah is not on record, so the council story is held. Your front is Greta, with Maisie's answer for print beside John's. Maisie gave nothing for print on the yard and you read no notice board, so Susie filled inside 2 with notices.

Inside 1 named Toby Waller and Jonah Scott among Terry's helpers. Ken read it on Wednesday morning: "Take Toby Waller and my brother out of that." Your plan said nothing for Ken on inside 1, so it ran without them.

> **Front · Greta Brook ill since February**
> - Greta Brook, let go from the sawmill by the foreman, John Holmes, was diagnosed with cancer in February. *Heard from Maisie Myers, for print.*
> - John Holmes: "Her work had been poor for months. Gone to pieces, if you want it plain. I'd no choice." *Heard from John Holmes, for print (7 June).*
> - Greta Brook would give no reason. "He let me go." *Heard from Greta Brook, for print (7 June).*
>
> **Inside 1 · Eleven festivals on the quay**
> - Terry Sutton, 63, of the council office, has put on eleven festivals on the quay since 1963. *Record (C-03).*
> - December's was the best night the town had had in years. *Record (C-03).*
> - His helpers last year included Larry Baker, Kate Holmes, Molly Scott and Mona Sayer; Kate Holmes was up all night before it finishing her part. *Record (C-03).*

## Your prediction

You said Terry wouldn't raise the claims himself, would guess who was behind your questions, and that Jonah would turn out to want his chair. Terry didn't raise them; asked for print, he gave you one line. He named nobody.

## What you can do

A chase turn is one act: **talk** (one person, up to three questions, each answer for print or not), **watch** (a place for a part of the day), **read** (the volumes, the county paper, a notice board) or **wait** (a day). The edition is the fourth turn: a front (500 words) and two inside (250 each), each claim marked saw it, a witness saw it, heard from someone named, or record; soften, hold or spike; plan with "if". Free: walking within a turn, hover, noting a tip, asking why of a public event you have found.
