# Round 3: what happened

## Turn 1 · Thursday 21 June, afternoon · the council notice board

The board by the counter has the office hours and nothing else.

## Turn 2 · Thursday 21 June, evening · the shore

A still, grey evening. Hover gives the walkers: Susie Lyons, Lily Lyons, Sam Brook, Sid Tanner, Grace Waller, Una Waller, Kate Holmes, and Gary Hughes at the far end. Greta Brook comes back along the water's edge.

> **Greta Brook, the shore, Thu 21 June, 20.40.**
> (a) Was printing her illness unasked wrong: "It's in the paper now. Nothing to be done about it." *For print.*
> (b) Did John Holmes know she was ill: "What John knew is John's business. He let me go." *For print.*
> (c) Anything in her own words: she looks out at the water for a while. "John and I didn't see eye to eye. That's all." *For print.*

## Turn 3 · Friday 22 June, afternoon · the garage forecourt

No record, and Greta said nothing of what John knew, so your plan's last branch ran. Sally Baker is at the bench. Gary Hughes (hover: 38, the garage) is under a bonnet with a face like thunder. You named no questions, so you asked what happened, why, and whether he'd put his name to it.

> **Gary Hughes, the forecourt, Fri 22 June, 15.20.**
> (a) "I'd been doing the better work. Make of it what you like. Your paper had her job in it, and not a word on me till the county paper had it." *For print.*
> (b) "She had the stronger record, they said. Two months she'd been here. When I came in February they'd been short-handed fifteen months." *Not for print: she works with me.*
> (c) "Put my name to it. I'd been doing the better work." *For print.*

## Turn 4 · the edition of Thursday 28 June

No record on the claims, so the council story stays held. The front is Greta in her own words, with no reply from John Holmes; inside 1 is Gary Hughes; Susie filled inside 2 with notices. Ken reads the garage piece on Wednesday. "Fine," he says, and sends it down.

> **Front · "John and I didn't see eye to eye"**
> - Greta Brook, 66, let go from the sawmill this month, in her own words: her three answers above. *Heard from Greta Brook, for print.*
>
> **Inside 1 · Passed over at the garage**
> - Gary Hughes, 38, who came to the garage in February, was passed over when Sally Baker was made head mechanic on 9 June. *Record (the Courier, 14 June; the county paper, 16 June).*
> - "I'd been doing the better work. Make of it what you like." *Heard from Gary Hughes, for print.*

## Your prediction

You said Greta would say the mill never knew she was ill, so Holmes didn't. She said nothing of her illness, or of what John knew.

# Round 4 · Thursday 23 May 1991 · the Courier's back room, the bound volume

**The edition of 28 June sold 947.** Trust was 8 at the month's end, and Ken put you on the staff for good.

Eleven months on, last June's volume has your five pieces as printed, with their marks, beside Toby's garage piece and Susie's harbour day. **No correction** stands against them.

**What the volume added after June:** Maisie Myers bought the big trip abroad, and so did Toby Tanner (August). Oliver Brook and Peter Lane started a band, Driftwood; Andy Shaw, 77, joined (December). Ivan Baker was born to Sally and Terry Baker (December). Susie Lyons retired after five years at the Courier (January). Thea Waller and Colin Berry, both of the clinic, were married (February). Peter Lane will step down as owner at the Old Bank Café next year (March). **Terry Sutton will step down as chief officer at the council office next year** (April). **Gary Hughes started work at the sawmill** (May). Today Toby has **Driftwood's first night**: the Anchor on Friday, and "Coming Home on a Sunday", Peter Lane's first song, went down best.

**What you heard in the year** (your notebook):

> **Terry Baker, the clinic, July:** "Gary Hughes has walked out of the garage. Going to start again somewhere else." *For print.*
> **Hannah Tanner, November:** "John Holmes and I are on speaking terms again." *For print.*
> **Terry Sutton, December:** "I mean to put on a summer festival on the quay." *For print.*
> **Molly Scott, January:** "Ivan's had a heart attack." *Not for print: he's my husband.*
> **Greta Brook, January:** "I've come through it. Fifteen months of bad terms with John Holmes, being told how to do everything, and no one close. It's behind me." *For print.*
> **Sylvia Waller, February:** "Lily dragged up what Terry did, in front of everyone at the office." *Not for print: they're both in my office.*
> **Peter Scott, behind the Anchor's bar, April:** "Colin Berry started on Thea about her family, the room listening. 'You always', 'you never'. She said nothing." *For print.*

**Seen at the Courier:** from August Susie was looking for work elsewhere; in September Toby let it be known he wants to be editor too. Susie's desk by the window is empty.
