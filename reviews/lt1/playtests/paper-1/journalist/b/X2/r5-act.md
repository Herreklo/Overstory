# Round 5

## Marks
- Turn 4.1: bored. Lily gave me no comment, no comment, then "No. Not about Terry. He's my chief officer." That's Jonah's answer from last June almost word for word. I walked the shore for the same wall again. Surprise: cheat, the echo.
- Turn 4.2: curious, but robbed. "You've been asking about me" is the town talking behind my back, the reactivity I keep asking for. It also killed the turn, and his trust still reads 2 of 3 after he shut the door on me. So what does that number measure? Surprise: delight, that he knew.
- Turn 4.3: engaged. Sylvia gave me nothing new, but I got the silent office for free: nobody speaks to Lily. Then the next morning both Sylvia and Jonah ring Toby about Lily, an hour apart. Three people in one room, each using our tip book on the others. That's a story. Surprise: delight, the silence at the counter.
- Turn 4.4: engaged. At last I was in the room for the scene: Ken said "take her name out", I argued, he read it again and said "Run it, then." It was short, but it was mine. Then sales fell to 919, the first drop, and nobody says why. Surprise: delight, Ken backing down.

## Pull
6. The race has three runners now and Ken is in it, but four of my last six talks were dead, and the front I won still came out as two bullets.

## Thinking aloud
I worry that last week's front read as Ken's paper backing Ken's partner: the only candidate it named was Sylvia, in Sylvia's own words, under his name. If that's why sales fell, I made the mistake I swore to avoid. So the race only goes back on the front with a second name in it.

Everyone refuses "about Terry". Nobody has been asked about themselves. Lily might talk about her own bid even if she won't talk about her boss.

And I owe John Holmes a reply. I held the middle of Greta's January words until I'd put them to him. It's been a year.

## Prediction
Lily will say for print that she wants the job, because her wall is loyalty to Terry, not silence. Holmes will say he didn't know Greta was ill, and he'll be sore about "gone to pieces" sitting beside her cancer.

## Needs
- Why did sales drop: the thin front, Sylvia's name, or nothing I did?
- Who appoints the chief officer, and when? (asked twice)
- Who told Terry I'd been asking?
- What do a source's trust numbers do, if a closed door leaves them at 2?
- Does talking to Toby or Ken cost a turn? This is the fifth time I've asked. And who am I?
