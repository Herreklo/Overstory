## Pull

## Thinking aloud
At last I've been told that Toby costs a turn. That's the wrong answer for me. In BG3 talking at camp is free and it's half the game. Here my colleagues are strangers who cost the same as anyone else in town.

Mary Tanner. In Answerable I sat a hearing on her death, in this town, in 1997. Andy Shaw was the other one, and he's just joined a band. So I know roughly how she ends, which is eerie, and it makes me want to go and see her while she can still talk. She also rang us herself. Apart from Sylvia, she's the first person who has come to the paper wanting something.

On the council, everyone walls "about Terry". Toby Waller is the one man who talked about the claims out loud, to a full bar, and he's on Ken's list. If he'll stand by it, Ken has to choose.

## Prediction
Mary Tanner is kin to Toby Tanner (his mother or grandmother), and he wrote her call down flat without telling me. She'll give me a line for print about being on her own.
