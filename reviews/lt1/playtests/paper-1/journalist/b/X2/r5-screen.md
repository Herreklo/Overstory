The saw is running. Sid Tanner and Neil Lyons are at the log; Maisie Myers and Gary Hughes are stacking boards under the lean-to. John Holmes hears you out at the office door.

Lily wasn't on record, so you watched. Lily Lyons is at the counter. Sylvia Waller and Jonah Scott are at their desks. Terry Sutton keeps his door shut. The morning goes by at the desks, and nothing happens that you could put in the paper.

## Turn 4 · the edition of Thursday 6 June

Lily wasn't on record, so the race is held, and the front is the sawmill. John gave you two lines for print and wouldn't comment on the fifteen months, so the piece says so. Your plan had nothing for inside 1, so Toby filled it with notices. Ken reads the front on Wednesday night and sends it down.

> **Front · A year on**
> - Greta Brook, 67, let go from the sawmill last June, told the Courier in January: "I've come through it. Fifteen months of bad terms with John Holmes, being told how to do everything, and no one close. It's behind me." *Heard from Greta Brook, for print.*
> - Asked whether that was fair, John Holmes would not comment. *Heard from John Holmes.*
> - Asked whether he knew she was ill when he let her go: "I knew. The whole yard knew. Her work was still poor." *Heard from John Holmes, for print.*
> - "I let her go. That's all." *Heard from John Holmes, for print.*
>
> **Inside 2 · At the sawmill**
> - Gary Hughes started work at the sawmill in May. *Record (the Courier, May).*

## Your prediction

You said Lily would put her bid on record, and that Holmes would say he didn't know Greta was ill and be sore about "gone to pieces". Lily gave you nothing. Holmes said he knew.

## What you can do

A chase turn is one act: **talk** (one person, up to three questions, each answer for print or not), **watch** (a place for a part of the day), **read** (the volumes, the county paper, a notice board) or **wait** (a day). The edition is the fourth turn: a front (500 words) and two inside (250 each), each claim marked saw it, a witness saw it, heard from someone named, or record; soften, hold or spike; plan with "if". Free: walking within a turn, hover, noting a tip, asking why of a public event you have found.
