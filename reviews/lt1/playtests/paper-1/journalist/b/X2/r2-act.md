## Thinking aloud
I feel a bit grubby about Greta. "Gone to pieces" sold papers and she got one line back. From the notices I'm guessing Sam Brook is hers. He retired from the clinic and joined "the Night Shift", which plays the Anchor, while she walks the shore alone at night. That's a deduction I'd like the game to let me confirm, not hand me.

The real story is Terry Sutton. Jonah rang Toby, not for print, about his own boss. Why would he? I suspect he wants Terry's job. Jonah also helped at Terry's festival. If the claims are festival money, Jonah may be in them too. Every road here runs past Ken, and Sylvia works in that office. I want to make Ken choose, and see what he does.
