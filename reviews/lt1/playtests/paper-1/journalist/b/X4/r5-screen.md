# Round 5: what happened

## Turn 1 · Thursday 30 May, afternoon · the council office, Lily Lyons

Lily Lyons, 34, comes to the counter. You name none of your sources.

> **Lily Lyons, the council counter, Thu 30 May, 3.20.**
> (1) Will she stand: "I've let it be known I want it." *For print.*
> (2) The claims, and the county paper: "In February I said out loud what the whole office knew. Somebody had to. I've never spoken to the county paper." *Not for print: he's my chief officer.*
> (3) What the record is, and who keeps it: "How you've done the work, year on year. Nobody keeps it on paper." *For print.*

## Turn 2 · Friday 31 May, morning · the sawmill yard, John Holmes

Gary Hughes is across the yard. John Holmes, 45, has his clipboard.

> **John Holmes, the yard, Fri 31 May, 9.15.**
> (1) Whose place Gary has taken: "We'd been one short since Greta went. He was the only one looking for work." *For print.*
> (2) How long short: "Since last June. Eleven months." *For print.*
> (3) What Gary will do: "What Greta did." *For print.*

## Turn 3 · Monday 3 June, morning · the sawmill, Gary Hughes

Gary Hughes, 39, hears you out with his gloves on.

> **Gary Hughes, the yard, Mon 3 June, 9.40.**
> (1) Where he went, and why come back: "I'd done the better work there. So I started again, and this is where the work was." *For print.*
> (2) Whose work: "Greta Brook's, John says." *For print.*
> (3) What made Sally's record the stronger: "Nobody ever told me." *For print.*

He goes back to work.

## Turn 4 · the edition of Thursday 6 June

Lily said for print only that she wants it, so your front is (b).

> **Front · A third after the chief officer's post**
> - Lily Lyons: "I've let it be known I want it." "How you've done the work, year on year. Nobody keeps it on paper." *Heard from Lily Lyons, named.*
> - Terry Sutton, asked last week whether the claims had to do with his going: "I was found out over the claims last June. It's nothing to do with this." *Heard from Terry Sutton, named.*
> - Terry Sutton will step down as chief officer next year. *Record (the April notice).*
> - Two others at the office, named in last week's Courier, are also after the post.
>
> **Inside 1 · One short since last June**
> - "We'd been one short since Greta went. He was the only one looking for work." "Since last June. Eleven months." "What Greta did." *Heard from John Holmes, foreman, named.*
>
> **Inside 2 · Gary Hughes's year**
> - "I'd done the better work there. So I started again, and this is where the work was." "Greta Brook's, John says." "Nobody ever told me." *Heard from Gary Hughes, named.*

Held: Lily's (2); Sylvia's (2); Terry's word on Lily; Toby's lines; the strokes.

Ken reads it on Wednesday night. None of it names his family. He sends it down.

## Your prediction

Mark Holmes doesn't work at the sawmill. John says the mill had been one short since Greta went, eleven months, and that Gary does what she did. Gary says the same.
