# Round 4

## Marks
- Turn 3.1: engaged — Terry gave more for print than I expected, and "I've put on eleven festivals for this town" is the first answer that sounds like a man and not a rule: a dodge with pride in it. Surprise: none.
- Turn 3.2: bored — I asked about "poor for months" and got "John let me go"; I asked how long at the mill and got "all my life in this town". It's the second answer in a row that slides off its question, and I learned nothing about her work. Surprise: cheat — the lines sound drawn from a pool, not from her.
- Turn 3.3: bored — a fair check, and it found nothing. Surprise: none.
- Turn 3.4: engaged — my own front, from a man on record, sold 999 and raised patience. Surprise: delight — the first time a choice of mine paid, and then the volume a year on with no correction against my pages.

## Pull
7 — the jump turned scattered threads into one: the claims, Lily, Terry going, and Ken's brother and partner after the same post. That's the store page's promise at last. But my notebook was written for me across eleven months I didn't play.

## From memory
- **Trevor Lane:** 63, head under the bonnet. He gave me nothing for print, then retired the same day after 34 years. Off the record he said Gary did the better work.
- **Sally Baker:** the alternator in the vice. She got the job over Gary. The garage was seventeen months short when she came, and her husband Terry is at the clinic. She rang me with a tip I already had. Baby Ivan in December.
- **Gary Hughes:** a face like thunder: "I'd been doing the better work." He walked out in July, "to start again somewhere else", and now he's at the sawmill.

I remember each of them by a line and a situation. I couldn't tell you what any of them looks like.

## Thinking aloud
Jonah's two calls disagree. He wants the post (for print), then says he has told Sylvia it should be hers (not for print). Both came through Toby, who wants to be editor and would gain if I botch Ken's family story. So I go to the sources and use none of Toby's lines. My rule from last year is that people speak on record about themselves, not about those they work with, so I ask Jonah about Jonah and Sylvia about Sylvia. There's also a chain I can't print but want to test: Lily is Sylvia's friend; she raised Terry's claims before the whole office; Terry said in April he'd go; now Sylvia wants his post. "Someone at the office" last June may have been Lily. Eve Vaughan's strokes stay out: a clinic worker telling the paper about patients is not something I'll print.

## Prediction
Jonah will say for print that he's standing. Sylvia will say she is too, and will say nothing about Jonah. Ken will ask me to hold or soften. Lily was the county paper's source.

## Needs
- Who appoints the chief officer, and when.
- Whether "comes past me" means Ken can spike it, or only read it.
- What I printed from July to May. The volume shows nothing of mine.
- Where Gary was between July and May, and whose work he does at the mill.
- Whether Toby's tip lines are the caller's words or his.
- Kin: the Lanes, the Lyonses, the Brooks. Hovering still shows only work.

## Turns
1. **Thursday 23 May, afternoon, the council office: talk to Jonah Scott.** (1) "You've let it be known you want to be chief officer. Do you still?" (2) "Who chooses, and when?" (3) "What would you do with the post?" For print. I don't mention Sylvia or his second call. — Expect: yes on (1); a process and a date on (2).
2. **Friday 24 May, morning, the council office: talk to Sylvia Waller.** (1) "Will you stand for chief officer when Terry Sutton goes?" (2) "Why you?" (3) "When will it be decided?" For print. — Expect: yes, for print; nothing about Jonah.
3. **Monday 27 May, morning, the council office: talk to Terry Sutton.** (1) "When exactly do you go, and why next year?" (2) "Did last June's claims have anything to do with it?" (3) "Who appoints your successor?" For print. *If neither Jonah nor Sylvia gave anything for print, I go to the sawmill instead and ask Gary Hughes when he left the garage, where he went, and what he does at the mill.* — Expect: a date, "no" on (2), a committee or a vote on (3).
4. **Wednesday, the edition (a plan).**
   - *Front:* if both are on record about themselves, "Two from the office after chief officer's post": Jonah and Sylvia in their own words, equal space (heard, named); Terry's going (record, the April notice); how it's decided (heard, named). It goes past Ken with a line at the foot: "Jonah Scott is the editor's brother; Sylvia Waller is his partner." If he wants it held, I ask what in it is wrong, and if he can name nothing I still ask him to run it. If only one of them is on record, I run that one, with "X was asked" for the other.
   - *Inside 1:* Terry Sutton to go, in his own words, beside the April notice. No link to the claims unless he makes it.
   - *Inside 2:* Gary at the sawmill, only if he spoke; otherwise held. Nothing from Toby's lines, nothing on the strokes.
