# Journalist · b (lives on) · X4, the explorer (opus, not a cold start)

## Arc

May and June 1991: Ken's brother Jonah, his partner Sylvia, and Lily run for chief officer. Ken asks to hold the front but can name nothing wrong, so the player runs it (patience −1). Round 6 spends two turns on its own question, who fed the county paper: Susie gives kin and a denial; Neil, having heard of it, three "no comment"s.

Six editions average 902.8: kept on. Trust ends at 10, patience 4; all 31 printed claims true.

## Marks

10 engaged, 4 curious, 6 bored, 3 confused, 1 would quit.

- **Engaged.** When its own choice paid (the 999 front; asking Ken what was wrong), when it lined up dates itself, and when the jump tied the threads.
- **Delight, five times.** The scoop that cost it, the front that stood a year on, Sylvia confirming Jonah's concession, dates lining up, kin told by a person.
- **Bored.** Answers that slid off the question (Greta, Gary), an empty watch and read, a front repeating a notice.
- **Confused.** What moved sales and patience, and the same sentence coming from two people.
- **Would quit.** Neil's bare refusals, on the player's own question.
- **Cheat, five times.** Each was a shared sentence: "the better work", "whoever's is the stronger" (from four people), "and it shows".
