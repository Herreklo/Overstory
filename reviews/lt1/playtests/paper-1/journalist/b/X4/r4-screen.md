# Round 4: what happened

## Turn 1 · Thursday 23 May, afternoon · the council office, Jonah Scott

Sylvia Waller and Lily Lyons are at their desks behind the counter. Jonah Scott, 42, comes to the counter. You don't mention Sylvia or his second call.

> **Jonah Scott, the council counter, Thu 23 May, 2.40.**
> (1) Does he still want it: "I'd like it, yes. With Terry going, somebody has to want it." *For print.*
> (2) Who chooses, and when: "Whoever's record is the stronger. Terry goes next year; that's in your paper." *For print.*
> (3) What he'd do with it: "Time enough for that when it's decided." *For print.*

## Turn 2 · Friday 24 May, morning · the council office, Sylvia Waller

Sylvia Waller, 28, takes you to the end of the counter.

> **Sylvia Waller, the council counter, Fri 24 May, 10.05.**
> (1) Will she stand: "I'd like it. Ten years I've been at that office." *For print.*
> (2) Why her: "Terry's going next year, and Jonah's told me it should be mine." *Not for print: Jonah's family.*
> (3) When it's decided: "Next year, when Terry goes. Not before." *For print.*

## Turn 3 · Monday 27 May, morning · the council office, Terry Sutton

Both spoke for print, so you go to Terry Sutton, 64. Before you ask anything, he tells you:

> **Terry Sutton, unasked:** "Lily Lyons has let it be known she wants it too, since yesterday. She carried this office before she'd been here a year." *Not for print: she works for me.*

> **Terry Sutton, the council counter, Mon 27 May, 9.30.**
> (1) When, and why next year: "Next year. I'm 64, and tired." *For print.*
> (2) The claims: "I was found out over the claims last June. It's nothing to do with this." *For print.*
> (3) Who appoints: "The record decides it, not me. Whoever's is the stronger." *For print.*

**By Wednesday the tip book adds:**

> *In Toby's hand, Tue 28 May:* Sylvia Waller rang. Lily Lyons is after it as well as her. "She's earned the right to try." Not for print: they work together.

## Turn 4 · the edition of Thursday 30 May

Jonah and Sylvia are both on record about themselves, so your front is the two of them.

> **Front · Two from the office after chief officer's post**
> - Jonah Scott: "I'd like it, yes. With Terry going, somebody has to want it." "Time enough for that when it's decided." *Heard from Jonah Scott, named.*
> - Sylvia Waller: "I'd like it. Ten years I've been at that office." *Heard from Sylvia Waller, named.*
> - Terry Sutton will step down as chief officer next year. *Record (the April notice).*
> - How it is decided: "Whoever's record is the stronger" (Jonah Scott); "The record decides it, not me" (Terry Sutton); "Next year, when Terry goes. Not before" (Sylvia Waller). *Heard, named.*
> - At the foot: Jonah Scott is the editor's brother; Sylvia Waller is his partner.
>
> **Inside 1 · Terry Sutton to go next year**
> - "Next year. I'm 64, and tired." *Heard from Terry Sutton, named.*
> - The April notice. *Record.*
> - He made no link to the claims, so they stay out.
>
> **Inside 2:** held. Gary Hughes wasn't asked.

Held: Sylvia's (2); Terry's word on Lily; Sylvia's call; the year's not-for-print lines; Toby's lines and the strokes.

Ken reads the front on Wednesday night. "Hold it till it's decided."

You ask him what in it is wrong.

He reads it again. "Nothing." It goes down as written, foot line and all. The editor's patience falls to 3.

## Your prediction

Jonah said for print that he wants it. Sylvia did too, and named Jonah, not for print. Ken asked you to hold. Nobody has told you who the county paper's source was.
