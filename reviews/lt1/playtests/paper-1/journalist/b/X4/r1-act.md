# Round 1

## Pull
6 — choosing what to print, with corrections that stay in the volume, is a question with wrong answers, which is what I want. But I've been handed a cast list and a garage tip, and nothing yet that I found myself.

## Thinking aloud
Ken's speech is a wall of names I'm told rather than shown. Still, it's the most interesting thing on the screen: an editor doesn't recite his whole family to a new hire unless one of them is about to be news. The council (Sylvia, Jonah) and the clinic (Toby Waller, Thea, Colin) are where his rule bites, and I suspect the real story is there, not at the pumps. There are two Tobys, Tanner and Waller. If that's the name pool again I'll be annoyed; if it's deliberate, my notebook had better keep them apart. The garage tip on its own is thin: a man retires and someone gets his job. What makes it worth a turn is the scene. Gary came first but is at the pumps, and Sally came later but has a part in the vice. Who does the skilled work may not be who has the claim.

Free actions first: I inspect the van with its bonnet up (whose is it?), the part in Sally's vice, and anything on the workshop wall (a licence, an owner's name, a notice). I note Ken's garage line as a tip.

## Prediction
Trevor will say the choice isn't his (an owner decides), but will speak well of Sally's work and say little about Gary. Gary will tell it the other way, leaning on having come first. One of the two will be gone, or say they're going, by July.
