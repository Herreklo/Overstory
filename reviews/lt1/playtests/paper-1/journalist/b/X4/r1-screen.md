# Round 1: what happened

## Free, before your first turn

- **The van:** green, in for work; nothing on it says whose.
- **Sally's vice:** an alternator, half stripped.
- **The wall:** a chalked price board (labour by the hour, a puncture, a service). No owner's name anywhere; the sign says GARAGE.
- **Noted:** Ken's line, as a tip.

## Turn 1 · Thursday 7 June, late morning · the garage, Trevor Lane

Trevor talks with you by the van, under the radio. Sally stays at the bench and Gary at the pumps.

> **Trevor Lane, by the van, Thu 7 June, 11.15.**
> (1) When he finishes, and why this year: "Nothing for the paper on that." *No comment.*
> (2) Who decides, and have they: "Not mine to say. It goes on the record, whoever's is the stronger. Nobody's told me." *Not for print: they're both mine.*
> (3) What each does that the other doesn't: "Gary's been doing the better work." *Not for print: I work with them.*

He goes back under the bonnet.

## Turn 2 · Thursday afternoon · the Courier's back room

**The tip book has a new line**, taken while you were out:

> *In Susie's hand, Thu 7 June, 12.40:* Maisie Myers (sawmill) rang. John Holmes has let Greta Brook go from the sawmill. Not for print: they're both at the mill with me.

**The 1989 volume** runs from January; earlier volumes are not on the shelf.

- **September, a notice, word for word:** "Trevor Lane will step down as head mechanic at the garage next year."
- **The council office:** C-02 (22 June 1989, *A new clerk for the council office*, by Susie Lyons): Lily Lyons, 32, came to a post "which has been a clerk short for eleven months"; Terry Sutton "said the office had been waiting a long time for her." C-03 (14 December 1989, *Festival on the quay "the best night in years"*, by Susie Lyons): Terry Sutton's eleventh festival; his helpers Larry Baker, Toby Waller, Jonah Scott, Kate Holmes, Molly Scott and Mona Sayer: "Every one of them pulled their weight," he told the Courier.
- **The clinic:** "Sam Brook retired after six years at the clinic" (August). "Andy Waller was born to Thea Waller and Colin Berry" (November).
- **The rest:** C-01, the harbour day, Audrey Scott running it (June); C-04, Agnes Fielding, 103 (December); notices of houses, a split, a band, arrivals, Leo Lyons retired from the garage (April).

**This year's issues:** "Gary Hughes moved to the town for a job at the garage" (February). "Sally Baker moved to the town for a job at the garage, with Terry Baker and the children" (April). "Terry Baker started work at the clinic" (April). "Ian Myers left for university in the city" (May; last week's front). Nothing else names the council or the clinic.

## Turn 3 · Friday 8 June, morning · the garage, Sally Baker

**At nine, the tip book:**

> *From the counter, Fri 8 June:* Garage. Trevor Lane retired yesterday after 34 years at the garage. (Toby's.)

Trevor named Gary's work, and nothing at the clinic read open, so you go to Sally. No Trevor today. Gary is at the pumps; Sally answers without taking the alternator out of the vice.

> **Sally Baker, the bench, Fri 8 June, 9.50.**
> (1) Where before, and what she was told: "They'd been short seventeen months when I came. Gary came in February and they were still short." Where she was before, she doesn't say. *For print.*
> (2) Who gets Trevor's job: "Nobody's told me who." *For print.*
> (3) Would she stay: "We've only been here since April. Terry's at the clinic now." *Not for print: that's my husband.*

**By Monday the tip book adds:**

> *From the counter, Mon 11 June:* Garage. Sally Baker became head mechanic on Saturday. (Toby's.)
> *From the counter, Mon 11 June:* Quay. The harbour day ran like clockwork; Harry Myers had a list for everything. (Susie's.)

## Turn 4 · the edition of Thursday 14 June

Trevor gave you nothing for print and your plan named no other front, so Ken led with Susie's harbour day. The one open item in the back numbers was Trevor's September notice; your inside 1 follows it from the record, and Toby's garage piece goes on the spike. Inside 2: held.

> **Inside 1 · Thirty-four years, as he said**
> - Trevor Lane retired from the garage on Thursday after 34 years. *Record (the counter's notice).*
> - He said last September he would step down this year. *Record (1989 volume).*
> - Sally Baker was made head mechanic on Saturday. *Record (the counter's notice).*

Held: Trevor's two answers and Maisie's tip (not for print).

Ken reads it on Wednesday night. It names none of his family. "Short," he says, and sends it down.

## Your prediction

Trevor said the choice wasn't his, but the work he named as better was Gary's, not Sally's. You haven't asked Gary. July hasn't come.
