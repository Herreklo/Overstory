## Thinking aloud
Who fed the county paper last June? It had exactly two stories that Saturday: the sawmill ("one of the men at the mill") and the council ("someone at the office"). Maisie's sawmill tip came to Susie Lyons by telephone on the 7th. Susie wrote the 1989 piece welcoming Lily Lyons to the office. Neil Lyons works the saw, and was at the Anchor the night before it ran. Susie was seen looking for work elsewhere in August, then retired. Lily says she never spoke to the county paper, which could be literally true if a Lyons spoke for her. There's a second chain: Sid Tanner at the saw and Toby Tanner, who wants Ken's chair. Either chain could just be the name pool, as in Answerable. Susie has left the Courier, and my rule says people talk about what they're no longer tied to. So she's the cheapest test. I won't print the answer, since outing another paper's source isn't news. But it's the only question here that's mine, and I'll spend two turns on it knowing it may cost me a front.
