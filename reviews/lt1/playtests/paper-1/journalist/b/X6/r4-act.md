## Thinking aloud
Trust is banked at 7, patience is 7, sales clear 850 with a margin. The win condition is met, so the stakes are gone. As a min-maxer I can see the dominant line: ask people about themselves, on the record, and print only that plus record notices. It has never been wrong, never been corrected, and sales stay in a 916 to 942 band whatever I print. There is no pressure to do anything else.

The one live friction is Ken's rule. The tip book now carries a fight: Sylvia says Jonah wants Sutton's job, Lily says Sylvia does. Jonah is Ken's brother and Sylvia his partner, so the biggest story of the year is the one Ken may spike. Sutton himself steps down next year, which is a record I can print for free. I'll ask the three principals directly, on the record, then see what Ken does when I insist. I think it is the only way to find out what the rule costs. I note who is feeding me: each tipster names someone else's ambition, not their own.

I will not touch Olive's Colin and Thea quote. It is on the record, but it names two of Ken's people in a private row, and a quote like that makes a scene look like a whole character.

## Prediction
Jonah Scott, asked first on the record, says yes he'll put his name forward and defends Sutton. Sylvia Waller, asked the same afternoon, has already heard, and hedges: "if the council asks". Neither denies it.
