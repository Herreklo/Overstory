Saturday's festival on the quay was, in the words of more than one person walking home, the best night the town has had in years. **21 December 1989, by Ken Scott. "Agnes Fielding, 1886 to 1989."** Agnes Fielding, the oldest person in the town, died last week after a long illness. She was 103. Born here in August 1886, she was the widow of Colin Eden, who died in 1969. She had her two pints at the Anchor most evenings into her hundreds, was there every Friday without fail, walked the shore of an evening, and past a hundred took up a garden, out in it every morning from spring to autumn. She had a second heart attack in November. Few in the town can remember it without her.
