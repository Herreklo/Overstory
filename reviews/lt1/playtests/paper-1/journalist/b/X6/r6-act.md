## Thinking aloud
My model of the machine so far. Talk is the only verb that pays; two Watches and two county reads gave almost nothing. Principals speak freely about themselves and clam up about anyone else, which is exactly the print rule, so the game feeds me clean quotes. Trust rises only when a front with something at stake (a confession, an on-record claim) has stood a week. Fluff leaves it flat. Sales run 916 to 972 and the best two fronts both had a "no comment" hook, so I'd say conflict with an unanswered question sells. Insisting against Ken (week 4) bought patience loss and the lowest sales, so I won't insist again.

Ken's family never enters this week's plan. I want a conflict story among people who are not on his list. The garage has one: Peter Lane said on the record that Gary Hughes left because the better work went to Sally Baker, who is now head mechanic. Gary, Sally and Peter are all free of Ken's rule. I also want the one clerk I haven't pressed: Lily may be the one who raised Sutton's claims. Jonah says she brought them up in front of the whole office in February. Asked about herself, she may confirm it for print.
