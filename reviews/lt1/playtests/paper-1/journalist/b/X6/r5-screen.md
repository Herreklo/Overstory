# Round 5: what happened

## Turn 1 · Thursday 30 May, afternoon · the council office: Lily Lyons

Lily Lyons is at the counter. Jonah Scott and Sylvia Waller are at their desks; Terry Sutton's door is shut. Hover: *Lily Lyons, 34, the council office.*

- *Mr Sutton steps down next year: do you want the chief officer's job?* "I want it. I've let it be known."
- *If so, what would you change in the office?* "No comment."
- *May I print that, with your name?* "You can print that I want it."

Notebook: three entries, Lily Lyons, the council office, Thursday 30 May, afternoon. The first and third are **for print** (about herself).

Your prediction was that Lily would say plainly she wanted the job, let you print it, and say nothing of Sylvia or Jonah. She did.

## Turn 2 · Friday 31 May, morning · the council office: Terry Sutton

Terry Sutton's door is open, and he waves you in.

- *Have the claims you were found out over been repaid, or will they be before you go?* "No comment."
- *Is stepping down linked to them?* "My own decision, as I told you. Thirty-odd years is enough."
- *May I quote you?* "On that, yes. And if you're writing about who follows me: Lily carried this office her first year. I made sure everyone knew it."

Notebook: three entries, Terry Sutton, Friday 31 May, morning. The first two are **for print** (about himself). The last is **not for print** (about his staff).

## Turn 3 · Monday 3 June, morning · the council office, from the opening

The office opens at nine. Terry Sutton goes through to his room and shuts the door. Jonah Scott and Sylvia Waller are at their desks, Lily Lyons at the counter. The telephone goes now and then; a man comes in about a drain and goes away with a form. Nothing passes between them that you can read, and nothing else happens that you see.

## Turn 4 · Wednesday 5 June · making up the edition of 6 June

Nothing new has come into the tip book.

Lily and Terry Sutton both went on the record, so your front ran. It names none of Ken's people. He reads the page through and sends it down.

**Your page, as it went to press:**
- **Front: "The job and the money."** Lily Lyons, 34, of the council office, wants to be chief officer when Terry Sutton steps down next year. "I want it," she told the Courier. "I've let it be known." She is the third: two colleagues told this paper last week that they want the post. Mr Sutton, who was found out last June taking from the expense claims, was asked whether the money had been repaid. "No comment," he said. Was his going linked to it? "My own decision, as I told you. Thirty-odd years is enough." *Marks: heard from Lily Lyons, for print; heard from Terry Sutton, for print; record (21 June 1990, 30 May).*
- **Inside: "A son at the garage."** Ivan Baker was born in December to Sally Baker, head mechanic at the garage since last June, and Terry Baker of the clinic. *Marks: record (notices).*
- **Inside: "From the café to the Anchor."** Peter Lane will step down as owner of the Old Bank Café next year. With Oliver Brook he started Driftwood in December; Andy Shaw joined them, and on their first night at the Anchor last month "Coming Home on a Sunday", the first song Peter Lane has written, went down best. *Marks: record (notices; 23 May).*

**The edition sold 972.** The editor's patience rises to 7. Readers' trust rises to 8: last week's front has stood.
