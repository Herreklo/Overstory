## Thinking aloud
Both soft numbers slid at once: patience 6, sales 895, down from a 916 to 942 band. I can't tell which of three things did it: my insisting, a dull succession front, or the calendar. My read is a consequence story sells better than a procedure story. The confession sold 942 and the festival rehash 919. The cheapest test of Ken's rule is its letter: it says "names". Lily Lyons is not on his list, and I haven't asked her yet. Jonah and Sylvia each told me she wants it, and both are not for print. Asked directly, she can put it on the record herself, which removes my tipsters. Sutton still owes me one answer, the money, because Lily said nobody has mentioned it. A story about Lily and the money names nobody on Ken's list. I will refer to Jonah and Sylvia only as "two colleagues who told this paper so last week", and see whether Ken reads the letter or the spirit. I'm still holding Greta's illness.

## Prediction
Lily Lyons, asked about herself, says plainly she wants the chief officer's job and lets me print that. She will not speak of Sylvia or Jonah. Every principal so far has said yes about themselves and no about anyone else.
