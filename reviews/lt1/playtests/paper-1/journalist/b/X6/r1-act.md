# Round 1

## Pull
5 — The win condition is stated outright (850 sales, trust 7, don't drain two patience meters), so I can go looking for the cheapest line to it. Nothing to attack yet, because I don't know what moves any of the three numbers.

## Thinking aloud
Sales start at exactly the 850 I'm told to hold, so I'm on the line from day one. Trust has to climb one point in about three editions. Patience starts at 5 and Ken has already told me the rule that costs it: naming his people without clearing it.

The obvious dominant line is a boring one. Fill the paper with claims I marked "saw it" or "record", and name no one on Ken's list. The tip points straight at the clinic, and the clinic is two Wallers, Colin Berry (Thea's husband) and no clear way to write about it without naming one of them. That is either a trap or a free pass. If Ken clears a story and it counts as covered, I can send every clinic piece past him and spend no patience. I don't know yet. Terry Baker, since April, is the one clinic person off Ken's list, so I'll start with him. A story about his job names no Waller.

My first three turns are meant to measure the machine as much as to fill pages. A cheap safe Talk shows what source trust does when I push on question three. A Read shows what the bound volumes teach about corrections. A Watch shows how far "saw it" can carry a whole front page. Susie holds harbour day, so I'm also checking whether writing it costs me with Ken.

## Prediction
Terry Baker answers questions one and two on the record and goes quiet on three. Colin Berry drifts in from the waiting room to listen, because his wife runs the place and he has been my guide.

## Needs
- What moves sales: topic, an exclusive, front page against inside, or just trust. Is 850 a bar I must beat each week, or the number to hold?
- What does passing a story past Ken cost: a turn, patience? Can he change or kill it? Does a cleared story count as safe for trust?
- Does trust move only when I print something wrong, or also when I print something right? Is a "record" claim safer than "heard from someone named"?
- Does a source's trust (2 of 3) fall with each pointed question, and what does 3 unlock?
- Can I do two things in one turn, such as two Talks or a Watch plus a Talk? Is a morning and an afternoon of the same day two turns?
- Does reporting harbour day, which is Susie's, step on her and cost patience?

## Turns
1. Thursday morning, at the clinic: Talk to Terry Baker, three questions. (a) What did you do before April and why here? (b) What has changed in how the clinic runs since you came? (c) What would a patient not know from the notice board? — Expect: warm, usable answers on one and two; question three is where he hesitates, and I see whether source trust moves.
2. Thursday afternoon, at the Courier: Read the 1989 bound volume and this year to date. Look for corrections, how past stories marked their claims, any clinic items, and how Ken's family has appeared. — Expect: it shows what a correction looks like, and gives me "record" claims that cost nothing to print.
3. Saturday afternoon, on the quay: Watch harbour day. Names, crowd, who runs what. — Expect: a story built entirely from "saw it", with no one on Ken's list in it. This is my safe front-page candidate, unless Susie's claim on it costs me.
4. Wednesday, the edition, as a plan. Front: the strongest "saw it" story. Inside one: the clinic piece, naming no one on Ken's list, or if it names one it goes to Ken first. Inside two: a "record" item from the bound volume. Hold or spike anything resting on a single "heard from" claim. — Expect: sales hold at about 850, trust edges up because every claim has a clean mark.
