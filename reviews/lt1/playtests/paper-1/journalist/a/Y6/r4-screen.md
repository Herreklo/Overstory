**2. Thursday 23 May, afternoon: Terry Sutton at the council office.** Lily Lyons is at the counter. Sylvia Waller and Jonah Scott are at their desks. Terry Sutton sees you in his room. All for print.
- *Did you and Jonah Scott have words here, and over what?* "No comment."
- *The gully steps, a year on: whose are they, and has anything been done?* "Nothing. Nobody's touched them."
- *Are you staying on as chief officer?* "I'm putting on a festival on the quay this summer."

**3. Thursday 23 May, evening: Mark Holmes.** Eve named no witness, so you went to Mark Holmes. He isn't at the Anchor. Peter Scott, behind the bar: "Mark? Fridays." You find him at home. All for print.
- *What did you and Terry Sutton fall out over?* "Terry and I fell out badly at the Anchor before Christmas." Over what? "I couldn't tell you. It's done." Then: "Alex Myers and I danced at the Anchor last August."
- *Have you seen him have words with anyone else?* "No comment."
- *And Sally Baker in October?* "Sally Baker and I had a screaming match at the Anchor. October."
