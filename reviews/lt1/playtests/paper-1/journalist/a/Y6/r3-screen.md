**1. Thursday 21 June, morning: Terry Sutton at the council office.** Lily Lyons is at the counter. Sylvia Waller and Jonah Scott are at their desks. Terry Sutton sees you in his room with the door open. All for print.
- *Whose are the gully steps, and when were they last repaired?* "No comment."
- *Was a fall or a fault reported this year?* "Nothing's come across my desk."
- *Is any work planned?* "Nothing. Nothing planned."

**2. Thursday 21 June, evening: Una Waller.** She is out when you knock. She comes back from the shore at dusk and talks at her door, at the foot of the steps. All for print.
- *Did you see or hear anyone fall on these steps in May?* "I wouldn't know."
- *Did Greta Brook fall on these steps?* "I wouldn't know."
- *Has anyone come to look at them or repair them since?* "Nobody's been near them."

**3. Friday 22 June, morning: Greta Brook, in her shed.** Una didn't place Greta on the gully steps, so you went back to Greta. The chair is finished; she is sanding a drawer. All for print.
- *Was it the gully steps?* "I had a bad fall on the steps. I've said."
- *When did you finish at the sawmill?* "I finished at the start of the month." Then: "Hannah Tanner took me out on the boat, the Wednesday before last."
- *Who helped you home?* "No comment."

**4. Wednesday 27 June: the edition.** Nothing new from the counter this week.
- **Front · An ankle on the gully steps.** Audrey Scott, 68, wrenched her ankle on the gully steps in May *(heard from someone named: Eve Vaughan)*. Terry Sutton, chief officer, would not say whose the steps are or when they were last repaired; "Nothing's come across my desk," he said, and "Nothing. Nothing planned" *(heard from someone named: Terry Sutton)*.
- **Inside · Harbour day changes hands.** Last year's organiser ran the 1989 harbour day; Harry Myers of the school ran this year's *(record)*. No reason from Harry: you didn't see him.
- **Inside · Greta Brook leaves the sawmill.** Greta Brook finished at the sawmill at the start of June, she told the Courier *(heard from someone named: Greta Brook)*.

Ken reads it. On the front: "My mother again. Out." Your plan kept her. "Over my word again, then." On *Harbour day:* "And here." Your plan complied: *last year's organiser*.

**Thursday 28 June.** Sales 875. Susie, from the counter: "A woman in this morning said you had Greta Brook's fall right." Ken puts the sales sheet down. "Readers' trust is at seven. That was the bargain. You're on the staff."
