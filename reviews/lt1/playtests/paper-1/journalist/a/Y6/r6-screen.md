# Round 6

## Your turns

**Free, before turn 1.** Noted: *Harbour day, Sat 8 June: on? who runs it?*

**1. Thursday 6 June, afternoon: Audrey Scott, at home.** She is 69. All for print.
- *Your ankle on the gully steps in May last year: what happened, and who saw it?* "I went over on my ankle on the gully steps last May." Who saw it, she doesn't say.
- *You ran harbour day in 1989, and Harry Myers in 1990: why did you stop, and who runs it this Saturday?* "Harry Myers took it over last year." This Saturday? "I wouldn't know."
- *Are you in Terry Sutton's festival this summer?* "Not me."

**2. Saturday 8 June, morning: watching the quay.** On the walk, the county paper at the co-op: nothing from the town this week. At seven the swimmers are in the harbour: Toby Tanner, Lily Lyons, Colin Berry, John Holmes, Trevor Lane, Toby Waller, Sylvia Waller, Thea Waller, Sam Brook, Una Waller, Oliver Brook, Leo Lyons, Kate Holmes and Edward Baker. Later the weekend boats go out: Ken Scott, Gary Hughes, Toby and Grace Waller, Sylvia, Thea, Sam Brook, Eve Vaughan, Neil Lyons, Mark Holmes, Olive Shaw, Peter Scott and Edward Baker. Going down to the boat, Sylvia tells Grace she had the washing out yesterday. Nothing is set up on the quay: no stalls, no tables. By noon it is empty.

**3. Monday 10 June, morning: Sam Brook, in his garden.** Nobody ran a harbour day on Saturday, so you went to Sam. All for print.
- *Are you in Sutton's festival this summer, and when is it?* "Not me." When? "I wouldn't know."
- *What made December 1989 a washout?* "It was a washout. Why? I couldn't tell you. It's done."
- *Who else was in it?* "No comment."

**4. Wednesday 12 June: the edition.** Nothing new from the counter this week.

Your dummy, made up from your plan:
- **Front · No harbour day this year.** Saturday came and went on the quay with nothing set up *(saw it)*. Harbour day was held in 1989 and in 1990 *(record)*.
- **Inside · Audrey Scott's ankle.** "I went over on my ankle on the gully steps last May," Audrey Scott, 69, told the Courier *(heard from someone named: Audrey Scott)*. *Inferred, mine:* she gave up harbour day after the fall.
- **Inside · Not in the festival.** Sam Brook is not in Terry Sutton's festival this summer. "Not me," he told the Courier. Of December 1989's: "It was a washout" *(heard from someone named: Sam Brook)*.

Ken reads it. On the ankle: "My mother. Out." Your plan kept her. "Over my word, and on a guess." He puts the page down and doesn't look at you again that evening.

## Thursday 13 June 1991 · the Courier, nine in the morning

The edition is in bundles by the door. Your front page is *No harbour day this year*.

Ken has the sales sheet. "Eight hundred and fifty-three."

Toby, from the counter: "Woman in this morning said you had Terry Sutton's festival before anyone."

At eleven Ken takes a call at the big desk. He listens, says "Yes, Mother," and puts the receiver down. "She wants the line about harbour day put right. It goes in next week."

## The next five weeks

You keep the job; the six editions averaged 882. With patience at one, any override or scoop ends it, so from here you comply with every cut Ken asks, visit Eve each week, and print every for-print line you hold.

**20 June.** Page 2 carries the correction to Audrey's line. Eve rings in Hannah Tanner and Sally Baker "having a slanging match in the street"; it leads, with Mark Holmes's dance with Alex Myers and, from Eve, Lily Lyons and Sylvia Waller's ferry day out (Ken: "Sylvia. Out." You comply). 911 sold; patience 2.

**27 June.** The harbour day ran on Saturday the 22nd, smoothly, with Maisie Myers in charge: it comes from the counter and leads, a fortnight after your front said there would be none. Eve gives you Hannah Tanner's solo at the choir's spring concert and Molly Scott's family staying a fortnight. A man at the counter says you had the row in the street right. Trust 9. 892.

**4 July.** Gary Newman has moved to the town to work at the garage, with Lynn Newman: from the counter, your front. Eve has nothing for you. Thin insides. 881.

**11 July.** Eve rings: Greta Brook "had a bad fall on the steps and was laid up for a month." It leads, with Greta's own words from last June beside it, that she finished at the sawmill. 932; patience 3. On the Friday, at the clinic, Eve tells you Colin Berry has left the clinic: "Not for print. He works with me." Nobody outside the clinic says it. Sam Brook, Oliver Brook and Hannah Tanner say less to you after the Greta piece, and so do Kate Holmes and Olive Shaw.

**18 July.** Eve, from the choir: Lily Lyons sang a solo at the spring concert. It leads a quiet paper. A woman at the counter says you had Greta's fall right. Trust 10. 870.

Ken writes each number in the planner. The town's festival is still "this summer".
