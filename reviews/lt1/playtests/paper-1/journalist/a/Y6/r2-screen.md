# Round 2

## Your turns

**Free, before turn 1.** Hover: Greta Brook, 66; work: none. Noted: *Greta's fall, May: which steps, whose?*

**1. Thursday 14 June, morning: Greta Brook, at her house.** She is in the shed at the back, gluing the leg of a kitchen chair, and doesn't stop while you talk. Everything she says about herself is for print.
- *Which steps, what day in May, and what made you fall?* "I took a tumble on the steps in May and was off my feet for a month." Which steps? "The steps." The day? "May." What made you fall? "I couldn't tell you. It's done."
- *Who saw it or helped you, and who treated you?* "They knew at the sawmill straight off." Then, wiping the glue off her thumb: "I'm not at the sawmill now."
- *Had anyone complained about those steps before?* "I wouldn't know."

Free, on the walk back: she named no steps. On your way back you pass the gully steps, the town's longest flight, from Harbour Row up past the houses to the Landing. Stone treads, evenly worn, a handrail on the left going up. No fresh mortar, no new rail, nothing marked.

**2. Thursday 14 June, afternoon: watching the gully steps.** She named no witness, so you watched the steps. Hannah Tanner comes down at two with an empty bag and goes back up at three with a full one. Andy Shaw goes down in running things and comes back up an hour later, slower. Karen Holmes's door stays shut; you can hear a saw in her shed. Una Waller comes home to the attic of the house at the foot of the steps a little after four. Nobody falls, stumbles or stops to look at anything. Nothing happens on the steps all afternoon.

**3. Friday 15 June, morning: Eve Vaughan, at the clinic.** Greta named no steps, so whose they are isn't known; your plan went to Eve when the steps weren't shown to be public. She is in the back room again.
- *Has anyone else been hurt on steps this year?* "Audrey Scott wrenched her ankle on the gully steps in May." *(for print)*
- *What happened in May and June that the Courier missed?* "Neil bought a second-hand car in May." *(not for print: her husband)*
- *Colin's busy week in May: for print now?* "Not from me. Colin works with me." *(not for print)*

**4. Wednesday 20 June: the edition.** No new tips from the counter this week. The county paper on Saturday had nothing from the town.

Your dummy, made up from your plan:
- **Front · Greta Brook laid up after a fall.** Greta Brook, 66, had a bad fall on the steps in May and was laid up for a month *(heard from someone named: Greta Brook; and Eve Vaughan of the clinic)*. "I took a tumble on the steps in May and was off my feet for a month," she told the Courier. "They knew at the sawmill straight off." She could not say what made her fall *(heard: Greta Brook)*. No council answer: you didn't go to the council.
- **Inside · The gully steps.** Stone treads, evenly worn, a handrail on one side, no sign of recent work *(saw it)*. Hannah Tanner, Andy Shaw and Una Waller on them on Thursday afternoon *(saw it)*. Greta named no steps, so this piece says nothing of where she fell.
- **Inside · An ankle on the gully steps.** Audrey Scott wrenched her ankle on the gully steps in May *(heard from someone named: Eve Vaughan)*.

Ken reads it. The front: nothing. On the ankle: "That's my mother. Her name comes out." Your plan said comply with any other family name, so it went in as he asked: *an ankle was wrenched on the gully steps in May.*
