## Thinking aloud
Trust goes up 1 when a reader says a story was right and down 1 when someone gets one corrected. Both arrive late, through the counter. Patience has stopped moving (4, 4, 4), so it reads as a countdown with three overrides left. Trust is the cheap currency and patience the dear one. The 1989 list was Susie's, quoting Sutton, and Ken was on it. He's the one man who knows, and he booked the correction without arguing. Two things to grind. First, the raffle: it comes round again, it's public and a whole pub sees it. That makes it perfect bait for a reader saying "you had it right", and the barman knows the draw night. Second, the probe I want most: does attribution shield me? Every wrong claim so far was in the paper's own voice. If a story made only of "X said" can never be corrected, quoting everyone beats the game.
