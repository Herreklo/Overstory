# Round 6 · what happened

## Turn 1 · Thursday 6 June, afternoon · the garage, Sally Baker

Sally Baker is alone at the garage, at the bench. Hover: *Sally Baker, 40, head mechanic, the garage.* Before you go, you ask the file why the 1989 festival was a washout: "Nothing on record says why."

> **Sally Baker, the bench, Thu 6 June, 2.40.** Asked where she and Hannah Tanner were when they had words yesterday, and who else was there: "Hannah Tanner and I argued in the street yesterday." *(for print)*
> **Sally Baker, 2.41.** Asked when Gary Hughes left the garage last year, and whether he said where he was going: "Gary left in July." *(for print)*
> **Sally Baker, 2.42.** Asked whether you may print the first in her name: "You can print it." *(for print)*

## Turn 2 · Saturday 8 June, afternoon · watching the quay

Nobody is setting up on the quay: there is no harbour day today. Through the afternoon the weekend boats come back in, and Ken Scott, Gary Hughes, Toby Waller, Sylvia Waller, Thea Waller, Sam Brook, Eve Vaughan, Neil Lyons, Mark Holmes, Grace Waller, Olive Shaw, Peter Scott and Edward Baker tie up and go home. Sylvia Waller, tying up, says she had the washing out yesterday. Maisie, Harry, John, Sally and Hannah don't come.

## Turn 3 · Monday 10 June, morning · Hannah Tanner's door

Watch showed nobody at odds, so you went to Hannah Tanner. Hover: *Hannah Tanner, 75, retired.*

> **Hannah Tanner, her door, Mon 10 June, 10.05.** Asked where, and who else was there: "Sally Baker and I had a shouting match in the street on Wednesday." *(for print)*
> **Hannah Tanner, 10.06.** Asked whether she had met Sally Baker before she came in April last year: "She came to the town in April last year." Then: "She was made head mechanic that June." *(for print)*
> **Hannah Tanner, 10.07.** Asked whether you may print it, in her name: "I suppose so." *(for print)*

## Turn 4 · Wednesday 12 June · the edition

No harbour day on Saturday, so your empty-quay branch ran: Sally's account on the front, Hannah's beside it, the swim inside. Ken read the swim and said: "Sylvia, Toby Waller, Thea and Colin come out of that." Your plan didn't say to insist, so you took them out.

- **Front · Words in the street.** Sally Baker, head mechanic at the garage, and Hannah Tanner argued in the street on Wednesday, Mrs Baker told the Courier. *Mark: heard from someone named (Sally Baker).* Ms Tanner called it a shouting match. *Mark: heard from someone named (Hannah Tanner).* The two put it differently.
- **Inside · The swim at seven.** On Friday 31 May at seven, Toby Tanner, Lily Lyons, John Holmes, Trevor Lane, Sam Brook, Una Waller, Oliver Brook, Leo Lyons, Kate Holmes and Edward Baker swam in the harbour. *Mark: saw it.*
- **Inside:** the notices.

Ken initials the page. Next morning: "Eight hundred and ninety-nine."

**Your prediction:** Sally gave the plain act, in the street, yesterday, and named nobody else there. You didn't ask her why, so there was no wall to meet.

# The tenth hour · 13 June to 18 July 1991

You keep the paper as you have: who, where and when from people who give their names, nothing on causes or health, and Ken's family out when he asks. Three chase turns a week go to whoever a tip names, a watch of the quay or the harbour at seven, and the county paper on Saturdays, which has nothing from the town in all five weeks. On the Fridays you watch the harbour, the same fourteen swim at seven. Ken reads each page and initials it; after the swim, none names his family.

**20 June.** Susie's October tip takes you to Mark Holmes: "Sally and I had words at the Anchor, back in October." Print it, he says. Sally says only "no comment" now. Front: the October row, in his words; inside, the harbour day to come, in Maisie's. Ken: "Nine hundred and twenty-four." Patience 9.

**27 June.** On Saturday 22 June you watch the quay: tables out, the town about, and the harbour day runs smoothly with Maisie Myers in charge. You saw it, and Maisie says "It went well," for print. Front. Toby's harbour-day piece is spiked. 864.

**4 July.** From the counter: Gary Newman has come to the town with Lynn Newman and started at the garage. He tells you "I've started at the garage," for print. Front; Toby's piece spiked again. The county paper on Saturday has nothing from the town. 887.

**11 July.** The tip book has gone quiet: Susie and Maisie have nothing you haven't printed. You run the harbour day again in Maisie's words. 861.

**18 July.** Quiet again. You run the Newmans settling in at the garage. 881.

Five editions, average 883; no week falls below 860 or passes 925. Trust stays at 10, patience at 9. The job is yours; nobody complains, and nothing is corrected. The oldest entries left in the tip book are the ones you held back: Neil's car, the November words, Harry's operation, John and Harry in the street.
