## Turn 3 · Monday 18 June, morning · the sawmill yard, John Holmes

Greta says he let her go, against his "We parted company", so you put it to him for reply. He is at the office door.

> **John Holmes, the office door, Mon 18 June, 9.10.** Asked: "Ms Brook says you let her go. Do you want to answer, for print?" "I've said all I'm saying." *(no comment)*

## Turn 4 · Wednesday 20 June · the edition

No second named source agreed with Greta (Sid won't be named; John didn't answer), so your second branch ran: her account in her name, no cause stated. Greta's fall gave the short second story.

- **Front · "John Holmes let me go," says Greta Brook.**
  - Greta Brook worked at the sawmill until Friday 1 June. *Mark: heard from someone named (John Holmes, last week).*
  - "John Holmes let me go," Ms Brook told the Courier. *Mark: heard from someone named (Greta Brook).*
- **Inside · Off her feet after a fall.**
  - Greta Brook took a tumble on the steps in May and was off her feet for a month, she told the Courier. *Mark: heard from someone named (Greta Brook).*
- **Inside:** Susie's notices.

Nothing from Maisie, nothing about Neil's car, nothing about Una Waller. Ken reads the page, initials it and hands it back.

**Your prediction:** Greta went on the record. She said John let her go and that she fell on the steps in May, in plain words and without heat. Asked whether the two had anything to do with each other, she said she couldn't say. She didn't take up "parted company".
