# Round 5 · what happened

## Turn 1 · Friday 31 May, morning · watching the quay

At seven the swimmers come down to the harbour: Toby Tanner, Lily Lyons, Colin Berry, John Holmes, Trevor Lane, Toby Waller, Sylvia Waller, Thea Waller, Sam Brook, Una Waller, Oliver Brook, Leo Lyons, Kate Holmes and Edward Baker. After, Oliver Brook takes the boats out with Eric Lane, Matt Sayer, Steve Baker, Molly Scott and Bert Kemp, and the moorings stand empty for the rest of the morning.

At the end of the quay, stone steps go down to the water, dark and wet below the tide line. Nobody feeds the gulls. Greta Brook doesn't come. Nothing else happens on the quay.

## Turn 2 · Saturday 1 June, afternoon · Greta Brook's door

Greta answers the door herself. Hover: *Greta Brook, 67.*

> **Greta Brook, her door, Sat 1 June, 2.30.** Asked where the steps were that she fell on in May last year: "I took a tumble on the steps, May last year." Asked where, she shrugs. *(for print)*
> **Greta Brook, 2.31.** Asked whether she fed the gulls there: "I fed the gulls on the quay, January two years ago." Then: "John Holmes waved to me across the harbour, back in December." *(for print)*
> **Greta Brook, 2.32.** Asked whether you may print it, in her name: "Put it in if you like." *(for print)*

She says good afternoon and shuts the door.

## Turn 3 · Monday 3 June, morning · the Courier, Ken Scott

Ken is at the big desk with the week's planner.

> **Ken Scott, the big desk, Mon 3 June, 9.15.** Asked why he and Gary Hughes took the bus to the city last June: "I couldn't tell you. It's done."
> **Ken Scott, 9.16.** Asked whether a story on the quay steps and the council office would pass him: "Everything passes me. If it names my family, I'll say so."
> **Ken Scott, 9.16.** Asked whether you may print the bus trip, in his name: "I'm not your source. Get it from someone who was there."

He turns a page of the planner.

## Turn 4 · Wednesday 5 June · the edition

Greta named no place for the steps, so your second branch ran. Gary's "this month" was May, so the line says May.

- **Front · Maisie Myers to run the harbour day.** Maisie Myers, of the sawmill, is organising this year's harbour day, on the quay, she told the Courier. *Mark: heard from someone named (Maisie Myers).*
- **Inside · A new hand at the sawmill.** Gary Hughes started work at the sawmill in May, he told the Courier. *Mark: heard from someone named (Gary Hughes).*
- **Inside · A raffle win at the Anchor.** Ivan Tanner and Molly Scott have won a packet on the Anchor's raffle. *Mark: heard from someone named (Susie Lyons).*

Ken reads the page, initials it and hands it back.

**Your prediction:** the quay has stone steps down to the water, wet at the bottom, with nothing patched on them. Greta didn't come, and nobody fed the gulls.
