## Thinking aloud
The swim is the best thing I've been given: one Friday, seven o'clock, every name a person I know. Ken's rule has a place to bite now, so I'll test it with a harmless piece. Greta's dates stay consistent between years ("January last year" became "January two years ago"), which makes me trust her more than her shrug. I'll drop the steps: three say "steps", one flight exists, nobody links them. "Why" has been walled four times (Greta, Una, Toby, Ken); who, where and when have always been answered, so I'll ask only those. Sales are lowest on fact-only fronts (879, 891) and highest when Greta was the front (938), a nudge toward printing rows, which I won't do on a cheap pretext. Last year's harbour day fell on the second Saturday and Maisie runs this year's, so I'm betting on Saturday 8 June. Sally Baker is in three entries (hospital in March, Mark Holmes in October, Hannah Tanner yesterday), and Gary left her garage last July before turning up at the mill: she is the freshest thread.

## Prediction
Sally Baker will give me the place and who was there without fuss, then wall on the cause with "I couldn't tell you. It's done." Why: who, where and when have always been answered, and what-about has been walled four times.
