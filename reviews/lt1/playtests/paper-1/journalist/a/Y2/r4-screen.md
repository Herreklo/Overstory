John Holmes is at the office door with his papers. Gary Hughes is under the lean-to beside Maisie Myers; he wipes his hands on his trousers when you come over. Hover: *Gary Hughes, 39, the sawmill.*

Your notebook:

## Turn 2 · Friday 24 May, morning · the sawmill yard, Maisie Myers

Maisie is at the stacks under the lean-to. She wipes her hands when she sees you.

> **Maisie Myers, the lean-to, Fri 24 May, 9.30.** Asked what's planned, and where on the quay: "I'm organising the harbour day this year. On the quay." *(for print)*
> **Maisie Myers, 9.31.** Asked what's different from last year: "Harry had it last year." Then: "John fed the gulls on the quay, April last year." *(not for print: her partner; her coworker)*
> **Maisie Myers, 9.32.** Asked whether you may print it in her name: "You can put my name to that." *(for print)*

Your notebook links the name: *Harry Myers, 44, the school.*

## Turn 3 · Monday 27 May, morning · the Courier, Toby Tanner

Gary named nobody whose place he took, so you went to Toby. Your turn named no questions; the notebook put one, on the topic you gave. Toby is at his desk; Ken is at the big desk.

> **Toby Tanner, his desk, Mon 27 May, 9.20.** Asked what the words with Ken in November were about: "Ken and I had words, back in November." And what about: "I couldn't tell you. It's done." *(not for print: his coworker)*

He goes back to his work.

## Turn 4 · Wednesday 29 May · the edition

Gary gave a month and his name, so your first branch ran. Your plan named one inside story; the other inside slot took the notices. Nothing from Sally Baker, Edward Baker, Trevor Lane or Neil, as you said.

- **Front · A new hand at the sawmill.** Gary Hughes, 39, started work at the sawmill this month, he told the Courier. *Mark: heard from someone named (Gary Hughes).*
- **Inside · Maisie Myers has the harbour day.** Maisie Myers, of the sawmill, is organising this year's harbour day, on the quay, she told the Courier. *Mark: heard from someone named (Maisie Myers).*
- **Inside:** the notices.

Ken reads the page, initials it and hands it back.

**Your prediction:** Gary talked, and for print. He said he started this month and that he wouldn't know whose place he took. He said nothing of John or the office door, and nothing about how long the place had been open.
