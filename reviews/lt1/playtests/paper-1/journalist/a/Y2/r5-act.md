## Thinking aloud
Put the strays together. Greta fed gulls on the quay, John fed gulls on the quay, the harbour day is on the quay, the 1989 festival was on the quay. Greta fell on "the steps" in May and three people said so, yet the mill has none. The quay is the one public place in every thread, and quays have steps down to the water. My bet is that the steps are the quay's, not the mill's. If so, John's wall may not be about the fall at all. And a quay is the council's, where Ken's brother Jonah and Sylvia work. That is where his rule would bite, and it is the first place I can see something to lose. The skipped year also left Ken and Gary on a bus last June, the month Greta went, and Gary left the garage by July. I won't build on that. I'll ask Ken, and see what it costs.

## Prediction
The quay: stone steps down to the water, worn in the middle and wet, one tread patched. Greta Brook will be there with bread for the gulls in the morning. Why: she did it in 1989, she's retired, and everyone's "steps" must be somewhere public.
