John Holmes tucks the papers under his arm and comes two steps out of the office door. Hover: *John Holmes, 44, foreman, the sawmill.* The saw keeps running; Sid Tanner and Neil Lyons don't look round.

## Turn 2 · Thursday 7 June, afternoon · the sawmill yard, Maisie Myers

Nobody said where Greta is, so you went to Maisie Myers. Your turn named no questions for her; the notebook put turn 1's three.

Maisie is under the lean-to with the afternoon's boards. Hover: *Maisie Myers, 39, the sawmill.* She keeps stacking while she talks.

> **Maisie Myers, the lean-to, Thu 7 June, 2.40.** Asked whether Greta Brook still works here, and since when: "She's gone. Sacked, Friday." *(not for print: about her coworkers)*
> **Maisie Myers, 2.41.** Asked what happened, and who was there: "We all saw it. Me, Sid, Neil and Vera." *(not for print: about her coworkers)*
> **Maisie Myers, 2.42.** Asked whether you can print it in her name: "Not with my name on it." Then, squaring the next board on the pile: "She had a bad fall on the steps in May. Laid up a month." *(not for print: about her coworkers)*

Your notebook links the new name: *Vera Holmes, 42.*

## Turn 3 · Friday 8 June, morning · the back room, the bound volumes

John and Maisie agree she is gone and on the day: no contradiction, so the turn went to the volumes.

**The sawmill, 1989 and this year to date.** One notice, April 1989: *Maisie Myers has moved from the school to the sawmill.* John Holmes appears once, in December (below). Greta Brook's name is not in either volume.

> **14 December 1989 · Festival on the quay a washout** · *By Susie Lyons.* Saturday's festival on the quay, put on by Terry Sutton, 62, of the council office, was a washout. Mr Sutton first spoke of it in May. Ken Scott, Sylvia Waller and Sam Brook were in it with him, and Eddie Lane, Peter Scott, John Holmes and Harry Myers each had a part. "Every one of them did their bit," he told the Courier.

The rest of 1989: **15 June · Harbour day runs smoothly** (*Toby Tanner*), Audrey Scott, 67, in charge; **22 June · A new clerk for the council office** (*Susie Lyons*), Lily Lyons, 32; **21 December · Agnes Fielding, 1886 to 1989** (*Ken Scott*), the oldest in the town, at 103. Notices: Matt Sayer and Olive Shaw split up; Trevor Lane and later Alex Myers won the Anchor's raffle; Sam Brook joined the Night Shift, which played the Anchor in April and September; Leo Lyons retired from the garage; Sam Brook retired from the clinic; Una Waller came for the school; Andy Waller born to Thea Waller and Colin Berry. This year to date: Gary Hughes came for the garage (February); Sally Baker came for the garage, with Terry Baker, who started at the clinic (April); Ian Myers left for university; Toby Tanner bought a small boat (May).

# The Courier · Thursday 14 June 1990 · nine in the morning

The bundles by the door: **front,** *Greta Brook leaves the sawmill;* **inside,** the notices. Elsewhere, **A new head mechanic at the garage** (*Toby Tanner*): Trevor Lane, 63, has retired; Sally Baker, 39, who came in April with her husband Terry Baker (now at the clinic), takes his place; Mr Lane says he will not miss the cold mornings. And **Harbour day "went off well enough"** (*Susie Lyons*): Harry Myers of the school was in charge, and the quay was cleared by dark.

Ken has the sales sheet. "Nine hundred and thirteen." He writes it in the planner. Susie is at the window.
