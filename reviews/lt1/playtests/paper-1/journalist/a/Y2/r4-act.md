# Round 4

## Marks
- Turn 3.1: curious — the office door has a worn sill and no steps. My prediction was wrong, and it is the most useful thing I've learned in three rounds: Greta, Maisie and Sid all said "the steps", and none of them is at the mill. Surprise: delight (small) — a wrong guess that reframes the case.
- Turn 3.2: bored — "Nothing from the town this week." A whole go gone, and I can't tell whether the county paper has nothing or the game has nothing. Surprise: none
- Turn 3.3: bored — "I couldn't tell you. It's done," then "I'd rather not say." Una's "if it's worth the ink" is the first line in the game with a voice of its own. Surprise: none
- Turn 3.4: confused — a front about nobody saying anything, and a row nobody can explain. Sales fell to 922, the first time a number moved against a weak edition, and that is the first honest signal I've had. But trust 9, patience 8 and a permanent job arrived across a year skip with no cause shown. Surprise: cheat (mild) — I'm told I did well and never why.

## From memory (asked on the screen)
- **Greta Brook:** 66, no work listed. I found her door by guessing. She said John let her go and that she fell on the steps in May and was off a month, calm, on the record. She "couldn't say" whether the two were linked, and told me about feeding gulls on the quay. I know least about her of anyone I've written about.
- **Maisie Myers:** 39, stacks boards, came from the school in 1989. A 3/3 source who tells me things every week and never lets me use a word of it. Named the witnesses, dropped the fall, pointed at Neil's car and the Una row. Now she wants to run harbour day.
- **John Holmes:** 44, foreman, papers under his arm at the office door. "We parted company," "nothing more to say," "I've said all I'm saying." A wall in three sizes. Had words with Una Waller in January 1990.

## Pull
5 — A skipped year and a new face at the mill tug at me, but the Greta case is dead and I've still nothing to lose.

## Thinking aloud
Gary Hughes came to the garage in February 1990, left it by July (Sally, not for print) and now stands beside Maisie at the sawmill. He's the only new man since Greta, and new men haven't been taught the wall. I won't assume he took her place; I'll ask. I've also got one thing I saw and never played: Ken and Toby had words in November and "you were there". That is the only thing in this year that touches the people I'm closest to, so I want it. Trevor Lane's January row is with Audrey, Ken's mother, so that one waits for Ken.

I'm still looking for a risk the game can punish. A true, pointless week costs 16 copies, which is small.

## Prediction
Gary Hughes will talk, and for print, because he's new and has nothing to protect. He'll say John hired him at the office door and that the place had been open a good while. He won't know why. Why: the wall is John's, and a hire is a plain fact.

## Needs
- Where "the steps" are. The yard has none.
- What the November words between Ken and Toby were about, since I was there.
- What moved trust 7 to 9 and patience 7 to 8 over the year.
- Whether a year-skip kills an old thread or I can still pull Greta's.
- What Gary Hughes did between the garage and the mill.
- Whether the retired Susie is still free to ask by telephone.

## Turns
1. Thursday 23 May, eleven, the yard: Talk to Gary Hughes. (a) What's your work here, and since when? (b) Whose place did you take? (c) May I print that, in your name? — Expect: he answers plainly, names a date, maybe not a person.
2. Friday 24 May, morning: Talk to Maisie Myers, on the record, about harbour day. (a) What's planned and where on the quay? (b) What's different from last year? (c) May I print it in your name? — Expect: she's keen. This is a front I can run safely, and I want to see what working a 3/3 source buys.
3. Saturday 25 May or Monday 27 May: if Gary gave a name or a place, Talk to that person. If not, Talk to Toby about November, off the record. — Expect: Toby gives a short version and a "drop it".
4. Wednesday 29 May, the edition, with ifs. IF Gary gave a plain date and name: Front: the new hand at the sawmill, dates only, no cause. Inside: harbour day, Maisie in her name. IF Gary is dry: Front: harbour day. Inside: Greta in a line, or Susie's raffle tip, which is for print. Nothing on Sally's health, the Baker sick weeks, Neil's car, or Trevor's row until Ken has seen it.
