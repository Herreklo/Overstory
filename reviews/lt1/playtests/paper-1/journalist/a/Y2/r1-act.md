# Round 1

## Pull
6 — A Wednesday deadline, three goes for three pages, and a boss who has told me where the traps are. But I'm a blank reporter again, and I don't yet see a choice with teeth beyond who I visit first.

## Thinking aloud
Ken's family speech is nine names in one breath, and two of them are Toby (Toby Tanner the reporter, Toby Waller at the clinic). I'll need a cheat sheet. It also tells me the story will eventually brush one of them, and that his rule will cost me when it does. The tip is the real hook. Susie hands me a story about her own son's workplace and marks it "not for print: Neil's my son". So the only way to print it is to get it from someone who will say it on the record, and Neil is standing twenty feet away feeding a log through a saw. I won't burn him by quoting the tip.

The structure interests me most: three goes, one front page and two inside pages, and no notices or harbour day (Susie has those). Do I spread, one go per page, or dig and fill the inside with thin stuff? That is a real trade-off. Sales 850 is a floor, not a goal, so the only upside is trust 7. I can't yet see what a scoop is worth against a safe week. I also half-recognise Tanner, Andy and Colin from another harbour-town game. I'll ignore that and play what's on my screen.

## Prediction
John Holmes will confirm Greta Brook is gone but call it something other than a sacking ("her choice", "short of work"), and he will say the reason is not for print. Why: a foreman with papers in hand and a reporter in the yard is already guarded, and nobody here stopped work for me.

## Needs
- Whether I have a name, a past or a reputation in town, or I'm only "the new one" again.
- What moves a source's trust from 2 to 3, and what drops it. Tone? Order of questions? Telling them what's for print up front?
- Where Greta Brook lives, and whether I can find a person without a turn.
- What a good front page earns against a safe one (sales, trust, patience).
- Whether Susie's conflict of interest (her son at the mill) counts under Ken's rule, and whether I should tell Ken.
- Whether I can ask Toby or Susie things at the office for free, or whether that's a turn.

## Turns
1. Thursday morning, the yard: Talk to John Holmes. Three questions, plainly: (a) Is Greta Brook still working here, and since when? (b) What happened, and who was there? (c) Can I print that, in your name? — Expect: the bare fact is for print, the why is not, and I leave knowing who was there.
2. Thursday afternoon: Talk to Greta Brook, if John's answer or the yard tells me where she is. If I can't find her, Talk to Maisie Myers instead, because she's neither the foreman, nor my tipster's family, nor (as far as I know) a Tanner. — Expect: Greta's account disagrees with John's, and one of them is wrong about when.
3. Friday or Monday, decided after 1 and 2: if two people contradict each other, I Talk to whoever saw it. If the dismissal looks clean, I Read the 1989 volume for the mill and spend the rest of my goes on the two inside pages. — Expect: a witness I can name, so I can mark it "a witness saw it" rather than "heard from".
4. Wednesday: the edition. Front: Greta Brook, written as a plan with "if". Hold anything that rests only on Neil. Inside: whatever the other goes bought.
