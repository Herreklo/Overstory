## Turn 3 · Monday 27 May, afternoon · the sawmill yard

Harry named Maisie Myers, so you go to the mill. John Holmes is at the office door. Sid Tanner, Neil Lyons and Gary Hughes are at the saw; Maisie Myers and Vera Holmes are stacking. Hover: *Maisie Myers, 40, the sawmill.*

> **Maisie Myers, the sawmill, Mon 27 May, 3.10.** Asked if she will be on the quay this year: "I've the harbour day this year, so I'll be on the quay." *(for print)*
> **Maisie Myers, 3.11.** Asked why she handed it on last year: "I'd rather not." *(no comment)*
> **Maisie Myers, 3.12.** Asked if any of it is for print: "Put it in, by all means." *(for print)*

She goes back to the stack.
