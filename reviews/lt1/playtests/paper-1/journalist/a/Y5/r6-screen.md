# Round 6 · what happened

## Turn 1 · Thursday 6 June, late morning · Hannah Tanner's garden

Hannah Tanner is out in her front garden.

> **Hannah Tanner, her garden, Thu 6 June, 11.30.** Asked where in the street she and Sally Baker argued, and at what hour: "I've said what I had to say." *(no comment)*
> **Hannah Tanner, 11.31.** Asked who saw it: "I'll not say." *(no comment)*
> **Hannah Tanner, 11.32.** Asked if she would say it again in the paper under her name: "I'd rather not." *(no comment)*

She goes back to her beds.

## Turn 2 · Friday 7 June, afternoon · the garage

Sally Baker is alone in the garage, at the bench. Hover: *Sally Baker, 40, head mechanic, the garage.*

> **Sally Baker, the garage, Fri 7 June, 3.15.** Asked if it is so that she and Hannah Tanner argued in the street on Wednesday: "We argued in the street, yes." *(for print)*
> **Sally Baker, 3.16.** Asked if it is so that she and Mark Holmes had words in the Anchor last October: "Mark Holmes and I had words at the Anchor, back in October. Sylvia Waller and I had a dance there last month." *(for print)*
> **Sally Baker, 3.17.** Asked if there is anything she wants the Courier to print about either: "Print what I said." *(for print)*

She named no witness, so turn 3 stays with Greta.

## Turn 3 · Saturday 8 June, morning · Greta Brook's shed

The shed door is open. Greta Brook is at the bench. Hover: *Greta Brook, 67.* No work listed.

> **Greta Brook, her shed, Sat 8 June, 10.05.** Asked if she had a fall on the steps in May last year: "I'll not go into it." *(no comment)*
> **Greta Brook, 10.06.** Asked if she was laid up a month: "I was off my feet a while. I was off sick a few days last month." *(for print)*
> **Greta Brook, 10.07.** Asked if that was before John Holmes let her go: "That was May. John let me go in June." *(for print)*

She turns back to the bench.

## Turn 4 · Wednesday 12 June · the edition

Nobody denied it, so the front is Hannah and Sally.

- **Front · Hannah Tanner and Sally Baker argued in the street.** "Sally Baker and I argued in the street yesterday," Hannah Tanner told the Courier on Thursday *(heard from someone named: Hannah Tanner).* "We argued in the street, yes," says Sally Baker, head mechanic at the garage *(heard from someone named: Sally Baker).* Neither gave a cause, and the story gives none.
- **Inside · Greta Brook, in her own words.** "I was off my feet a while. I was off sick a few days last month." Asked if that was before John Holmes let her go: "That was May. John let me go in June" *(heard from someone named: Greta Brook).*
- **The other inside slot:** Toby's notices.
- **Not printed:** Sally's words on Mark Holmes and Sylvia Waller (your plan named the Hannah and Sally story only); nothing from Sid.

Ken reads the dummy at the make-up table. Nothing in it names his family. He strikes nothing.

**Your prediction:** Sally confirmed the argument in one flat line and gave no cause. On Mark Holmes she said yes in her own words, and added a dance with Sylvia Waller that you hadn't asked about.

# The next five weeks · 13 June to 18 July 1991

**The edition of 13 June sold 925.** Ken wrote it under 848 and said nothing about the front. Readers' trust stood at 8, his patience went to 8. On the source list, Kate Holmes, Olive Shaw, Sid Tanner and Hannah Tanner now stood at 0 of 3; from then on each said "no comment" to anything you asked.

**20 June.** Nothing new came into the tip book. You went back to Mark Holmes about October: "No comment." Olive Shaw, behind the bar: "No comment." You ran Sally Baker's own line as the front, "Mark Holmes and I had words at the Anchor, back in October," with Olive's line from your notebook beside it. It sold **938.** Trust 9, patience 9.

**27 June.** Saturday 22 June you were on the quay for the harbour day. It ran smoothly, with Maisie Myers in charge, and you saw it. It went on the front. **872.** Trust 10.

**4 July.** On Saturday 29 June, first thing, a telephone message from Greta Brook: "I had a bad fall on the steps yesterday." You called that afternoon: "I'll not go into it." Her message went on the front in her own words, with nothing about the sawmill. Toby had the week's other news on his page: Gary Newman has come to the town to work at the garage, with Lynn Newman. **920.** Patience 10.

**11 July.** Nothing came into the tip book. You held nothing, and the front was the notices moved up. **849.** Ken said nothing.

**18 July.** Susie Lyons rang on Friday: "Leo and Colin Berry sang a duet at the Anchor." *(not for print: her partner)* It named Colin Berry, so you held it. The front was thin again. **853.**

At the end of the five weeks: sales 925, 938, 872, 920, 849, 853; readers' trust 10 of 10; the editor's patience 10 of 10. No correction ran. The county paper carried nothing from the town.
