## Thinking aloud
The win condition was trust 7 by the end of the month. I'm on it a week early, and the only thing that can undo it is a mistake the game has never shown me how to make. So the game is whatever I make of it. Three people shut the same door in one line each. Two things came through instead: Lily's October, and Holmes's "everyone in the yard saw it". The one yard witness I know is Sid Tanner, who hands me a tip every Thursday and whom I have never asked a single question. I'll meet him away from the mill, since distance from coworkers is what loosened Lily. I'll read the record first. It is the one source that doesn't shut a door.
