## Turn 2 · Friday 8 June, morning · the back room, the 1989 volume

Two items from the council office all year.

> **22 June 1989 · A new clerk for the council office** · *By Susie Lyons.* Lily Lyons, 32, has moved to the town to take up a post at the council office. She starts this week. **Born.** To Alex and Lisa Myers, a son, Colin: a brother for Tom.

> **14 December 1989 · Festival on the quay a washout** · *By Susie Lyons.* Saturday's festival on the quay, put on by Terry Sutton, 62, of the council office, was a washout. Mr Sutton first spoke of it in May. Ken Scott, Sylvia Waller and Sam Brook were in it with him, and Eddie Lane, Peter Scott, John Holmes and Harry Myers each had a part. "Every one of them did their bit," he told the Courier.

Around Lily's arrival: **15 June 1989 · Harbour day runs smoothly** (*Toby Tanner*): Audrey Scott, 67, organised it and was in charge from the first table to the last. That month's notices: Eddie Lane in the Courier with the biggest cod of the season.

## Turn 3 · Monday 11 June, morning · watching the council office

Lily Lyons and Sylvia Waller are first in, a little before nine, both with their hair still damp. Jonah Scott comes in after them, then Terry Sutton, who goes to his room. Three callers come in through the morning, one at a time; Lily sees each at the counter and each goes. Hover gives them only as *a caller*. Nobody raises a voice. Nothing else happens there.

# The Courier · Thursday 14 June 1990 · nine in the morning

The bundles by the door are this week's. **Front:** *Greta Brook leaves the sawmill.* **Page 3:** *A year at the council office.* Elsewhere, **A new head mechanic at the garage** (*Toby Tanner*): Trevor Lane, 63, has retired from the garage; his place as head mechanic goes to Sally Baker, 39, who came to the town in April and started at the garage, bringing her husband, Terry Baker, and the children; Terry Baker has started at the clinic; Mr Lane says he will not miss the cold mornings. And **Harbour day "went off well enough"** (*Susie Lyons*): Harry Myers of the school was in charge, had it in hand from May, and the quay was cleared by dark; he has offered to help whoever does it next year.

Ken has the sales sheet. "Nine hundred and forty." He writes it in the planner and turns the page to 21 June. Toby is out. Susie is at the window with the notices.
