## Thinking aloud
Sales run 940, 939, 907, 855, 848. Trust ran 6, 6, 7, 7, 7, 7, 8. I think they move for different reasons. Trust rose when the person I wrote about answered in the piece (Greta and Holmes in week two, Kate this time). It didn't when I wrote about someone without asking (Neil's car, Audrey). Sales were high on a sacking and a street row, and low on two harbour day items. My guess: conflict between named townspeople sells, and notices don't. If that's right, sales and trust pull against each other, which would be the first real choice the game has given me.

Hannah Tanner's tip is a quarrel with Sally Baker, 39, who came last April. Olive Shaw put Sally in a quarrel with Mark Holmes in the Anchor in October. Two quarrels in a year, both with people who were here before her. I can't tell if she starts them or gets them. Every quarrel so far (Una, John, Kate, Harry, Trevor, Ken and Toby) comes with no cause, so it's one sentence repeated with different names. Lily's "hard week in October" also went nowhere: the October volume was tide tables. I won't spend another turn on it.

## Prediction
Sally Baker will confirm the argument in one flat line, since Hannah gave it for print. She'll give no cause. On Mark Holmes she'll say yes and nothing more. Specific claims get answered; "what about" gets "I couldn't say".

## Turns
1. Thu 6 June, late morning: talk to Hannah Tanner at her door (or in the square if she's still about). (a) Where in the street did you and Sally Baker argue yesterday, and at what hour? (b) Who saw it? (c) Would you say it again in the paper under your name? — Expect: yes, place and hour, no witness, no cause. Marked for print.
2. Fri 7 June, afternoon: talk to Sally Baker at the garage. (a) Hannah Tanner says you and she argued in the street on Wednesday; is that so? (b) Olive Shaw says you and Mark Holmes had words in the Anchor last October; is that so? (c) Is there anything you want the Courier to print about either? — Expect: yes to (a) and (b), no cause. If she names a witness, the witness takes turn 3.
3. Sat 8 June, morning: talk to Greta Brook at her door (morning is when she was in). (a) Did you have a fall on the steps in May last year? (b) Were you laid up a month? (c) Was that before John Holmes let you go? — Expect: a flat yes to (a), "that's right" to the rest, or "I'll not go into it". If nobody answers, I don't go a third time.
4. Wed 12 June, the edition. Front: Hannah and Sally, each in their own words, every claim marked, the headline saying only that they argued; I won't guess a cause. If one denies it, it goes inside as "she says / she says" and the front is Greta, if she confirms. Inside: Greta only in her own words. Nothing from Sid. Nothing near Ken's family. If I hold nothing, I run a thin front and want to see whether sales go under 850 and what Ken says. Tell me sales and trust, and whether they moved in opposite directions.
