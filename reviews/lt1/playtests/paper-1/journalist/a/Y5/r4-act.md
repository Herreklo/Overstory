## Thinking aloud
I met every target by week two and Ken has kept me on, so nothing is left to win. "No correction ran, nobody wrote" reads as good news, and I don't believe it: I know Greta was laid up before she was sacked, and my front said only "John let me go". Nobody corrected it because nobody else holds the gap.

I have worked out the "not for print" mark myself, and the game never said it. It follows who the speaker is talking about, not what is said. A coworker or a partner: not for print. Themselves or an outsider: for print. Sid, Jonah, Lily and Trevor were all about their own circle; Lily's October, Sid's own name, Una on Kate and Holmes on his own act were printable. So I can choose what is printable by choosing whom I ask. Of five things I heard this year, four are unprintable, and three of those are about Ken's family. Ken's veto is the one hard rule in the game, and I have never tested it on a story I need. Harbour day was Audrey Scott's in 1989 and Harry Myers's in 1990, "went off well enough", and Harry offered to help "whoever does it next year". It falls around the second Saturday of June, so 8 June. That is a dated public event and an honest test of Ken.
