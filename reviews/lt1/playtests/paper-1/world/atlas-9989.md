## Before you run it

1. **Never hint at a private act nobody found out.** Harry Myers's trip money (e-6385, e-6913), Susie Lyons's expenses (e-6445), Eddie Lane's till (e-6609), Colin Berry's medicine cupboard (e-6912), Eric Lane's catch (e-7425, e-7726), the timber account taken from by Sid Tanner, Sarah Lane and Leo Holmes (e-8392, e-8488, e-8675), Gary Newman's till (e-8795). 2. **Confidences have no content.** "Finally told Ben Reed what had been kept back" never says what. Don't fill it in.
3. **Names repeat and surnames lie.** Two Evan Newmans (p-7, died 1977; p-81, the Anchor's manager), two Amelia Wallers (p-13, died 1969; p-92, born 1987). Check kin in the table.
4. **Rule 8.** The run has married couples of the same sex (Lily and Hannah Tanner, married 1949; Arthur Eden and Ryan Vaughan, 1953), and Owen Lane's parents are Karen Holmes and Rose Lane. Re-dating puts these in the 1940s and 50s. State them plainly, as the record does: never a secret, a reveal, gossip or a source of trouble. Nothing in the run treats anyone by sex or origin; add nothing that does.
5. **Template wording is not a fact to explain.** "A better player than the one who left" when nobody had left (e-6828, e-8830); "retired" for stepping down at 34 (Peter Lane, e-7137); "Oliver Brook and Andy Shaw became friends" dated the month of Andy's death, after it (e-8901); a promise to help a six-year-old "with the roof" (e-8251). A cause label can misname its event: e-8450's label says Gary Myers was found out at "the till", but e-7880 says the trip money. Trust event text over labels.
6. **The summer festival is not in summer.** Its nights fall in December 1989, October 1991, May 1993, September 1996 and March 1998. Keep the engine's month; call it "the festival on the quay" where a December summer festival would jar.
7. **Quirk lines are not acts.** "People have noticed:" and "Typical:" lines (the boathouse door painted three times) show a person, not something done to a thing.
8. **What the export lacks.** No day or hour; no place for an event except the words in its text (the Anchor, the sawmill, the school, the church hill, the co-op); no events before 1989 except as cause labels (e-3672, the skipper's job; e-2477, Andy Shaw's café); habits show only their latest start and break (their history is in `habit:` events); jobs and lead flags are January 1999's and carry the date the person joined, not when they took charge (use the work column below).
9. **Deaths are natural only.** Four in the run, all public. Work accidents and the 1996 storm hurt nobody; there is no violence and no crime beyond small dishonesty at work.
10. **Still drinking at the end:** Sam Brook (from 1993), Mark Holmes (from 1997), Stella Lane (from December 1998). Starting is private; stopping is local.
11. **Births the past generator got wrong.** Thea Waller's Hal and Stella Lane are born in May and August 1979, and Sylvia Waller's Amelia and Ruth Waller in April and September 1987: three and five months apart. Give their ages by the year and, if it matters, treat each pair as twins (`authored`); never print the months. Hal and Stella are Eric Lane's children, but the run has no partner record between Thea and Eric: don't invent a marriage.
12. **The Courier is inside the secrets.** Susie Lyons (to 1991) and Colin Berry (1991 to 1998) took from its expenses, and the editor's wife and father-in-law are named in the public washout of 1993 (e-7499). A paper concept's paper cannot report on itself honestly.
13. **The casting is ours.** Houses A to E and the homes on the Gully Steps and Harbour Row are cast, authored. A why-probe may say who lives there; it never cites the engine for it, and no cause is drawn from it.

## Six corners

Circles to hand to different players, so one lucky story doesn't carry a concept. Each is a household or circle of nine, with almost no one in two corners. *Weight* is the events its people are in (inner: with two of them) and how many are public. *Opening* is the situation a player could find in 1989.

1. **Andy Shaw's friends: Driftwood and the Gully Steps.** Andy Shaw, Ben Reed, Olive Shaw, Trevor Lane, Audrey Scott, Oliver Brook, Edward Baker, Karen Holmes, Sarah Lane. *Opening:* an old man on the harbour steps at seven, Olive just split from Matt Sayer, Karen avoiding Olive, Oliver Brook first in at work every morning from B. *Carries:* songs and credit, debt, illness, a death, a feud of 27 years mended (storylines 2, 3, 4, 20). *Weight:* 521 events, 163 inner, 58 public (the most). *Cast:* B, Gully Steps, Harbour Row. *Suits:* band, mystery (Andy's death), family. *Beware:* the richest corner; a player here has an advantage.
2. **The quay elders: the festival committee and the Night Shift.** Terry Sutton, Mona Sayer, Kate Holmes, Larry Baker, Sam Brook, Tara Myers, Mark Holmes, Mary Tanner, Greta Brook. *Opening:* Terry announcing the 1989 festival; long marriages going quiet; Kate's songs in the Night Shift. *Carries:* civic success and failure, the expense claims, betrayed confidences, strokes, drink, a death in 1997 (storylines 12, 13, 14). *Weight:* 604, 196 inner, 30 public; mostly private. *Cast:* the festival ground, the moorings (Sam's, Mary's). *Suits:* family, rpg, journalist.
3. **The Courier.** Ken Scott, Sylvia Waller, Toby Tanner, Colin Berry, Thea Waller, Susie Lyons, Steve Baker, Toby Waller, Grace Waller. *Opening:* a three-person paper; the editor's future wife at the council; Colin Berry at the clinic, letting Toby Waller take his blame. *Carries:* small dishonesty and blame at work, credit taken, a marriage of rows, the washout naming the editor's family (storylines 6, 7, 14's washout). *Weight:* 475, 184 inner, but only 14 public: most of it must be found out, not read. *Cast:* D (Ken's inheritance). *Suits:* journalist, mystery, rpg.
