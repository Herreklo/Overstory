# Y3: my games

## A paper version of Stardew Valley

Marks: engaged on all nine turns, with lots of small delights. Pull 7, then 8, 8, and 8 at the exit.

What kept me: people who answered what I did. Pam's birthday leek ("Nobody knows it's my birthday"), and later the same day she called me "leek kid". Evelyn's tulips, Gus's tip about Shane, a room of small lives in the Saloon. The field full again, and a coop I was clearly saving towards. The writing turned watering and chopping into something calm, not bookkeeping.

What was hollow: every prediction came true, except one that went wrong in my favour. Nothing went wrong, so by the end the world felt like it was agreeing with me. Turns compressed into "clear until 6 pm" left me few small choices. The Saloon evening was something I watched, not played, because my turns were written before I saw who was there. The first screen was a wall of numbers. The session ended just before the payoff (ordering the coop).

Compared with others: this is my first game, so it's the baseline. It was warmer with people than the real Stardew, and it lost the hands-on feel and the small accidents.

## Saltlight

Marks: engaged and curious at first. Then would quit, cheat and confused from round 2 on, with delights scattered through (Admiral the crab, Ivy's sailor, Sal's lantern jar). Pull 7, 6, 4, then 3 at the exit. I wouldn't buy it at $14.99.

What kept me: the places. A handrail worn bright in the middle, the paint at step 20 peeling in layers like tree rings, a chandler's window rubbed clear at a child's nose height, my room in the rain. Things looked used and lived in, and they made me wonder more than the people did.

What was hollow: four times the screen swapped my own words for a menu line, even on the one question that mattered (why Rob doesn't talk to passengers). The only small job, the darned cardigan, was locked to a hook, and four people brushed it off with the same answer. Belonging rose whatever I did. My room filled with things I didn't choose. Nobody needed me, and I risked nothing.

Compared with Stardew: that one agreed with me too much. This one didn't listen at all. It was prettier and much emptier.
