# Y4 memory

## Return of the Obra Dinn (paper version), the barque *Hesper*, May 1812

Marks: mostly engaged, with delight at small planted items. Wrong guesses (Carr in irons, the bright key) sent me somewhere better, and none felt like a cheat. Pull stayed 8 or 9 throughout, and 8 at the end.

What kept me: bodies that cross-check memories (slit coat and knife, nose stitch and sailmaker, cotton twine and the cook's needle), voices that name people, and loose ends I could chase (the cat that vanishes, the empty peg, the key out of reach, the ice trip at first light).

What was hollow: no confirmation after three rounds, because writing a fate cost a turn out of three and I kept spending turns on looking. The game master hands over a full inventory when I ask to look, so I never scan and spot things myself.

Compared with other games: this is my first game, so there is nothing to compare it with yet. Against the real Obra Dinn it is denser in clues per turn but lacks free fate-writing and the confirm-in-threes rhythm.

## Saltlight, Wrenmouth, October 1994 (Chapter One: Arrivals)

Marks: curious or engaged on the first inspects (the whitewashed chandler's with its stopped 1988 calendar, the Marigold's one glossy plank), then bored at the end. Pull went 6, 7, 7, and 5 at the exit.

What kept me: dated, physical leftovers (the rope still on its hook, pale tin edges on empty shelves) and a guess of mine that turned out wrong in an interesting way. Admiral the crab, Rob not looking up.

What was hollow: Belonging paid +13, +14 and +23 for visiting, not for working anything out. The journal writes my opinions for me. Two characters repeated lines word for word. "No wrong answers" gave my deductions nothing to be right against, and the chapter ended on a lantern-walk cutscene I only watched.

Compared with Obra Dinn: that game asked a question every turn and let me be wrong. This one never asked. It has a better-made town than the Hesper had, and less for an explorer to do in it. I would try one more chapter, not buy it yet.
