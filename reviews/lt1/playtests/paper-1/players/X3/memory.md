# Memory (X3, cozy player)

## Game 1: A paper version of Stardew Valley

**Pull:** 7 in round 1, 8 in rounds 2 and 3, 8 overall at the exit.

**Marks:** engaged almost throughout. Delights: the robin watching me pick salmonberries, Pam's gruff thanks for a birthday leek, the unseen green thing in the Community Center, the music behind Robin's shut door, the rain morning with the cauliflowers open and Jodi's letter. One small cheat when the first berries were walked past; one real cheat when the afternoon was played for me and I ended at 10/270 energy with the stone still short. Planting alone was bored.

**What kept me:** people who answer in their own voice, small kind acts that visibly pay off (a leek, a daffodil, a cauliflower for Jodi), a routine with a satisfying overnight tally, and loose threads I wanted to follow.

**What was hollow:** nothing could go wrong, so my predictions all came true. Everything (calendar, friendship numbers, prices) was handed over up front, so there was little to find out. Chores resolved in a sentence, so I never felt the clock or my own mistakes. The Flower Dance gate read as a wall until Lewis explained it.

**Compared with others:** this is my only game so far, so no comparison yet. Against the real Stardew, it keeps the tone and loop but loses the hands-on detours and surprise.

## Game 2: Saltlight (Chapter One: Arrivals)

**Pull:** 7 at the start, 7, 5, then 5 at the exit.

**Marks:** engaged and curious early (Wilf's rag, the chandler's calendar stuck at March 1988). Bored and cheated in the middle: my own typed words were replaced by menu lines three times, and "You can't take that with you" blocked the cardigan and a jam jar. The lantern walk was a delight.

**What kept me:** the prose, Wilf, Ivy's sailor story, the red tiles stopping at the party wall, the lantern walk.

**What was hollow:** the same lines repeated (June's tea, "a visitor's" from four people), a Pennock answer that was a non sequitur, Belonging rising about 18 a round whatever I did, a room I never saw, 1 sketch and 0 keepsakes after a chapter, no stakes so my kindness cost nothing.

**Compared with Stardew:** Stardew gave me things to spend and lose, and people who answered what I asked. Saltlight is prettier on the page but has no handle. I would not buy it yet.
