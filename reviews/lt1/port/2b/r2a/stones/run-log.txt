## 2026-10-23, the step's readback and the import's merge
- Stone.Load reads a stone as the Blender builder exports it: the FBX and sidecar in Generated/, L0 and L1 found by the
  sidecar's object names, the bounds checked against the sidecar's (to 0.1 mm; the skirts run 30 mm under the bed),
  and the bed (y = 0 for a bed-* pivot) set on the ground by Kit.Place, not the mesh's lowest point.
- Readback -parts step on the installed step.fbx (md5 7bfb5b4af394) fails 4 checks (logs/readback-step.txt). Unity's
  FBX import merges the corners of one FBX control point when their UVs lie within about 1e-3 and their normals
  within about 1.3 degrees. With weld on or off, and with mesh optimisation on or off, L0 gives 411 vertices from 432
  corners and L1 193 from 207. A merged corner takes a neighbour's channels:
  - L0's edge distances are up to 0.665 mm off in 3 triangles, and 1 normal is 0.54 degrees off;
  - L1 has 2 normals up to 1.29 degrees off, and 6 triangles have a slot that is neither their own edge nor an arris's
    distance.
- The proof of the fix: the same file re-exported in a Blender batch, every triangle on its own three control points,
  through stone.py's export settings (scratchpad; nothing in the repo touched). With weld off it reads back with every
  check passing (logs/readback-step-test.txt): L0 432 vertices, its normals 0.000 degrees off and its edges within
  0.00012 mm; L1 207 vertices, its normals exact and its arris slots within 0.023 mm. Asked of the coordinator for
  stones.py's make_object. The harness sets weld off on the generated file's .meta, which is git-ignored.
- L1's slot check follows the widened contract. A slot is either its triangle's own edge, held at exactly one corner
  at that corner's height (the import may reorder the corners, so the slot travels with its corner), or the distance
  in the plane to one of L1's turned edges (1 degree or more), to 0.05 mm. A masked slot must be an arris's. The
  export projects into the face's plane, a hair off the triangle's own: the step's worst is 0.023 mm, where L1's
  pixel is 5 mm or more.
- L1's chamfer codes run 7 to 12 mm (the mean of the kept legs per triangle); L0's are 12 mm throughout.

## 2026-10-23, the wall stone and the coping read back; the contexts wired

- The orientation check is fixed. It found a stone's top by summing its kind-0 triangles. The wall stone's and the
  coping's tops are beds (kind 3), so both failed on their first readback. The check now has two parts:
  - **The frame:** the bounds against the sidecar's, in its pivot (bed-front-left).
  - **The winding, from the geometry:** each triangle's plane must lie in front of the drawn faces' area-weighted
    mean, as SN1's convex block has it. The tread's top (kind 0) is the one exception: wear dishes it, so it is
    concave. Each of its triangles must face up instead, and their mean must lie within 25 degrees of up.
  - **Result:** all three stones pass at both levels, 0.000 mm off their sidecars' bounds. The nearest planes lie
    22.7 / 21.8 mm (the wall stone, L0 / L1), 22.6 / 21.9 mm (the coping) and 95.1 / 106.6 mm (the step) in front
    of the mean.

  three vertices, as the FBX route requires:
  - **The wall stone** (wl-gully/fl-1/r/pn-4/bank/co-21/sp-1, 398 x 157 x 103 mm, the face battered at 1/6): L0 has
