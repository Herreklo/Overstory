The FBX route's check, seen to bite (R2a stones, Blender; 23 October)

Every `stones.py export` writes the specimen's L0 a second time on the part's own vertices (shared control
points, the first export's way) to a scratch FBX, reads it back with the same check, and fails the export
unless the check refuses it. The result is kept in the sidecar as readback.sharedProbe.

Export of step.fbx 04615df7f74b3455 (stones.py export, 23 October):
  sharedProbe.refused: readback step_L0_shared: 89 control points for 432 corners, 430 corners on 87 shared
  ones: Unity's import would merge them

The installed file, read back by the same check:
  step_L0: 144 triangles, 432 corners, 432 control points, "three a triangle, its own, none shared";
           positions exact, normals flat (own error at most 3.2e-6), all eight UV layers bit for bit
  step_L1:  69 triangles, 207 corners, 207 control points, "three a triangle, its own, none shared";
           positions exact, normals flat (own error at most 1.0e-6), all eight UV layers bit for bit
