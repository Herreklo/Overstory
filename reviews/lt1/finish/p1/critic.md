# Painted finish P: the cold critic (Fable, blind to our verdicts, 28 October)

Judged against the painted references (Hopper 03, Stålenhag 13a and 13d, Dredge 07) and the story, with photo 09 for the facts only.

**Verdict: far.** "Not a painted surface. A clean CG clapboard kiosk with a grey mud mask over its lower third."

**The read at a glance** (the street stop, 2 s): a small new white shed, two planks over a window, wet or muddy at the bottom. "Boarded" lands. "Old" and "three winters empty" do not: the upper two-thirds is immaculate.

**Faults, worst first:**
- **Value design.** Four equal horizontal stripes (the white wall, the grey foot, the dark plinth, the dark roof), with no hierarchy and no big shape. The foot band runs round the corner at one height like a dado.
- **The wall is still CG.** The laps repeat with identical hard lines. The near brushwork reads as a compression artefact. In sun the wall blows out to flat white.
- **The flake marks** are flat grey blobs with aliased edges, laid across the lap joints as decals. Real paint fails along the grain, from each lap's drip edge.
- **The wash and the peel share one value and one edge,** so their two causes can't be told apart.
- **The boarding:** one flat panel reads as a roller blind. The battens read as trim-white, square and level.
- **The plinth** reads as concrete panels as dark as the roof. **The roof** is the loudest thing from 12 m.
- **At 20 m** the laps make moiré; at 12 m and high, a clean white box with a dirty foot. It never reads as painted at any distance.

**Good:** the low-sun cast shadows of the battens, sill and head, crisp and Hopper-like; the battens' placement and proportion.

**The one change:** three unequal value planes (one gradient down the wall, the window dark, the sun shadow), then one failed-paint shape that follows the water from the sill's ends to the plinth, with the wash and the peel as two distinct marks.

**The limit:**
- **What this approach can reach:** Firewatch and Dredge (flat value planes and few marks on a mid-poly model).
- **What it can't reach:** Stålenhag, by marks baked in UV space. His stroke width is constant on the canvas, while a UV mark looks brushy at one distance only.
- **What reaching him would take:** screen-space edges, a quantised value palette per material, and laps that collapse to two or three strokes at distance.
