# Value plans: the frames' notan, before colour

Each frame in five values, 1 darkest to 5 white, as a painter plans it before colour (`docs/visual-direction.md`, value first). The renders are checked against these in greyscale at one-eighth (`blender/tools/light_sheet.py notan`): the share of the frame in each value, where the darkest dark and the one white fall. A frame passes when its darkest and its white are where the plan puts them and the middle (values 2 to 4) is no more than about sixty percent of it. A fog nocturne is the exception, as Whistler's are: most of it may sit in a narrow middle band (up to about three quarters), so long as it keeps one true dark and one true white.

## 17:50, sea fog

**Camera 3, Gully Steps (the target frame).** Darkest (1): the near unlit walls and plinths at the bottom left and the near ground at the foot of the steps; nothing in the fog may be as dark. White (5): the glass of the near lantern, alone. 4: the wall it washes, and the lamps up the steps, each a step softer and closer to the fog. 2 to 3: the fogged lane beyond and the sky, the sky lighter than the houses against it so the roofline reads. The steps' treads lighter than their risers.

**Camera 1, over the roofs to the Holm and Watch Knoll.** Darkest (1): the shaded planes of the near roofs and the chimneys, a dark frame across the bottom. White (5): the cluster of lit windows and lamps on the Holm, small. 3: the fog on the water and the sky; the water a value below the sky. 2: Watch Knoll and the Fire Watch as one silhouette against the sky, standing out of the fog bank.

**Camera 9, Harbour Row's quay.** Darkest (1): the near quay edge, the water beside it and the foot of the near building. White (5): the lamp at the vanishing point. 4, not 5: the shopfronts, so they lead to the lamp rather than fight it; their light on the wet quay at 3 to 4. 2 to 3: the fog down the row, each house a step lighter.

## 16:00, low sun

**Camera 3, Gully Steps.** Darkest (1 to 2): the lane's shadow at the foot of the walls and the shaded wall on the right. White (5): the sunlit wall facing the sun. The sky (east, away from the sun) a value below the sunlit wall, so the wall reads against it.

**Camera 1, over the roofs.** Darkest (1 to 2): the near roofs in shade, a dark frame. 4: the sunlit roof planes. 3: the far shore in haze, Watch Knoll a step darker than the sky behind it. 4 to 5: the sky towards the sun.

**Camera 10, into the light (new).** From over the Landing, down the lane and across the Basin into the low sun: the houses and the lane as dark silhouettes (1 to 2), the sun's beams across the lane and the haze over the water (4), the sun's glow in the sky (5).
