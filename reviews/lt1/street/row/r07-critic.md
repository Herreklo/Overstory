# r07: cold critic

**Verdict: far.** Three clean architectural models on a grey table under a studio dome: the whites are not white, the red store is black, the sky is a flat card, and nothing has been touched by a person.

## The read at a glance

- **Eye level:** a boarded cottage, a white gabled house, a dark barn behind. Forms right; the cottage reads as grimy, not boarded. No one lives here.
- **Street stop (20–25 m):** the white house and the sky nearly merge (wall 64–74, sky 69); windows are grey patches; 109 is a black mass with white frames.
- **12 m up:** a tidy scale model; roofs one even black texture.
- **High:** three boxes with lids on a uniform floor; the windows are gone (pane p50 71–73 against wall 70).

## Faults, worst first

1. **The overcast value ladder is compressed and keyed low.** Stop still, clay A: sky 69 (sd 0.5), 108's white wall p50 64–74, ground 50, roofs 22, 109's wall 23, window regions p50 55; whole frame p5/p50/p95 = 19/51/71. Normanns gate 27: sky 96, wall p50 86, road 43, roofs 30–40, panes 14–18; whole frame 25/65/96. Nedre Strandgate: wall 78, sky 86, road 75. The photographs put the white house 10 L\* under the sky and 40 above the road; the render puts it level with the sky and 15 above the ground. Nothing but trim exceeds L\* 75; the real street lives between 78 and 97.
2. **107 is painted dirty, not boarded.** Its wall is p50 42–51 at the stop and 42–47 at eye level, 20 L\* under 108 (64–66). Pedersgata 81, derelict and boarded in 1989, has a wall at p50 89–92, the same white as its neighbours; neglect shows in boards (74–83, 10–15 under the wall), broken sashes and open black holes (3). Three years empty does not turn paint grey.
3. **109's ox-red is a black.** Clay wall p50 22.6 against its own roof 15.6; in colour, p50 18 at chroma 12. Real ox red under cloud sits near L\* 30–38 with chroma 25–35. The house reads as tarred.
4. **Window darkness is lost with distance.** Eye level is now right: B/C panes p50 15 against wall 72 (Normanns: 13.5 against 86). At the stop the window region is p50 44–55 with only the darkest 5 % at 14–23; at 12 m the board sheet gives panes 28 against wall 70; high, 45 and 63 against 69. The photo's windows are black holes at 15 m. The sheet's 12 m and high rows show no difference between A, B and C (63/63/63; 71/73/73).
5. **Sky and ground are dead flat:** sky sd 0.5, ground sd 0.6–0.7 in every column, both lights. Every reference sky has a gradient; every road has kerb, wear and wet.
6. **Roofs are one tiled texture:** 108's 22 and 109's 16 at every distance, no ridge, no gutter weight, no moss; the references' roofs are 30–43 with light on the courses.

## Good

- Form holds against the photos: 108's gable proportions, dentil band, bracketed window heads and bargeboard match Normanns gate 27; 109's hoist dormer and tall end match the Strandgate stores; 107's boards nailed over the sashes match Pedersgata.
- The eye-level glass (B/C) is the one measured thing near the reference: pane 15 vs wall 72, a 57-step gap against the photo's 73.
- Low sun's wall ratio is right: 108 lit front p50 79.5, shaded gable 40.7; ground shadow 25–29 against lit 51.

## Against the earlier round

Columns 2 and 5 of r07 are r03's columns 2 and 6 to within 1 L\* in every region measured (sky 69.2/69.2, 108 wall 58.9/58.9, 109 wall 29.9/29.9, colour 108 wall 62.9/62.9). The row has not moved. The glass at eye level has: r03 close-glass window p50 48 against wall 76 (28 steps); r07 B/C 15 against 72 (57 steps). Better at eye level; the same everywhere else.

## The colour still

Not stunning. Overcast (columns 5 and 6, identical in every region): the houses get colour and the world does not; sky 71 at chroma 0, ground 54 at chroma 1.2, 108's white 66 at chroma 1.7, 109's red 18 at chroma 12.7. A tinted model on a grey card. Low sun: sky grey-blue (h 249, C 9), floor tan (h 81, C 8), lit white warmed to h 91, shade cooled to h 257: a product render's recipe, correct and lifeless. Nothing is the colour of Stavanger: no slate sky with light in it, no wet asphalt, no ox red with blood in it.

## Artist or generator?

Generator. What decides it: the same clapboard rhythm and shadow weight on all three houses; 107's smears, the same shape under every window; one roof texture at one density at every distance; sky and ground at sd 0.5; eight windows on 108 with no curtain, plant, blind or sash position to tell a restored family home from a store. Hopper and Sohlberg decide each window; Stålenhag lets one lit window carry the house. Here nothing was decided.

## Feeling

A model on a table. It shows neglect only as grime, and cannot yet show warmth or pride: a white restored house would be pride, but this one is grey (64) and empty. The low sun pass gives a showroom's warmth, not a place's.

## The one change

Re-key the overcast still to the photographs' ladder and give each material its true lightness. Pass, measured on the street-stop clay still (p50 of clean patches): sky 87–93 with a visible gradient (sd ≥ 3); 108's front 80–86, that is 6–12 under the sky; ground 40–46; 107's wall within 8 of 108's, its boards 10–15 under its wall; 109's wall 30–38 and at least 12 above its roof; roofs 28–40; panes ≤ 20 at eye level and ≤ 30 at the stop. Whole-frame p95 ≥ 90.
