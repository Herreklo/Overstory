# 109: the sea house (1850), beside ref 01 and 107

- Bad: in overcast its red is 0.8 L lighter and a little cleaner than the floor's (L25.3, C15.7 against L24.5, C12.8). Its low-sun hue is the light's, and accepted.
- Fixed: the peel under its windows no longer ladders. Each sill fails in its own time. The first lap under it peels most, and the boards below peel less and stop two to five boards down (`../row/r02-close-109.jpg`).
- Holds: it reads as a Stavanger sea house, gable to the quay, built from `seahouse.json`. Its coat is the record's 1960 ox-red, then one derived coat in 1979.
