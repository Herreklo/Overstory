# 109: the sea house (1850), beside ref 01 and 107

- Bad: in low sun its red still reads brick-orange at a glance, as the floor's does. Its paint no longer adds to that: the age washes take the oil's dull red-brown (`wall_oxred.wash`), not the white coat's yellow-grey. The rest is the light: under the 18° sun its red would be L40 C30 h44 even without the sheen. The next lever is the shared sun's tint, which this step did not touch.
- Bad: overcast reads it a lighter, slightly pinker red than the floor's.
- Fixed: the peel under its windows no longer ladders. Each sill fails in its own time. The first lap under it peels most, and the boards below peel less and stop two to five boards down (`../row/l06-close-109.jpg`).
- Holds: it reads as a Stavanger sea house, gable to the quay, built from `seahouse.json`. Its coat is the record's 1960 ox-red, then one derived coat in 1979.
