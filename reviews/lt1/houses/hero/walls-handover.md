**Stopped, 27 October, at the style pause:** none of these changes has been rendered or judged in Unity. They do build: C 45.3k/8.0k, D 56.6k/8.4k and E 40.6k/6.6k triangles, near and street.
**What holds:** the corner boards are now chamfered, and proud by `push.trimProud` (on classical houses too), with the front and back boards running past the corner by that depth. The drip board is a section with a chamfered nose, also pushed. `clad()` now takes explicit `sills` and `butts` (an infill's sides, where a board shows its end grain), and the old behaviour stays the default.
**In progress, not started in code:** C's husmor windows (a thicker sill with nose and drip, a 19 mm lost-trim casing, the old opening's infill laid as its own thinner, narrower 1971 boards through `clad(butts=…)`); D's peel as whole patches in uv6.y, which is free in PaintSurface and House.shader, plus the PaintSurface change to draw it; cutting the plinth from about 16k to 6k near and 0.6k street, and sitting it in its ground (the lead's high-view fault). The plinth is unchanged.
