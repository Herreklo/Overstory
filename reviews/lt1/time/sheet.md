# The corner through the years, in clay (LT1f)

The corner's five houses are built from their diaries at 1989, 2000, 2010 and 2020 by the test-quality generator and set in the heart's clay. Only the year changes: the cameras, the sun (20 October, 15:00 UTC), the seeds and the town around them stay the same. Cold readers (Sonnet) read the frames, the town's record, or frames with the facts shuffled. The full record is in `docs/experiments.md` (LT1f), and the scores are in `readers/scores.md`.

## Look at

1. `sheets/strips-real.png`: the four views across the four years. The frames at the player's size are in `frames/real/`.
2. The three ways of showing time:
   - `ways/toggle/toggle-eye-2010-2020.png` and the others: the toggle in place. These are animated PNGs; open them in a browser or Quick Look.
   - `ways/strokes/strokes-eye.png` and `strokes-12m.png` (animated), with `strokes-*-steps.png` as stills: the view repainted stroke by stroke, each recorded change in the order it happened.
   - `ways/window/window-photo-eye-B-2000-in-2010.png` and `window-pane-12m-D-2000-in-2010.png`: a window into another year, as a pane or as a photograph held at the spot.

## What the readers did

- **Order: right.** 11 of 12 views were put in order.
- **Date: wrong by a century.** All 48 dates fell between 1885 and 1965. Clay has nothing made after 1989 that looks it.
- **Parts: at the bar.** About four in five of the parts readers marked were right (81 per cent against what clay draws).
- **Changes: found three in four.** Boarding, shopfronts and the veranda read at once.
- **Shuffled facts: the control worked.** Scores against the true record fell by 32 points, so readers read what is drawn, not conventions.
- **Against the page: the page won.** All six readers who saw both preferred the record. Clay cannot show paint, care short of boarding, whether a shop is open, copies against originals, or the date.
- **The ways:** repainted stroke by stroke found 7 of 8 changes with no false claim, the dated strip 6 of 8, and the window 3 of 8. That is one reader each, so it is an indication only.

## What it means

Form carries the big changes. The rest comes with the first house parts: coats and wear, shop glass and lettering, a trace on every copy, and parts that look their decade. What owners did after 1989 is a research gap, studied before step 4.

## For you to judge

- Which way of showing time to take further: repainted stroke by stroke (ours, and it read best) or the toggle (the evidence's baseline).
- Whether the photograph held at its spot is worth keeping for close views.
