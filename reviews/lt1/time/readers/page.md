# The corner's record

Five houses on a stepped lane that climbs from a harbour's quay road, in a Norwegian west-coast town. Every dated event the town's record holds for each house, with its cause.

## House A

Where: on the quay road at the foot of the stepped lane, on the left of the lane as you look up it.

- 1912: built: a two-storey house in the jugend (art nouveau) style, with a mansard roof. Cause: a cannery owner builds.
- 1931: a shopfront fitted in the ground floor, across 2 window bays. Cause: the cannery owner's widow lets the ground floor to the quay road as a shop.
- 1931: an ironmonger opens in the shop (Vik Jernvare). Cause: an ironmonger opens.
- 1949: repainted. Cause: upkeep.
- 1974: the shop closes. Cause: the ironmonger retires.
- 1988: a café opens in the shop (Kaffebrenneriet). Cause: a young couple open a café in the empty shop; the rent is low after the crash.
- 1996: repainted. Cause: the café's owners buy the house and paint it, the first coat since 1949.
- 2009: the shop closes. Cause: the café moves to a bigger room on the square; the quay road's trade has gone up the hill.
- 2016: the shopfront taken out and the ground floor made a home, with copies of the windows it had covered. Cause: after the oil-price fall no one rents the shop; the owners make the ground floor a flat, and the city's rules for the wooden town ask for copies of the 1912 windows.
- 2016: repainted. Cause: painted when the ground floor is made a flat.

## House B

Where: on the quay road at the foot of the stepped lane, on the right of the lane as you look up it.

- 1884: built: a storey-and-a-half house in the Swiss chalet style. Cause: a boat-builder builds on the quay road.
- 1935: burned down. Cause: burned down in a chimney fire.
- 1936: built: a two-storey house in the functionalist style, with a flat roof. Cause: rebuilt after the fire, in the new style.
- 1987: repainted. Cause: new owners paint it.
- 1997: a shopfront fitted in the ground floor, across 2 window bays. Cause: the owners let the ground floor as a bakery as trade comes back to the quay road.
- 1997: a bakery opens in the shop (Quay Bakery). Cause: a bakery opens.
- 2003: extended: a balcony over the lane. Cause: the owners add a balcony over the lane.
- 2003: repainted. Cause: painted with the balcony.
- 2017: repainted. Cause: upkeep.

## House C

Where: halfway up, on the left, beside the landing where the lane turns.

- 1879: built: a storey-and-a-half house in the Swiss chalet style. Cause: a cooper builds.
- 1971: windows replaced: all of them. Cause: new windows, cheaply.
- 1998: restored, windows copied from the originals (those on the upper floor). Cause: the upper flat's new owners put back copies of the 1879 windows.
- 1998: repainted. Cause: painted with the upper flat's windows.
- 2011: windows replaced: those at the back. Cause: the ground-floor flat's owner fits factory 'old style' windows in the back wall, the one that faces down the lane.
- 2013: repainted. Cause: upkeep.

## House D

Where: halfway up, on the right, on top of the rock outcrop above the landing.

- 1828: built: a storey-and-a-half house in the classical (empire) style. Cause: a fisherman builds.
- 1950: extended: a lean-to at the back. Cause: a shed.
- 1965: windows replaced: those on the front. Cause: new windows to the street.
- 1986: windows and doors boarded up: all of them. Cause: the owner dies; the heirs live in Oslo.
- 1986: neglected. Cause: empty.
- 1995: neglected. Cause: still empty; the heirs cannot agree to sell, and the roof lets in water.
- 2007: the boards taken down: all of them. Cause: a young family buys the empty house.
- 2007: restored, windows copied from the originals. Cause: they restore it with the city's help: windows copied from the old ones left in the gables.
- 2007: repainted. Cause: painted when it is restored.

## House E

Where: at the top of the lane, the small house that closes the view up it.

- 1826: built: a storey-and-a-half house in the classical (empire) style. Cause: a fisherman builds.
- 1965: windows replaced: those on the front. Cause: new windows to the street.
- 1993: neglected. Cause: the last owner dies; the heirs live away and leave the house empty.
- 2008: windows and doors boarded up: all of them. Cause: boarded after a break-in.
- 2015: neglected. Cause: still empty.
