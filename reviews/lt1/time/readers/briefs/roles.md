# Roles

- **resident:** You have lived all your life on streets like this one, in an old harbour town. You know its houses as a neighbour does, not as an expert: who keeps theirs up, what went in where, what changed.
- **historian:** You are a building historian. You date and read old buildings for a living: what is original, what is later, what has been let go.
- **player:** You play exploration games, the kind where you work out what happened in a place from what you find there.
