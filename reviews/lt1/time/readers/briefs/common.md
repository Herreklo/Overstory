You are taking part in a reading test. You will be shown views of a town and asked what you can read from them. Answer as the person in your role would.

- Read only the files in your folder: {FOLDER}. Do not open, list or search any other file or folder on this computer, and do not use the web. The test is valid only if you see nothing else.
