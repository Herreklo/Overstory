# Step 3, R2: the date, the history include and the page quad

22 October, on the M4 (Metal), Unity 6000.6.6f1. Built in `db34f2a` (the include, the arms, the quad) and the commit that adds this file (the runner's keywords made in `Start`).

What was built:
- **The date.** `_OS_Date` is set by `Overstory.World.FabricDate`. Its x is the year with the month as a fraction, y the day of the year, z the hour, w reserved. `LT1Stone.Stage` sets it to 1989.
- **`Shaders/History.hlsl`:**
  - `OS_History(address, layer)` reads the layer's page from the global array `_OS_PagesRGBA` (P0, P1 and H1, a slice each), through the piece table. The table is a global `StructuredBuffer` read in the vertex stage by UV3's piece index and passed flat.
  - The law is a named stub, `OS_HistoryLaw`, which returns the stored input. It already receives the piece's resets and age.
  - Under `OS_HISTORY_OFF` it reads nothing and returns the material's quiet means.
- **The two arms:**
  - `OS_HISTORY_OFF` (`history-off`): in today's `Stone.shader`, the vertex history interpolator is gone. The six quantities R1 will move to pages (polish, lichen years, moss, water held, the water layer, years since bare) are held at the material's quiet means. The seed, face kind and sky/ground bake are kept.
  - `OS_HAZE_OFF` (`haze-off`): `OS_Atmosphere` returns the lit colour unchanged, in Stone, Street, Tree and AirMotes, its only callers.
- **The quad:** `LT1History`, with `Experiments/LT1/History/HistoryQuad.shader`.

What the table shows:
- **Today's vertex-carried history costs nothing measurable.** All minus history-off is +0.09 ms at eye level and −1.66 ms at the street stop, inside the corner's ±1.5 ms.
- **Haze costs at most about 1.5 ms.** All minus haze-off is +1.55 and −0.02 ms. So the haze march, which `docs/measuring.md` named "the first suspect" for the 12.5 ms of shading, is not where that cost is.
- **The corner is too noisy for a sub-millisecond A/B.** It is GPU-bound at about 40 ms, and its round-to-round spread reaches 16.6%. Sub-millisecond costs have to be measured on the quad or the empty frame, as the pass budget does.

How the keywords are declared:
- Both are global `multi_compile`, because the harness and the golden views turn them on everywhere at runtime (`Shader.SetKeyword`). `shader_feature` would strip them from the player, and a local keyword does not answer a global switch.
- `OS_HISTORY_OFF` is in both stages: off drops the vertex interpolator and the piece-table read.
- `OS_HAZE_OFF` is `multi_compile_fragment`.
- Both are measuring arms and could become `shader_feature` (or be stripped) in a shipping build.

## R0's two Metal questions

- **Does `EditorUtility.CompressTexture` keep our mips?** Yes.
  - Two 256×128 textures share mip 0. One has our own mip 1, the box filter inverted, which no filter of mip 0 makes; the other has the box. After BC7 at Best, both keep 3 mips.
  - Their compressed mip 1 bytes differ.
  - Ours decodes to ours within BC7's error (mean 1.6 to 1.8, max 13 steps), and is 84 to 89 steps (mean) from the box.
  - Three 1024×512 slices with 3 mips compress in 0.19 s.
- **Does Metal copy a `Texture2D` into an array slice?** Yes, for RGBA8 and BC7, every mip.
  - `Graphics.CopyTexture(tex, 0, mip, array, slice, mip)` was used into a non-readable array (`Apply(false, true)`), so nothing CPU-side was drawn.
  - The quad drawn from it is bit-identical to the quad drawn from a CPU-filled array, at 2560, 640 and 160 wide (magnified, about 1:1, and minified to mip 2).
  - `SystemInfo.copyTextureSupport`: Basic, Copy3D, DifferentTypes, TextureToRT, RTToTexture.

All three answers are in `r2-pages.json`, with the page and frame hashes.

## Against R0 and the schemas

- **BC7 error is about four times R0's bound on these pages.**
  - Mip 0: mean 1.8 to 1.9, 99th 6 to 7, max 11 to 15 steps, against R0's 0.46/2/5.
  - Mip 2: 3.7 to 4.9 / 17 to 18 / 27 to 34.
  - Our synthetic pages carry ±5-step grain, uncorrelated in all four channels. That may be harsher than R0's pages; R1's real pages will tell.
  - By R0's own rule (over 2/255, H1 becomes two BC5 pages), these pages would go to BC5.
- **The 4-texel grid keeps BC blocks inside a chart only at mip 0.** A block at mip *m* covers 4·2^*m* texels of mip 0, so at mip 2 it spans two of the quad's 8-texel-high charts. That is the jump in error at mip 2. Keeping three mips block-pure needs a 16-texel grid, or accepting the error at mip 2.
- **The GPU piece entry is 16 bytes, not `chunk-format.md`'s 24.** It holds the first slice, two pairs of u16 resets, and the age (low u16) with flags (high u16). Ids stay on disk.
- **History off holds six quantities, not the whole vertex record.** The seed, face kind and bake stay on the vertices.
- **The quiet means are vertex means** over the gully's FBX meshes (`LT1History.QuietMeans`), set in `LT1Stone`:
  - slate: polish 0.099, lichen years/200 0.237, moss 0.171, held 0.010, years since bare/200 0.569, water 0.375;
  - rubble: 0.023, 0.385, 0.108, 0, 0.555, 0.269.
  - The gully has no granite, so granite takes the slate's until a granite stair is measured.
