## Review 2

### Gully Steps, builds A and B: strict review (crops2)

Two builds are present. **T**: thin low walls, two landings in the city crop, holes with a visible back. **C**: tall thick walls stepping down at the landing, one landing, holes drawn black. In pairs 01–07, A = T and B = C; in pair 08 the labels are swapped (A = C, B = T). Verdicts follow the labels as given.

#### Per pair

**01 (low sun, foot).** Both: every joint between tread blocks is an open black slit; neighbouring blocks in one tread sit up to a third of a riser apart (A 192–197, 75–130; B 638–642, 107–148), reading as careless laying, not wear. B's fallen-stone hole (20–65, 207–300) is a flat black void. **B worse.**

**02 (low sun, on the steps).** A has three wide open joints with lit end faces and a dark cap where the gap opens on the tread (125–135, 192–325; 197–207, 325–480; 97–125, 482–655); B one, narrower (304–325, 372–495). Both walls read laid. **A worse.**

**03 (street stop).** Both wall ends at the foot are hollow, two thin shells with ground between (A 590–670, 540–690 and 1090–1130, 550–685; B 560–655, 540–690 and 1065–1195, 550–685, wider). A's walls are kerb height, one to two risers, yet carry full slab copings; in sun (730–800, 60–160) the upper wall is a line of slates. B's step-downs read built; its hole (955–965, 195–210) is a speck. **A worse.**

**04 (12 m, low sun).** B's hole (341–362, 391–432) is a black void. A's fallen stones came from under a coping that still sits there (325–372, 370–452), so the coping floats; the hole itself reads as a hole. **B worse.**

**05 (city camera).** A's walls read as drawn lines; the floating coping recurs (490–527, 443–500). B's walls hold the silhouette; its void (500–520, 455–485) is a speck. A rests every ~13 treads, B runs 15+ before its first rest. **A worse.**

**06 (drizzle, on the steps).** Flat light exposes the open joints: A four, caps standing out (110–125, 85–190; 325–335, 190–320; 395–410, 324–480; 295–325, 485–660); B one (505–530, 372–495) plus a quarter-riser step between blocks (30–38, 68–162). **A worse.**

**07 (drizzle, foot).** A's void (340–385, 210–300) is the blackest thing in the frame. B's coping floats over a hole (310–367, 157–267); its small hole (15–40, 545–575) reads right; a flat green wedge (362–400, 350–415) reads as a decal. **A worse.**

**08 (blue hour, 12 m).** A's void (382–402, 390–432) and wide hollow end (200–300, 600–720) against B's floating coping (350–400, 370–420) and kerb walls, weighed as in 04. **A worse.**

#### Faults

- **Open black joints between tread blocks**, no fill, no gradation, widening to slots with lit end faces: **both**, wider and more numerous in T (02-A, 06-A above).
- **Holes drawn as pure black voids** in every light: **C only** (01-B 20–65, 207–300; 04-B 341–362, 391–432; 05-B 500–520, 455–485; 07-A 340–385, 210–300; 08-A 382–402, 390–432).
- **Coping floating over fallen stones' gap**: **T only** (04-A 325–372, 370–452; 05-A 490–527, 443–500; 07-B 310–367, 157–267; 08-B 350–400, 370–420).
- **Kerb-height coped walls**: **T only** (03-A, 05-A, 08-B).
- **Hollow wall ends** at the foot: **both** (03 above; 08-A 200–300, 600–720; 08-B 310–340, 650–700), wider in C.
- **Block height steps of a quarter to a third of a riser** in one tread: **both** (01-A 192–197, 75–130; 01-B 638–642, 107–148; 06-B 30–38, 68–162).
- **Lichen on walked tread centres**, hard-edged in grey light: **both** (06-A 560–1000, 130–620; 07-B 560–760, 240–400).
- **The 1961 cement-pointed panel does not read** anywhere: **both**.
- **Shimmer risk**: 2–4 px black slits, 1–2 px nosing highlights, edge-on wall shells: **both**.

#### Verdict

Neither is fit as the reference: open black joints and hollow wall ends fail the bar in both. C is closer. Its one-build defects, black void holes and wider hollow ends, are rendering fixes; its 15+ lower run should be checked against the dozen rule. T's one-build defects are rules: kerb-height walls with copings, and fallen stones drawn from under a coping that stays. Take C (B in 01–07, A in 08) as the base once its holes show stone behind them, then fix joints and ends.

5. The 1961 joint bonded, toothed or straight.

## Review 4

### Gully golden review, packet 4

#### The two builds

**Block build.** Cheek walls twice the other's width, of rounded, pillowy blocks on deep undercut shadows; solid coursed end piers; one rest, where the coping steps up a course and a black cavity opens under the upper coping; heavy pale mottling across the treads. Labels: B in 01–07, A in 08.

**Plate build.** Cheek walls of uniform thin slabs in regular courses with chamfered edges, like stacked slates; wall ends open as two splayed fins under a floating coping; two rests; the coping runs at one height from foot to top; cleaner treads. Labels: A in 01–07, B in 08.

#### Per pair (x0–x1, y0–y1)

- **01.** B: black cavity under the upper coping, no back readable (10–85, 150–260). A: right wall's top end a hollow fan of plates (1040–1095, 0–75); its hole shows rubble (80–125, 200–300). Worse: B.
- **02.** A: joint slots stand above the nosings as black flags (318–332, 327–343; 215–243, 483–520); stacked-plate wall (900–1280, 60–420). B: course lines bow (1000–1280, 50–400). Worse: A.
- **03.** A: both wall feet two fins under a floating coping (590–660, 520–700; 1095–1155, 510–690); top-right end a ladder of plates (860–885, 65–135); no change of wall height (880–1160, 180–420). B: blocks bend like rubber at the step (1013–1057, 273–337). Worse: A.
- **04.** A: fin at the foot (195–245, 630–715); hole a see-through slit (325–370, 375–455); coping half B's width (430–560, 40–240). B: notch at the step (467–487, 133–150); bent blocks (451–511, 100–157). Worse: A.
- **05.** A: fin (370–400, 630–690); right wall a 10 px line (760–940, 360–640). B: the step reads (540–600, 280–330). Worse: A.
- **06.** A: black flags (297–323, 483–700; 110–125, 80–190); slate-stack wall (900–1280, 0–300). B: pillow blocks (900–1280, 0–300); mottling near white across the walking line (600–1000, 280–500). Worse: A.
- **07.** B: cavity under the stepped coping (301–366, 155–255); mottling mid-tread (430–800, 300–480). A: hole with rubble readable (345–420, 200–290); coping end a thin fin (155–175, 335–400); treads blank (400–900, 280–520). Worse: B.
- **08.** B (plate): fin (375–420, 640–715); hole black (530–564, 373–453); wall face flat, no coursing (430–560, 300–650). A (block): notch (667–680, 143–153); bent blocks (640–720, 120–200). Worse: B.

Block build better in six pairs; plate in 01 and 07, only on the block build's cavity.

#### Faults in one build

**Plate build:** hollow wall ends (03-A twice, 04-A, 05-A, 08-B, 01-A; boxes above); wall half the width its role needs (04-A, 05-A); courses as uniform chamfered plates (01-A 980–1280, 0–420; 02-A; 06-A); no height change, so neither the retaining stretch nor the 1961 rebuild is readable (03-A, 04-A, 05-A, 08-B); holes see-through from 12 m (04-A, 08-B); taller joint flags (02-A, 06-A); blank lower-flight treads (07-A).

**Block build:** cavity or notch where the stepped stretch meets the old work (01-B, 07-B, 04-B, 08-A); courses bend through the pitch change instead of stepping or tapering (02-B, 03-B, 04-B, 08-A); blocks read as stacked pillows with no beds (01-B 980–1280, 0–420; 02-B; 06-B); mottling ignores the walking line and goes near white in drizzle (02-B 300–1000, 100–650; 06-B; 07-B); one rest, leaving a 16-riser lower flight (04-B 300–800, 300–700) where the plate build rests every ten: check the set-out's facts.

#### Faults in both

Black slots at tread joints, shorter in the block build (02-B 120–135, 258–380; 06-B 195–210, 265–380). One-pixel bright nosing lines and black slits at the street stop (03-A/B 680–900, 330–500) and orange rims on every course in low sun (02-A/B 900–1280, 60–420) will shimmer. Single bright specks on stone (04-A 498,320; 04-B 716,296; 05-B 952,156).

#### Verdict

Not fit. Carry the block build, but a golden cannot hold a cavity where a mason's joint should be. In order: (1) close the junction of the stepped stretch: a straight vertical joint on solid blocks, the coping stepping on stone; (2) step or taper the courses, never bend them; (3) flatten the block faces and give them beds, no undercut; (4) holes show rubble at eye level; (5) mottling off the walking line, dimmer in drizzle; (6) close the tread joint slots; (7) settle the rests against the set-out; (8) soften nosing lines, slits and specks for motion.
