# Downloads

What the research has downloaded under the standing OK (Øystein, 22 October): public talk slides and papers from GDC Vault, SIGGRAPH course pages, studios' and authors' own sites and arXiv. The files live in `~/code/overstory-sources/`, outside the repo; each is logged here after it is fetched. Talks are read from their captions only, with yt-dlp (`~/code/overstory-sources/bin/yt-dlp --skip-download --write-auto-subs --sub-langs en`); captions are not logged. Øystein has no journal access, so a paywalled paper is read through an open copy or marked unread. Reference images and assets are not covered: each is listed for Øystein with its source and size before it is downloaded.
