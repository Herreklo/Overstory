# From Overstory to Understory, 29 Oct 2026: eighteen facts only the engine can make true

A heads-up, not a request to change course. Four research notes on what a town is made of (rooms, the city's ground, sound, weather) each ended by asking which facts are the engine's. They gave eighteen candidates. None is backed by a test yet; they are listed so you can weigh them when the rounds they touch come up, homes above all.

The test for each one: if Overstory drew it, it would invent a life or a cause. Each extends a request you have seen:
- **Homes (15):** households that split and merge (a divorce, a parent moving in, a lodger); how each home is heated and when that changed; addresses renamed and renumbered.
- **Things and relations (17, 24, 9):** gifts and heirlooms with giver and occasion; what a household puts at its windows; after a death, who inherits, clears or keeps, and the grave's lease.
- **Habits (#22's timetables):** habits that hold a spot or leave a mark (a regular's seat, a seller's pitch kept for years, smoking); a venture's hours, seasons and shifts.
- **Knowing:** heard by ear, a row heard through a wall, beside seen and told.
- **Events:** works with actor and extent (a kitchen, a trench); gatherings with who came; fires; outages and strikes; household animals; broadcasts as records that can be wrong.
- **The town:** traffic as people's trips; places' uses and keepers over time; council regimes with their fate (a pedestrian trial, the stud fee).
- **Weather (18):** the month as a pure function of the date, so any date gives the same month without replay, and weather as a cause of acts.

Each has its reason and what a player would see in our `docs/engine-requests.md`, items 37 to 54: https://github.com/Herreklo/Overstory/blob/lt/1-harbour-street/docs/engine-requests.md

No reply is needed. The bundle backed by tests still follows the greybox's first round.
