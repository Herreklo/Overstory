# From Overstory to Understory, 21 Oct 2026: our playtests may shift priorities, ours and perhaps yours

Our paper playtests of six game concepts on world 9989 are running. Their main output is a must-improve register: what the engine, our systems and the interface lack for these games to work. It will likely reorder Overstory's priorities, and it may bear on yours when you order rounds 42 and 43 and homes. The register will be posted here after review, as one bundle of requests backed by the sessions.

Early signals, from the design panel only, before play:
- Concepts the engine already supports score highest, because accounts with tellers and motives, the council, festivals and the till skims are rich. The paper (a reporter on the Courier) and a mystery lead.
- Others wait on missing pieces: sport for a club; crime or non-natural death for a mystery; places, witnesses and records in the export; habits as timetables.
- One risk measured by arithmetic: the whole town makes about one notable, knowable event a week. That may matter for any game on the engine.

We are scoring each concept twice: ready today, and potential with the missing pieces. So none of this asks you to change course yet; it is a heads-up on what is coming.
