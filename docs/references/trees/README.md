## Gaps

- No big town sycamore in Norway; no old park beeches and ashes yet (Ledaal, Eiganes: being researched).
- Birch on the coast around 20 October is thin on Commons: on the coast the crowns are probably patchier (green, yellow and gale-stripped branches together) than inland; check against local photographs before the colour is fixed.
- Many of Stavanger's coastal conifers may be planted mountain pine or Sitka rather than native Scots pine: confirm before a generator chooses species.
