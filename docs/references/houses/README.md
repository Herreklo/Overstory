# House references

01 to 10 are the 1989 harbour fronts, each logged in `docs/research/downloads.md`.

11 to 16 were sent by Øystein in chat on 29 October as inspiration for later: the Eiganes villa and houses not made of wood. No source was given. They are for study only.

| File | What it shows | What to take |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `11-brick-gable-cottage-anchors-roses.webp` | A small gable house in hand-made brick, every brick its own shade. Its windows and verges are blue-grey and its door is teal. Flat soldier arches sit over the openings, iron wall anchors are fixed on the face, and roses climb by the door | Brick as many single bricks, not a tiled texture. Arches and anchors are part of how the wall is built. A garden grows against the wall |
| `12-brick-range-corbel-frieze-eyebrow-dormers-cg.webp` | A CG render of a converted brick farm range: red brick with a stepped corbel frieze under a rendered band, rendered piers, red eyebrow dormers and skylights in a tile roof | Brick and render on one wall. A conversion over the years reads as a change of use, with new glass and dormers on an old range |
| `13-villa-white-boarded-mansard-green-shutters.webp` | A white boarded villa of about the 1910s: an orange-tiled mansard roof, a balcony bay in the middle, dark green shutters, stone steps and a gravel forecourt | The villa's symmetry, its shutters, its steps and its garden. The neighbours are of the same kind |
| `14-villa-white-blue-tile-hip-garden-aerial.webp` | A white villa from above: a blue glazed-tile hip roof, a front gable with an oculus over a porch and balcony, beech hedges and a big lawn, among others of its kind | The villa as one plot in a garden district: hedges, trees, lawn and a drive |
| `15-villa-rendered-hip-terraced-garden.png` | A white rendered villa on a hill with green shutters, a dark hip roof and a terraced garden; a pool and a flagpole | A rendered villa. The pool is a later owner's work, an event in its record |
