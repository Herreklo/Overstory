# House references

01 to 10 are the 1989 harbour fronts, each logged in `docs/research/downloads.md`.

11 to 57 were sent by Øystein in chat on 29 October as inspiration for later: the Eiganes villa and houses not made of wood. No source was given, apart from the credit printed on 45. They are for study only.

Some show the town after 1989, not as it stood then, and belong to the later years we build (2000, 2010, 2020): 21 (2010s flats), 24 (the theatre's extension), 32 (the city hall's new facade), and the later work in 12 (the conversion), 15 (the pool) and 30–31 (the new dormers and café), and the newer blocks and hotel on the right of 46. In a 1989 scene they are what the town will become, never what it is.

48 to 57 came with Øystein's note: "houses in stavanger has changed a lot. The old beautiful houses in Eiganes etc, not AS much, but a lot of new style buildings have shown up." All but 53 show that later town: new buildings set among the old, old ones reused, and new villas.
