# Paper playtests, round 1: usage

What the round cost and why its design changed (prereg.md, amendment 1). Read from the app's usage card (`get_usage`).

| Time (21 October) | 5-hour window | Weekly, all models | What ran |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 20:22 | 16% | 5% | resumed; nothing of the round running |
| 21:10 | 75% | 20% | the cards (2 agents), Saltlight, the pre-registration, 12 calibration runs (about 90 player agents), 3 setups started; the architecture session alongside |

- A calibration run, two short games with eight fresh player agents, cost about 0.8% of the week. A fresh agent carries about 60k tokens of base context before it reads anything.
- A concept run has seven rounds, a heavier game master (the world pack, the ledger) and eight player agents: about 1.5 calibration runs, about 1.2% of the week.
- The 72 runs as designed: about 90% of the week. The lean design's 24: about 30%.
- At most 20 agents run at once, so a run's game master and player take two slots and at most eight runs play together, leaving room for a setup or an auditor.
