# Idea: building a group of people

Status: ideas, not decisions. Games about assembling people, growing and succeeding: manager, strategy and RPG. Ranked by fit with the engine; the band is the recommendation.

## The ceiling of the genre

The best group games make people stat bundles. Football Manager hides personality (ambition, loyalty, temperament, how a player takes pressure) and reveals it only through scouts' and coaches' remarks, the closest the genre comes to reading people. XCOM and Battle Brothers make soldiers with names and permadeath, Darkest Dungeon gives them stress and quirks, Wildermyth lets heroes' stories and scars accumulate, Midwinter (1989) lets relationships decide who can recruit whom, Mewgenics and Crusader Kings pass traits down generations. Rock Star Ate My Hamster (1988) was already a band manager: four gold discs in a year or bust.

Everywhere the late game turns into a spreadsheet. Chemistry is a hidden number, nobody remembers what you did, and replacing a person replaces numbers.

## Our edge

**You never see a stat. You see people, and they remember.**

- **People are read, not shown.** No attribute screens. You learn a guitarist from how she plays tonight, what the drummer says about her, how she took it when the van broke down. Every remark comes from a named person and can be wrong.
- **The group's history lives in its members.** Each keeps their own account of what happened (the accounts arc, #20). Two members remember the same night differently and act on it.
- **"What if" can be shown.** The engine can run the world again, so at the end it can show what would have happened if you had kept the drummer.

## Decide monthly, watch the evenings

A group game needs the player's decisions to reach the engine, which `research/reality-check.md` rates Hard. The way through: one set of decisions per month (who to sign, which gig to take, what to promise, whom to talk to), and the month's key evenings, a rehearsal or a gig, played as scenes the player watches (`ideas/moments-in-focus.md`). The engine's first planned player command, inviting a musician to a rehearsal (#22), is exactly this kind of decision.

## Five concepts

| Concept | Pitch | The moment | Weakness |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **The band** | Find musicians in a small town, keep them together for ten years, climb from a pub back room to the big hall | In 2019, a documentary built from the members' own accounts: the singer blames the drummer, the drummer blames you, and you remember the night it started | The music needs people. You rarely hear a whole song: you hear the room, the rehearsal through a wall, the crowd |
| **The club** | A small town's football club across thirty years: the striker's marriage, the youth player's father, the cannery that sponsors the shirts and closes | The centenary book, full of wrong memories | Needs a match engine; every comparison is with Football Manager |
| **The crew** | A fishing boat's crew, 1960s to 1980s: seasons at sea, families ashore | The empty bunk | Most of the play is at sea, away from the town |
| **The venture** | Open a place, a café in the old bank or a rehearsal loft in a closed cannery; hire people, gather regulars, watch the building and street change | The building, years later, showing what you did to it | Tycoon loops drift towards spreadsheets |
| **The family** | One family across a century; heirs inherit grudges and promises | Finding a promise in a grandparent's diary | Long, and overlaps the heirs in `ideas/seven-games.md` |

## The recommendation

**The band, with the venture as its home base.** Your rehearsal loft in an old warehouse is the building that changes with you, and other bands form around it (`research/world-that-remembers.md`, principle 10). It joins talent discovery (`ideas/talent-discovery.md`), chemistry and promises (`ideas/game-layer-ideas.md`), the venue ladder (`research/scenes-and-sound.md`) and the engine's own milestone of a band for a year.

The mark: **"Ten years. Five people. Everyone remembers it differently."**

## Multiplayer: a band is a hidden-role game

Four friends each play one member. Everyone wants the band to succeed, and everyone has a private ambition: a solo career, leaving town, someone at home who wants them back. Diplomacy and The Traitors, inside something built together.

## The risk

The engine reports that living barely changes anyone: thirty years move how someone chooses by about 3% of the distance between two people. Ten years in a band needs people who visibly change, harden, sober up or lose faith. The accounts arc's third step, accounts becoming lessons, is aimed at this, and it is paused.

## The first test

Read the engine's band for a year as a story when it runs. If that year is not interesting to read, no game on top of it will be.
