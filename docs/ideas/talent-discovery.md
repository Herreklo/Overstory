# Idea: talent discovery

Status: idea. A setting-free mechanic for the person model; music is the first worked case. Not yet designed in detail.

## The idea

Talent hides inside ordinary people. Everyone has aptitudes, but only some have interest, fewer have turned it into skill, and fewer still have been seen by anyone who matters. Discovery means finding talent where it lives, in ordinary life, before the world has measured it, and feeling that you found it yourself.

## The core pattern (any setting)

Discovery comes from four things the person model already has:

1. **Aptitude, interest and skill are separate.** A person can have a rare gift and no interest, or burning interest and ordinary gifts.
2. **The world measures only some people.** Leagues, charts, exams, competitions and awards cover only those who took part. Everyone else is unmeasured, and that is where discovery lives.
3. **Talent shows only in situations.** Each situation reveals part of a person, never all of it. The picture is built from fragments.
4. **Leads come from people.** Tips are beliefs held by people with their own taste, insight and bias. Learning whose eye to trust is part of the game.

A setting pack supplies only the content: which skills count, where the world measures, and which everyday situations let talent leak out.

## How deep discovery goes, by setting

The mechanic is strongest where most talent is unmeasured and weakest where everyone is already measured.

| Setting | What hides | Where it leaks out |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Music | Singers, players, writers, producers | Choirs, weddings, karaoke, bedroom recordings |
| Football | Players in small countries, lower leagues, youth | Local matches, school teams, five-a-side |
| Writing and publishing | Novelists, poets, journalists | Letters to the paper, local competitions, a diary someone reads |
| Comedy | Comedians, writers | The funny one at work, a best-man speech, open mics |
| Food | Chefs | A family restaurant, a staff meal, a village festival |
| Startups and science | Founders, inventors, researchers | A side project, a fix at work nobody asked for, a student thesis |
| Crime and investigation | Detectives, informants, analysts | Someone who notices what others miss |

In a top professional league, everyone is already measured, so discovery shrinks to classic scouting. Settings with a large unmeasured population get the full mechanic.

## Kinds of hidden talent

| Kind | What is true | What it takes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The unaware | High aptitude, no interest, no skill | Someone has to notice, and they have to want it |
| The hobbyist | Interest and real skill, never shown | Courage and an opening |
| The stalled | Talent and skill, but a job, family, debt or fear got in the way | Changing their circumstances, or their mind |
| The known | Already measured by the world | Nothing to discover; others are bidding too |
| The overrated | Loved locally, low ceiling | Seeing through the hype |

## Turning talent into a career

Finding talent is half of it. The person must want it (values such as Success against Security), be able (job, family, money) and dare (Anxiety, Social boldness). Taking up the talent can rewrite their self-story, from "a fisher, like my father" to "a singer". That is a turning point the player can cause, and a life the player can damage.

## The edge over rivals

Rivals scout through the world's instruments: charts, rankings, competitions, showcases. They see the measured. The player's edge is going where nobody looks, and seeing the truth before the world does.

## Variety between runs

A gifted person may exist in a world on every run, but whether they are found, and whether they make it, depends on the run.

## Music as the first case

In a music setting, discovery can be the opening of the game: the first hour is finding your first act in your own small town.

Where music talent leaks out, and what each place shows:

- a church choir or a funeral song: the voice, not the nerve;
- karaoke or a wedding microphone: nerve and presence, not craft;
- humming at work, correcting a harmony under their breath: the ear, nothing else;
- a bedroom recording a friend shares: craft, not the stage;
- a school concert: raw potential, years from ready;
- a busker: stamina and nerve, sometimes more.

Talent beyond performers counts too: the fisherman who writes poems, the mechanic with a perfect ear for a mix.

## Guardrail

Discovery must feel natural, never like checking every place every week. Signals and leads point the player to where evidence might be; the search itself is a choice about where to spend limited time.
