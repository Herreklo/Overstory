# More game types to throw at the world

Ideas, not decisions (21 October). These are the candidates for a second round of paper playtests (`docs/plan.md`, 1f+), beside the first seven: football club, band, observer, family saga, journalist, murder mystery, and an RPG role.

## What the world has that others don't

1. **Lives with causes and witnesses.** Every event has its reasons, its weights, and who could have seen it (private, local or public).
2. **A town that records history truthfully:** wear, repairs, care and coats, signs and ghost signs.
3. **Time that can jump,** with the screen always following the record.
4. **Evidence, not verdicts:** accounts and records can be wrong.

A game type is strong here when its loop feeds on these four, and weak when it would play the same without them.

## Not a fit

- **Action-led games** (combat, chases, racing): they hide what the world is good at.
- **Crime sandboxes** built on violence: they run against Rule 8 and against "evidence, not verdicts".
- **A pure city builder of traffic and zoning:** our strength is people and history, not throughput.

## Øystein's read (21 October)

The postal carrier, the restorer and the photographer: "sounds a bit boring, but ok". As standalone games they are lenses with too little at stake. What grips is people you care about, conflict, pressure, and choices that cost something. So they are tested as mechanics inside stronger games, not alone:
- the camera as the journalist's or detective's tool, whose photos are evidence that can be printed;
- the postal round as the way into the murder mystery;
- restoration as a thread in the family saga.

The Courier's editor stays a standalone candidate: deadlines, a rival paper, printing about people you know, and a town whose beliefs shift with every edition.

## Recommended for round 2

These four have the strongest loops on what exists today:
1. **The Courier's editor**, the information game. It uses the engine's most distinctive data.
2. **The postal carrier**, routines and absences, paired with a slow mystery.
3. **The restorer**, which tests criterion 1 directly through play.
4. **The town photographer**, time and records as play.

Two hybrids to try:
- the photographer as the journalist's tool;
- the postal carrier as the way into the murder mystery.
