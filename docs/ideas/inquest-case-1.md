# Inquest, case 1: The fuel pier

Status: a hand-built case for a paper test of `docs/ideas/inquest.md`. Every fact in it is the kind the engine could produce once the requests in that doc land. It is written by hand so we can learn whether the play is good before anything is generated.

## The death

> **Even Sande, 36, fisherman.** Found in the harbour at 21:40 on Friday 3 November 1989, beside the fuel pier, 200 metres from his own berth. Wearing an orange boatyard oilskin with "A. VIK" inked in the collar. A cut on the right temple. His watch stopped at 21:12. His boat, *Solvei*, was gone from her berth and was found the next morning drifting off the breakwater with an empty tank.

The player is Even's ghost, with no memory of that night.

## The people

| Liv Vik, 41 | Arne's wife. Took a job in Bergen in June. |
| Jonas Holm, 24 | Deckhand on *Solvei* in the summer. His girlfriend lives across the fjord. |
| Mari Dale, 22 | Jonas's girlfriend. |

| Bjørg Lie, 62 | Runs the kiosk on the quay until 21:00. In 2019 she is 92, in a care home. |

## What happened (the truth, for the facilitator only)

- **16:40.** The evening ferries across the fjord are cancelled for the storm.
- **17:55.** The storm takes down a line. Power goes out across the quay. The fuel station runs on its own generator, so its sign at the end of the fuel pier stays lit. The fuel pier's lamps are on the same generator, but they have been dead since September.
- **18:30.** Jonas takes *Solvei* with the spare key Even lent him in August, to fetch Mari across the fjord. He coils the mooring lines neatly on the pontoon. The fuel gauge shows half. The tank is nearly empty: the new gauge part arrived at the boatyard on 26 October and Arne never fitted it.
- **19:30.** Even is at Sjøbua, by candlelight. Arne has been drinking there since six.
- **19:40.** *Solvei* runs dry off the breakwater. Jonas anchors and waits.
- **20:30.** Arne leaves, drunk, and in the dark takes Even's coat from the rack: their father's old yellow oilskin.
- **20:50.** Even leaves, takes the only coat left, Arne's orange one, and walks to his berth to check on *Solvei* in the storm. The berth is empty.
- **21:00.** Bjørg closes the kiosk and sees a man in Arne's orange coat walk towards the fuel pier.
- **21:05.** Even walks out on the fuel pier, where he can see the outer harbour, to look for *Solvei*.
- **21:12.** On the unlit, wet edge he slips, hits his head on a bollard and falls in. His watch stops. The pier's ladder is not there: it was taken to the boatyard for welding on 12 October and never brought back. He holds on to a fender tyre.
- **21:12.** Kari, at her window before a late visit to a friend, sees in the fuel station's light an orange boatyard coat go down by the edge. She thinks it is Arne, drunk on the pier again. In July she called the police about him twice, and the second time the officer told her the police were not a taxi service for drunks. She does not call.
- **21:40.** Harald, on his storm round, finds Even in the water by the fender tyre. Dead.
- **22:30.** Jonas rows *Solvei*'s tender ashore and tells no one. The police decide *Solvei* broke her moorings in the storm.

## The holes

Five holes lined up. Close any one of them and Even lives.

1. **The boat was gone,** so Even went out on the fuel pier to look for it. (Jonas still had the spare key; the ferry was cancelled; the gauge part was never fitted, so Jonas did not get back by eight.)
2. **He was wearing Arne's coat,** so his sister did not know him. (The power cut; Arne left first, drunk.)
3. **The pier was dark,** so he fell. (Lamps dead since September; the repair was postponed to the 1990 budget.)
4. **The ladder was gone,** so he could not get out. (At the boatyard since 12 October, never welded.)
5. **Nobody called,** so nobody came. (Kari took the coat for Arne's, and after July would not call the police again.)

**Under the holes.** Arne's bad summer lies under four of the five: the gauge part he never fitted, the coat he took, the ladder he never welded, and the July nights that taught Kari not to call. Under Arne's summer lies the boatyard cutting his hours after the 1986 oil crash. A town's bad decade killed Even, through people, and nobody meant it.

## The red herrings

All true, none causal. They are what a player, or a Traitor, will build a murder out of.

- **The insurance.** Even raised *Solvei*'s insured value in September, from 180,000 to 240,000 kroner. (Because of the engine overhaul.)
- **The debt.** Arne owed Even 4,000 kroner from July, and Even died in Arne's coat. The police questioned Arne. (He took the wrong coat in the dark.)
- **The row.** Jonas and Even argued about pay in August, and Jonas left town before Christmas. (He left because of the boat, and he did not kill anyone.)
- **The head wound.** The newspaper says only "head injury". (The police report says it matches a fall against bollard B3, where they found blood.)
- **The late entry.** Harald's logbook entry for 3 November is squeezed in afterwards in different ink. (He wrote it the next morning, shaken. It is accurate.)

## The evidence

Each document has a maker, a date and a stake. Some are wrong, in the ways real records are wrong.

### The harbour (official records)

1. **Police report,** Sgt. Rønning, 4 November 1989. Body found 21:40 at the fuel pier by the harbour master. Orange oilskin, "A. VIK" in the collar. Laceration to the right temple, consistent with a fall against bollard B3 (blood traces). Watch stopped at 21:12. Moderate blood alcohol. *Solvei* "presumed to have broken her moorings in the storm"; recovered 07:20 off the breakwater, tank empty. *Note: mooring lines found coiled on the pontoon.* Arne Vik questioned about the coat: says he took the wrong coat in the dark at Sjøbua and found the deceased's yellow coat in his hallway. Conclusion: accidental drowning under the influence. *(Stake: closing the case. Wrong: a boat that breaks loose does not leave its lines coiled.)*
2. **Harbour master's logbook,** Harald Nes. 4 September: "Fuel pier lamps out (generator circuit). Reported to the municipality." 12 October: "Ladder, fuel pier, taken to the boatyard for welding (A. Vik)." 3 November, in different ink: "Ferry cancelled 16:40. Power out on the quay 17:55. Round 21:30–21:45. Found E. Sande in the water at the fuel pier 21:40. Lamps still dead. Ladder still at the yard."
3. **Municipal technical committee minutes,** 28 September 1989. Item 14: "Repairs to harbour lighting (fuel pier) postponed to the 1990 budget, given reduced revenues."
4. **Ferry notice,** 3 November: evening crossings cancelled from 16:40 because of the storm.
5. **Boatyard work order 1189,** 17 October, *Solvei*: engine service done; "fuel gauge sender: part ordered". Signed A. Vik. Stapled to it, a delivery slip of 26 October: "1 × fuel gauge sender, for *Solvei*".
6. **Police log,** 22 July 1989: "Call from K. Sande about a man (A. Vik) drunk on the fuel pier. Second call this month. Caller advised that the police are not a taxi service for drunks." *(Stake: the duty officer's patience.)*
7. **Insurance letter,** 12 September: *Solvei*'s insured value raised to 240,000 kroner after an engine overhaul.

### The town in 2019 (the Afterward)

8. **Grave:** "Even Sande, 1953–1989. Taken by the sea." *(Wrong: the harbour, not the sea.)*
9. **Grave:** "Olav Sande, 1921–1989."
10. **Grave:** "Arne Vik, 1945–1997. Hard-working hands." The parish register gives liver failure.
11. **Grave:** "Kari Sande, 1950–2016. Nurse." A bench beside the chandlery, facing the fuel pier, has a plaque: "Kari's bench."
12. **Bjørg Lie, 92, in the care home:** "That night I closed at nine and I saw Arne Vik going out on the fuel pier in his orange coat, drunk as usual. Next thing, Even's dead. Arne was never the same after." And: "Jonas Holm and Even had a terrible row that summer, and Jonas ran off before Christmas. You ask me…" *(Wrong: she saw the coat and put Arne in it. Thirty years have merged two men into one.)*
13. **Havneposten, 6 November 1989:** "Fisherman drowned in storm. Even Sande (36) was found in the harbour on Friday night. He had been at Sjøbua earlier in the evening. His boat broke loose in the storm. The police suspect no crime." *(Leaves out the coat, the lamps and the ladder. "He had been at Sjøbua" does the rest.)*
14. **Havneposten, 14 March 1990:** "New lamps and a ladder at the fuel pier after last year's tragic death."
15. **Mari Dale, 52:** "Jonas came across for me once in a storm, in a borrowed boat. He never made it. He would never say whose boat it was. He was strange after that. The autumn the ferry kept stopping, '89 or '90."

### The family (letters and diaries)

16. **Kari's diary,** bought at her estate sale:
    - 14 January: "Dad buried. Even wants to keep the house. I can't afford it on my own."
    - 18 March: "Told Even I'm selling. He walked out. Fine."
    - 22 July: "Called the police about Arne again. The officer laughed at me. Never again."
    - 3 November: "Arne on the fuel pier in the storm in that orange coat. Saw him go down by the edge. Not calling them again. Not my job."
    - 4 to 30 November: torn out.
    - 1 December: "I see the pier every time I close my eyes."
17. **Kari's letter to Even,** 2 April 1989, folded inside the diary, never sent: "Come for dinner on Fridays, like when Dad was alive. I'm off on Fridays. I don't want the house to be the last thing we said."
18. **Jonas's letter to Kari,** 1999: "I had *Solvei* that night. I still had Even's spare key from August. I meant to fetch Mari and be back by eight. The gauge said half. I ran dry at the breakwater, rowed in at half ten and told no one. I'm sorry."
19. **Liv's letter to Arne,** June 1989, from his estate: "They cut you to three days and you started at the bar again. I can't do it. I've taken the job in Bergen."
20. **Even's pocket notebook,** waterlogged, returned to Kari: "Sept: insurance up, new engine. Lent Arne 4,000. Jonas still has the spare key!!"

### The year (scenes)

Each scene costs one return to watch. Scenes confirm what the documents suggest; the case can be solved without them.

1. 14 January, the funeral: Kari says, "Come for dinner on Fridays, like with Dad."
2. 18 March, the father's house: the argument about selling it.
3. 22 July, the fuel pier at night: Arne drunk on the pier; Kari phones from her window; the officer mocks her.
4. August, the pontoon: Even gives Jonas the spare key for an early job; they argue about pay.
5. 12 October, the fuel pier: the harbour crew takes the ladder away; Arne carries it to the yard.
6. 26 October, the boatyard: the gauge part arrives; Arne puts the box on a shelf.
7. 3 November, 18:30, the berth: Jonas casts off in *Solvei* and coils the lines.
8. 3 November, 20:30, Sjøbua by candlelight: Arne takes the yellow coat.
9. 3 November, 21:12, from Kari's window: an orange coat goes down by the edge.

## The changes, and what each one does

Once the board is solved, the player returns to make one change per return. The facilitator reads the Afterward card for each.

| Change | When | Even | What else changes by 2019 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Get the spare key back from Jonas | August | Lives | Jonas fetches Mari in his uncle's open dinghy and capsizes. A new grave: "Jonas Holm, 1965–1989." |
| Ask Arne to fit the gauge part | Late October | Lives | *Solvei* is back at her berth by 20:10. Jonas and Mari marry in 1992. |
| Walk Arne home before he takes the coat | 3 November, 20:30 | Lives | Kari knows her father's yellow coat, calls at once, and Harald's crew pull Even out at 21:25. Arne still dies in 1997. |
| Help Harald bring the ladder back | October | Lives | A Havneposten clipping from 1995: a stranger climbs out at the fuel pier ladder. |
| Answer Kari at the funeral | 14 January | Lives | Friday dinners. On 3 November Even is at Kari's, not at Sjøbua. The father's house is never sold, and in 2019 it is still in the family, painted yellow again. |
| Help Arne in June | June | Out of reach | Even barely knows Arne in June. The game says so: some holes are too deep to reach. |

There is no right answer. The game shows the cemetery and the town in 2019, and the player decides which future to keep.

## Running the paper test

- One to four players, 60 minutes, a budget of six returns shared by the team.
- Solo: the player holds all the evidence. Group: deal the four archives (harbour, town, family, year) one per player. With three players, merge the family into the town.
- The year's player spends the team's returns to watch scenes.
- **With a Traitor:** deal one player a secret card. They win if the inquest records the wrong cause (a murder, a scuttled boat) or commits a change that fails. They may describe their documents falsely; any player may demand to see a document, at the cost of one return.
- It succeeds if players say "it was all of them, and none of them" and argue about which change to make. It fails if they solve it by matching keywords without reading, or stop caring once the board is filled.
